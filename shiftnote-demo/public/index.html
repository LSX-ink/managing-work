How it works

Built for the cabin at the end of a shift, or the van on the way home.

  1. Talk or snap
    Record a voice note in your own words. Add photos of the work.
  2. It writes the paperwork
    Daily site diary, progress against plan, delay records and early warnings, in the company's own template.
  3. You check and approve
    Tap any line to change it. Nothing is filed until you hold to approve.
  4. It goes where it should
    Project manager, document control and the commercial team each get their part, and it's all searchable later.
