Privacy notice

Last updated 21 October 2026. Questions or requests: alfredw.lsx@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your name, business name, email, phone number, website link and the details you put in your brief or sign-up form, plus your payment records if you take the hosting and care plan. If you message us on Instagram, we also receive your Instagram username and the messages you send us.

Why

To reply to your brief or message, quote, build and look after your website (needed for our contract with you), to keep our accounts (a legal duty), and to send design tips and offers only if you ticked the box (your consent, which you can withdraw any time by using the unsubscribe link or replying "unsubscribe").

Instagram messages

When you message @kineticwebdesigns, our assistant (Alfred) may read your message and send a reply about our services, prices and next steps. We only reply to people who message us first; we never start conversations through the Instagram connection. Anything we can't answer automatically is passed to a person. We keep a record of the conversation so we can follow up on your enquiry, and we never use it for anything else.

Who else sees it

Only the services that run our business for us: Cloudflare (website hosting), Resend (email), Stripe (payments, which never share your card or bank details with us), Google (our business email) and Meta (Instagram messages). We never sell your details.

How long we keep it

Enquiries and messages that don't go ahead: 12 months. Client and payment records: 6 years, as HMRC requires.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your details, including your Instagram messages, at any time. Email alfredw.lsx@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your name, email or Instagram username. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email, except records we must keep by law (for example invoices for HMRC).

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or object to how we use them, by emailing alfredw.lsx@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. Your brief draft and colour choices are saved in your own browser only, so you don't lose them.
