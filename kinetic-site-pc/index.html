Good to know before you start

How long does it take?

A Starter website takes 1 to 2 weeks, a Business website 2 to 4 weeks and an Online shop 4 to 6 weeks, counted from your go-ahead.

How do I pay?

Half is paid up front to book your start date, and the rest when your site goes live. You get a clear invoice for each.

What do I need to give you?

Your vision in your own words, your three colours, and your logo and photos if you have them. No logo? Logo design is £150. No photos? Photo editing and stock images are £100.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Starter websites include 2 rounds of changes and Business websites and shops include 3.

What's in monthly hosting and care?

Your domain name, fast and secure hosting, security updates, backups and small text changes, all for £20 a month. Need lots of big videos or downloads? Extra file storage is £5 a month per 10 GB.

Who owns my website and domain?

You do. Your domain is registered in your name and your accounts belong to you. If you ever leave, we hand everything over.

Do you write the words too?

We write the copy with you. If you'd like us to write whole pages for you, copywriting is £60 a page.

Will people find me on Google?

Starter websites include the Google basics. Business websites and shops add help with your Google Business Profile, and Local Google setup is £150 as an add-on.
