## 4. Tactical points (attributed)
1. **Morocco defended without the ball and waited for mistakes** instead of controlling possession. Their 31.3% possession was the second-lowest rate at the tournament (Opta Analyst, OPTA). ESPN called it a "defensive, countering style" (Kyle Bonagura, ESPN Rocky piece).
2. **Compact block, numbers behind the ball.** Graeme Souness on Sky: Morocco "got back in numbers" and nobody was caught out. SKYR. Sid Lowe: "They are made of granite." GUA
3. **Amrabat as the screen.** Sky made him Player of the Match in its ratings (8 recoveries, 3 tackles, SKY). Opta: he led the tournament with 41 possession wins. OPTA
4. **Portugal dominated territory but created little.** Opta: they had 78.9% possession in the first 15 minutes, then little quality, with one shot on target in the first half. OPTA. ESPN: 12 shots, 3 on target, "none troubled" Bounou. ESPN Rocky piece
5. **The goal came from the left, which was unusual.** Morocco's threat is usually on the right through Hakimi and Ziyech (Opta). OPTA
6. **Santos benched Ronaldo again.** He was "once again left on the bench" (SKYR). This was the second straight game (ESPN). Santos: "Cristiano is a great player and he came on when we thought it was necessary. But no, no regrets." https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13954/12765664/portugal-defender-pepe-questions-appointment-of-argentine-referee-after-world-cup-exit and SKY
7. **Morocco won despite injuries.** Mazraoui and Aguerd were missing and Saïss went off in the 57th minute (ESPN Rocky piece, WIKI subs). Regragui: "We are the Rocky Balboa of this World Cup." ESPN Rocky piece, https://supersport.com/football/fifa-world-cup-2022/news/3d214ed8-f021-44bc-8911-bf586dc88c02/morocco-rocky-balboa-of-this-world-cup-says-regragui

## 5. Records
- **First African nation in a World Cup semi-final.** WIKI, ESPN, SKY. **First Arab nation:** WIKI, https://www.skysports.com/football/news/11095/12766794/world-cup-2022-france-2-0-morocco-theo-hernandez-and-randal-kolo-muani-on-target-as-didier-deschamps-side-reach-final
- Ronaldo had played 570 minutes of World Cup knockout football (8 games) without a goal. OPTA only (single source)
- **"Ronaldo's last World Cup" is FALSE.** He went to a record sixth World Cup in 2026. https://global.espn.com/football/story/_/id/48816135/cristiano-ronaldo-named-record-sixth-world-cup-squad-portugal and https://www.beinsports.com/en-us/soccer/fifa-world-cup-2026/articles/official-cristiano-ronaldo-becomes-the-first-player-to-play-in-six-world-cups-2026-05-19. Say "his last World Cup **match in Qatar**" or "the night his 2022 dream ended in tears".
- **En-Nesyri's 2.78 m**: widely reported, but not an official FIFA or Opta measurement. https://www.goal.com/en-bh/news/en-nesyri-morocco-winners-against-portugal-beats-ronaldo-s-record-for-highest-headed-goal/blt1a962780f666cf3d and https://medias24.com/2022/12/12/le-saut-de-youssef-en-neysiri-mesure-a-278m/.
