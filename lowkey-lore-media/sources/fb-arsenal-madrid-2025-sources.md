## 4. Tactical points (each is attributed; most are a single outlet's analysis)
1. **Shapes.** Arsenal were 4-3-3 out of possession and built up in a back three (Kiwior-Saliba-Timber). Lewis-Skelly stepped into midfield next to Partey as a double pivot. Madrid defended in a 4-4-2 with Mbappe and Vinicius up front, Bellingham wide left and Rodrygo wide right. (CV)
2. **Lewis-Skelly.** At 18y 194d he was the second-youngest Englishman to start a UCL QF, after Bellingham (UEFA, SKY-A). Nick Wright (Sky) says he kept Rodrygo quiet and made more final-third passes than anyone except Odegaard (SKY-A). He inverted for the third goal (CV) and laid it on for Merino (UEFA, SKY).
3. **Merino as false 9.** He dropped into Madrid's midfield line, which opened space for Rice in the left inside channel. (CV)
4. **Madrid's front pair.** Vinicius was "a constant outlet" on the counter and Mbappe had a tame finish saved by Raya (OPTA). Mbappe had chances but Raya and the back line held them (SKY, AJ). After half-time Madrid doubled up on Saka and Martinelli (CV).
5. **Rice as a No 8.** Wright says Arteta uses him as a No 8, and Rice had the most shots on the pitch (5) and nine touches in the box (SKY-A).
6. **Jover and the free-kick.** Rice told Amazon Prime that set-piece coach Nicolas Jover wanted a cross: "He's claiming for it but he's told me to cross it!" (MOLE) and "It didn't make sense from that angle to cross the ball." (ESPN-RICE). theScore ran the same story.
7. **Madrid's collapse.** Ancelotti said the team fell apart physically and mentally once it was 2-0 (SKY, UEFA: reaction "very poor"). Also to AJ: "For 68 minutes, the game was level, and then they hit two fantastic free kicks and the game changed."
8. **The wall.** Courtois said he could have added an extra man to the wall (ESPN-COU, GOAL-COU; see 6b).

## 5. Records
- VERIFIED: "Rice is the first player to score two direct free-kicks in a Champions League knockout match." (OPTA wording; same claim in SKY, ESPN-R, TNT-R "first player in Champions League history to score two direct free-kick goals in a knockout stage match")
- VERIFIED: he is the fifth player to score two direct free-kicks in any UCL match, after Rivaldo (v Milan 2000), Cristiano Ronaldo (v Zurich 2009), Neymar (v Red Star 2018) and Ziyech (v Man Utd 2023). (TNT-R; the same list appeared in search results from other outlets)
- VERIFIED: before this match Rice had never scored a direct free-kick in his professional career (OPTA, SKY, ESPN-RICE). Arsenal's previous direct free-kick goal was Sept 2021, Burnley away (Arteta in ESPN-RICE; SKY-A).

## 6. Controversial quote candidates
a) **Courtois blames the fouls.** "They were unnecessary fouls, when there was no danger. Perhaps we shouldn't make those fouls." Said to Movistar+ on 8 Apr 2025, right after the game. (ESPN-COU; FE-COU via AS: "They are two unnecessary fouls, where there is no danger."; GOAL-COU says it was aimed at Bellingham and Alaba). The wording varies because it was translated from Spanish.
b) **Courtois takes the blame for the wall.** "I usually put an extra man in. I take responsibility for that, I could have put an extra man in." Movistar+, 8 Apr 2025 (ESPN-COU; GOAL-COU translates it as "Maybe I could have added another. But I didn't expect so much curve."). This split opinion: Seedorf and Sneijder criticised his wall, Canizares defended him (search results only, unverified).
c) **Bellingham.** "We were lucky to get away with three." Said to CBS Sports on 8 Apr 2025 after the game (ESPN-JB; HAY-JB and others run the same quote).
