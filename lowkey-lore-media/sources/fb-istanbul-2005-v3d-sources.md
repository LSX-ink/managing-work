# Sources: Istanbul, 2005 UEFA Champions League final (Liverpool 3-3 Milan, Liverpool won 3-2 on penalties)
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_UEFA_Champions_League_final
- 25 May 2005, Atatürk Olympic Stadium; half-time 0-3; Maldini 1' (from a Pirlo free kick); Crespo 39' and 44'
- Gerrard 54' (header from a Riise cross); Šmicer 56' (long range, bottom corner); Alonso 60' (penalty saved, rebound scored) after Gattuso fouled Gerrard; three goals in a six-minute spell
- 117': Dudek double save from Shevchenko (under 3 yards); Dudek called it "the save of my life"
- Shootout: Serginho over (Dudek mimicking Grobbelaar's 1984 "spaghetti legs"), Hamann scored with a broken foot (his own words: "I didn't know I had a broken foot at the time"; Wikipedia says a broken toe, other reports a metatarsal), Pirlo saved, Shevchenko saved by Dudek's left hand; Gerrard man of the match
- Liverpool failed to finish in the top four; it was uncertain whether the holders would get a place the next season
- The banter lines ("Someone forgot to tell Steven Gerrard", "Shevchenko is still asking how", the Milan-fan send-off) are jokes, not factual claims
- Liverpool finished fifth in the 2004-05 Premier League (Chelsea, Arsenal, Manchester United, Everton above them): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004%E2%80%9305_Premier_League
- Milan's starting XI (Dida; Cafu, Stam, Nesta, Maldini; Pirlo, Gattuso, Seedorf; Kaká; Shevchenko, Crespo): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_UEFA_Champions_League_final
