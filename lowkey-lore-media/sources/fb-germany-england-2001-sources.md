## 4. Analysis (attributed)
1. Beckenbauer: after the third goal England played football "that would have beaten anyone in the world" — FIFA, IE.
2. Beckenbauer singled out pace, aggression and movement — FIFA, GMS.
3. Beckham's delivery created the chances: 3 of the 5 goals came from his set pieces/crosses — WIKI, GMS (FA also credits his crosses).
4. Gerrard decided it: the goal before half-time plus the through ball for Owen's third — WIKI, GMS, IT.
5. Second-half collapse: England came out "all guns blazing" (FA). Germany chased the game and England "picked them off" on the break (PF). McManaman: "every time we went forward in the second half we looked like we were going to score" (GMS). Single sources for each wording, so use the shared point (England ran riot after the break) rather than quoting.
6. Back line: Wörns was hooked at half-time (WIKI, ES). Beckenbauer: "Our defenders were slow and they just could not handle his pace" (IE only, UNVERIFIED as verbatim).
7. Eriksson's plan "Keep it tight", then "get at them" at half-time, and a Seaman save at 1-1: physio Gary Lewin in PF only (UNVERIFIED).

## 5. Records and context
- Worst German home defeat in 70 years: UPI, CA (Reuters).
- First time Germany conceded 5+ since 6-3 v France in 1958: WIKI, GMS.
- **"First home WC qualifier defeat" is FALSE.** It was Germany's *second*. West Germany lost 0-1 to Portugal in Stuttgart on 16 Oct 1985 (Carlos Manuel): https://www.11v11.com/matches/west-germany-v-portugal-16-october-1985-239509/, https://www.portugoal.net/classics-topmenu/4797-the-miracle-of-stuttgart-west-germany-0-1-portugal-1985-world-cup-qualifier. FA says Germany "had lost only one World Cup qualifier on home soil". Safe line: "only their second home World Cup qualifier defeat ever" or "first defeat in Munich since 1973".
- England's first win on German soil since 1965 (36 years): IT, plus the Hargreaves feature https://www.fourfourtwo.com/features/england-battered-germany-5-1-and-i-got-dropped-by-bayern-munich-i-was-devastated-owen-hargreaves-explains-clubs-reaction-to-famous-three-lions-victory
- Outcome: both teams finished on 17 points and England topped the group on goal difference: GMS, WIKI. England drew 2-2 with Greece thanks to Beckham's late free-kick: WIKI, PF. Germany drew 0-0 with Finland: PF, WIKI. Germany went to the play-off: GMS, WIKI.
- Play-off opponent Ukraine and the scores: WIKI only. UPI/IT named Ukraine or Belarus as likely opponents. Germany beat Ukraine 1-1 / 4-1 is UNVERIFIED here.
