# Sources: Argentina 2-1 England, 1986 World Cup quarter-final ("Hand of God")
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentina_v_England_(1986_FIFA_World_Cup)
- 22 June 1986, Estadio Azteca, Mexico City, attendance 114,580; referee Ali Bin Nasser (Tunisia); four years after the 1982 Falklands War
- 0-0 at half-time; 51': Hodge's clearance into his own area, Maradona (18 cm shorter than Shilton) punches the ball over Shilton
- Maradona's quote: "a little with the head of Maradona and a little with the hand of God"
- Four minutes later: a 60-yard, 10-second run past Beardsley, Reid, Butcher (twice) and Fenwick, finishing past Shilton; voted Goal of the Century on the FIFA website in 2002
- Lineker 81', his sixth goal of the tournament, Golden Boot; Argentina won the 1986 World Cup
- Hodge's swapped Maradona shirt sold at Sotheby's in May 2022 for £7,142,500 (Wikipedia, citing Sotheby's/NPR)
- The VAR line is a joke (VAR didn't exist in 1986), not a factual claim
- 1986 Golden Boot: Lineker 6; Butragueño, Careca, Maradona 5 each: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1986_FIFA_World_Cup#Goalscorers
