# Kinetic website build

The owner's rule (27 Oct 2026): "when making websites always use all the skills so you get the best version."
So every site, mock-up and stays page applies **all** of the design skills below. Don't pick one or two.

## Before you write any markup

Read each of these guides (all are in `jarvis/skills/`). Alfred's own `design_guide` tool returns their openings
together with `guide: "website-kit"`.

1. `business/client-websites/SKILL.md`: Kinetic's page structure that sells, plus the quality check
2. `anthropic/frontend-design/SKILL.md`: a clear, distinctive aesthetic direction, nothing templated
3. `taste/taste-skill/SKILL.md` and its `references-*.md`: art direction, and the tells of AI-looking pages to avoid
4. `impeccable/impeccable/SKILL.md` and its `reference-*.md` (layout, colour, typography, animate, critique, polish)
5. `emil-kowalski/emil-design-eng/SKILL.md`, `animate`, `apple-design`: polish and motion that feel right
6. `ui-skills/baseline-ui`, `improve-ui`, `fixing-accessibility`, `fixing-motion-performance`, `fixing-metadata`
7. `web-engineering/frontend-ui-engineering`, `performance-optimization`: fast, robust front-end
8. `web-3d/auteur` (and its `reference-*.md`), `web-3d/threejs-r3f`, `web-3d/gsap`, `web-3d/motion-principles`, `web-3d/css-native`: 3D, scroll cinema and motion done properly, with fallbacks for slow phones and reduced motion
9. `anthropic/brand-guidelines`, `anthropic/theme-factory`, `taste/redesign-skill`: brand fit and redesigning an existing site
10. `ui-ux-pro-max`: run its search (`design_advice` with `design_system=true`) for palette, fonts and layout pattern

## While building

- Match the business's own brand: logo, colours, uniform and interior, taken from their website and Instagram.
- Make every design different. No two clients share a layout.
- Keep Kinetic's must-haves: the before/after slider and the "what's wrong today / what we do better" panel (where
  they have a site), tap-to-call, booking, real reviews and hours, and phone-first.
- Never invent reviews, numbers or interiors.

## Before you call it done

Run each guide's own checklist or critique section against the page: taste-skill critique, impeccable critique and
audit, fixing-accessibility, fixing-motion-performance, fixing-metadata, performance-optimization. Then check the page
on a phone (390px) and a laptop (1366px). Fix everything you find before anyone sees the link.
