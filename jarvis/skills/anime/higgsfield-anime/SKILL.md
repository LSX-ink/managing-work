TEMPLATE
Anime style, high-quality 2D cel-shaded animation like a modern Japanese anime film, crisp clean line art, vibrant colours, soft cinematic lighting, detailed painted background.
Character: [name], a [age] year old [boy/girl/man/woman] with [hair colour and style], [eye colour] eyes, wearing [outfit], personality [brave / shy / cheeky].
Scene: [where, e.g. a rainy neon city street at night / a cherry blossom school rooftop at sunset].
Action: [one clear movement, e.g. she turns around slowly, wind blowing her hair, then smiles].
Camera: [slow push-in / low-angle hero shot / tracking shot / close-up on the eyes].
Mood: [epic / emotional / mysterious / eerie], with [falling petals / rain / sparks / light particles] in the air.
Smooth motion, expressive face, consistent character, 9:16 vertical, no text, no watermark, no extra limbs, no blurry faces.

TIPS
- Make a still image of your character first, then use it as the start frame for each video so they look the same.
- One action per clip. For a story, make several short clips and join them.
- Reuse the exact Character line in every prompt.
