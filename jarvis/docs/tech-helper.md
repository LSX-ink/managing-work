# Tech helper

Help with your gadgets, fixing tech problems and staying safe online. Everything is stored in your memory folder
or built in; nothing is scanned and nothing personal goes online. (For PC settings, Wi-Fi strength and
"is the internet working", see [pc-control.md](pc-control.md). For the general home inventory and receipts, see
household-plus and money-plus; the gadget list here adds model, serial and receipt links.)

## Your gadgets

1. **Add a gadget**: "Add my Dell laptop, model XPS 13, serial ABC123, bought 20 October 2024 for 999 pounds with a 2 year warranty."
2. **List gadgets**: "Show my gadgets." Pops up a table of type, model, serial, price and warranty end.
3. **One gadget**: "Show details for my laptop." Pops up its details, with a Show receipt button.
4. **Link a receipt**: "The receipt for my laptop is laptop-receipt.pdf in Shopping." (the file must already be in a memory folder)
5. **Show the receipt**: "Show me the laptop receipt." Pops up the file.
6. **Out of warranty soon**: "What's going out of warranty soon?" Pops up a table with days left.
7. **What gadgets cost**: "How much have I spent on gadgets?" Pops up a bar chart.
8. **Remove a gadget**: "Remove the old phone." Alfred asks you first.
9. **Log a battery reading**: "My watch is at 15 percent." (or "charging")
10. **Battery chart**: "Show my watch battery." Pops up a line chart of your readings.
11. **Update reminder**: "Remind me to update the router firmware every 60 days."
12. **What needs updating**: "What updates are due?" Pops up a table with overdue ones marked.
13. **Update done**: "I've updated the router." Starts the countdown again.
14. **Remove an update reminder**: "Stop reminding me about the router." Alfred asks you first.

## Fixing problems

15. **Troubleshooting list**: "What tech problems can you help with?" Pops up a list; click one to start.
16. **Start a guide**: "Walk me through the Wi-Fi not working guide." Also printer offline, slow PC, no sound,
    Bluetooth won't pair, phone storage full and iPhone backup. Pops up a checklist with the current step opened
    out; Alfred reads that step and waits for you.
17. **Tick a step**: "Done, what's next?" or click the tick box. Your progress is remembered.
18. **Start again**: "Start the printer guide again."
19. **Keyboard shortcuts**: "Show me the Excel keyboard shortcuts." (windows, explorer, browser, word, excel, text,
    powerpoint, vscode) Pops up a table. "What's the shortcut for screenshot?" searches them all.
20. **Jargon explainer**: "What's DNS?" Pops up a short plain-English card; "Show me the tech jargon" gives the whole glossary.
21. **Which cable or charger**: "Which charger for an iPhone 15?" or "What cable for a 4K monitor?" Pops up a table;
    if it doesn't know, it shows how to tell the plugs apart.

## Staying safe

22. **Password checker**: "Check how strong a password is." Pops up a private box: type the password into it and
    a meter shows the strength, an estimated crack time and tips as you type. It is measured in that window only,
    never sent to Alfred or saved. Don't say a password out loud or type it in the chat.
23. **Spot a scam**: "Is this message a scam? ..." then paste it. Pops up the warning signs found (urgency, secret
    details, fake links, look-alike sites) with what to do, such as forwarding texts to 7726. Rules only; no link is opened.
24. **Two-factor checklist**: "Show me the two-factor checklist." A tick list that remembers where you are.
25. **Backup checklist**: "Show the backup checklist."
26. **Tick or reset a checklist**: "Tick step 3 of the backup checklist." / "Reset the two-factor checklist."

## Numbers

27. **File size**: "How big is 2.5 gigabytes in megabytes?" Pops up a table of all units.
28. **How many fit**: "How many 4 MB photos fit on 64 GB?"
29. **Download time**: "How long to download 40 GB at 100 Mbps?" Pops up a table at several speeds.
30. **Screen sharpness**: "How sharp is a 15.6 inch 1920 by 1080 screen?" Gives PPI, size, shape and a Windows scaling tip.
31. **Resolution**: "What's 3840 by 2160?" or "Show me common screen resolutions."

## Home network list

You type the devices in; Alfred never scans your network.

32. **Add a device**: "Add the living room TV at 192.168.1.20."
33. **Show the list**: "What's on my home network list?" Pops up a table sorted by address.
34. **Remove a device**: "Take the old printer off the network list." Alfred asks you first.
