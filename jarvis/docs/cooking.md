# Cooking

Hands-free help in the kitchen. Most of these pop a window up on the Alfred screen: a step-by-step cook mode, kitchen
timers, tables and lists you can tap. Everything is saved in your memory folder on this PC (`cooking-recipes.json` and
`cooking-leftovers.json`), nowhere else, and nothing here uses the internet.

They work with the recipes, meal plan and pantry you already keep with Alfred (see home-and-life.md): "Save a recipe
called Chilli...", "Chilli for dinner on Friday", "Add eggs and milk to the pantry". Finding recipes on the web is in
web-in-alfred.md, unit and gas mark conversions are in the calculators, and the freezer list is in household-plus.md.

## Cooking a recipe

1. **Cook mode**: "Start cook mode for chilli." / "Take me through the pancakes from step 3."
   Pops up the recipe one step at a time in big text. Use **Next** and **Back** (or the left and right arrow keys),
   tap the dots to jump, and **Ingredients** to peek at the list. **Read this step** has Alfred read it out. On any
   step that mentions minutes a **Timer** button starts one of Alfred's timers for it. **Finished** logs that you made it.
2. **Scale a recipe**: "Scale the pancakes from 2 servings to 6." / "Make the chilli for 10."
   Pops up a table of the original amounts next to the new ones (it understands 1/2, ½, 1 1/2, ranges like 2-3, and
   g, kg, ml, cups, spoons, tins and cloves). Alfred remembers how many it serves for next time.
3. **Kitchen timers**: "Set kitchen timers: pasta 10 minutes and sauce 25." / "Open my kitchen timers."
   Pops up several named timers side by side, each with Pause, +1 min and Cancel, and a box to add more. Each one
   chimes when it ends and flashes until you press **Stop**. These live on the screen only, separate from Alfred's own
   timers.
4. **Tonight's cooking**: "What am I cooking tonight?"
   Pops up tonight's meal from the plan, how many steps and ingredients it has, what's missing from the pantry, and
   leftovers to eat today. Buttons: **Recipe steps**, **Kitchen timers** and **Add missing to shopping**.

## Planning

5. **What can I make?**: "What can I make with what's in the pantry?"
   Pops up your saved recipes ranked by how much of each you already have, with what's missing. Tap one to cook it.
   Salt, pepper, oil and water always count as in the cupboard.
6. **Batch cooking**: "Plan a batch cook of chilli, bolognese and soup."
   Pops up the cooking order (longest first) and one shopping table with amounts added up across the recipes.
   **Add to shopping** puts it all on your shopping list.
7. **Leftovers**: "Log leftover curry from yesterday." / "What leftovers have I got?" / "I've eaten the curry." /
   "Clear the old leftovers."
   Leftovers keep 2 days, soups, stews and curries 3, rice only 1. The list pops up with what to eat today and what to
   bin; tick one off when it's eaten. Clearing old ones asks you first.

## Your recipe book

8. **Dietary tags**: "Tag the pancakes as vegetarian and nut-free." / "Take nut-free off the pancakes."
   Tags: vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free.
9. **Filter by tag**: "Show my vegan recipes." / "Which recipes are gluten-free and dairy-free?"
   Pops up the matching recipes; tap one to cook it.
10. **Rate and review**: "I made the chilli tonight, 4 stars, needs more cumin." / "I cooked the pancakes on Sunday."
11. **Ratings**: "Show my recipe ratings." / "Show my reviews of the chilli."
    Pops up stars, how many times you've made each one, when you last did, and your latest note.
12. **Not cooked in a while**: "What haven't I cooked in a while?" / "What haven't I made in two months?"
    Pops up the recipes you haven't made lately, never-made ones first.

## Kitchen guide

13. **Roast times**: "How long to roast a 1.5 kilo chicken?" / "Roast beef, 2 kilos, rare." / "An 11 pound turkey."
    Pops up the oven, fan and gas mark temperature, cooking time, resting time and the core temperature to check for
    (chicken, beef rare/medium/well done, lamb, pork, turkey). **Start timers** opens kitchen timers for the roast
    and the rest.
14. **Safe temperatures**: "What temperature should chicken be inside?"
    Pops up a table of safe core temperatures (UK food safety advice). Use a probe thermometer in the thickest part.
15. **Substitutions**: "I've got no buttermilk, what can I use?" / "Show me ingredient swaps."
    About 60 swaps: eggs, self-raising flour, cream, sugar, stock, wine and more.
16. **Seasonal food**: "What's in season?" / "What's in season in December?"
    Pops up the UK fruit and veg in season that month; tap one to find a recipe with it.
17. **Cups to grams**: "How many grams is 2 cups of walnuts?" / "Show me cup weights."
    Pops up a table of 1, ½ and ¼ US cup and 1 tablespoon in grams for nearly 60 ingredients.
18. **Bread and baker's percentages**: "Bread dough with 500 grams of flour at 70% hydration." / "What hydration is
    1 kilo flour and 800 grams water, plus 50 grams olive oil?"
    Pops up the water, salt and yeast weights with baker's percentages, the total dough weight, and what kind of bread
    that hydration suits.
19. **Drink pairing**: "What wine goes with lamb?" / "What should I drink with a curry? Something alcohol-free."
    Pops up wine, beer or cider, and alcohol-free suggestions for the dish.
