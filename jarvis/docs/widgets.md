# Widgets

Widgets are little tools that pop up in a window on the Alfred screen for you to use with your mouse and
keyboard. Ask for one and it appears; drag it by its title bar, shrink it with –, close it with × (or Esc).
Closing a widget stops its sounds and timers. Everything runs in the page, so nothing is sent anywhere; only the
sketch pad and notepad save, and they save into your memory folders.

Alfred can already answer many of these out loud (sums, conversions, "flip a coin", the stopwatch and pomodoro
in Time and dates). Say "widget", "pop-up" or "show me a…" when you want the one you can use yourself.

- **Calculator**: "Open the calculator widget." Pops up a calculator with buttons. You can also type the sum
  (click the calculator first): numbers, + − × ÷, ^ for powers, % for percent, brackets, Enter for equals,
  Backspace to delete, Delete to clear.
- **Unit converter**: "Show me a unit converter for temperature." Pick length, weight, temperature or volume,
  type a number and the answer updates as you type, with every other unit listed underneath. ⇄ swaps the units.
- **Sketch pad**: "Open a sketch pad." Draw with the mouse or a pen (pens press harder for thicker lines).
  Pick a colour, change the pen size, use the eraser, or clear it. Type a name and press Save: the picture is
  saved as a PNG in Ideas (or the folder you asked for, e.g. "a sketch pad that saves to Work") and pops up.
- **Notepad**: "Open a notepad." / "Open my note Holiday plans in the notepad." Write, pick a folder, and press
  Save (or Ctrl+S): it's saved as a Markdown (.md) note. Saving never overwrites; a second save makes a new copy.
- **Stopwatch**: "Show me a stopwatch widget." Start, Stop, Lap and Reset; laps are listed with their times.
- **Countdown timer**: "Pop up a 10 minute countdown." Start, Pause and Reset; it chimes at zero. You can change
  the minutes and seconds in the window.
- **Pomodoro**: "Open the pomodoro widget." 25 minutes focus then 5 minutes break (or your own lengths: "a 50
  and 10 pomodoro widget"), with a chime at each change and a count of finished sessions.
- **World clocks**: "Show world clocks for Tokyo, New York and Cape Town." Live clocks for each place next to
  your own time. Big cities and zone names like Europe/Oslo work.
- **Calendar**: "Show me this month's calendar." / "Show the calendar for December 2026." Today is highlighted;
  Prev, Next and Today buttons (or the arrow keys) move between months.
- **Dice roller**: "Pop up a dice roller for 2d6." Choose how many dice and how many sides, then Roll.
- **Coin flip**: "Open the coin flip widget." Flip as often as you like; it keeps count of heads and tails.
- **Colour picker**: "Open a colour picker." Pick a colour and see its hex, RGB and HSL codes, each with a Copy
  button.
- **Metronome**: "Start a metronome at 90 BPM." Slide the tempo from 30 to 240 and choose the beat (the first beat
  of each bar clicks higher).
- **Ambient sound**: "Play rain sounds with a 30 minute sleep timer." Rain, white, pink or brown noise made by
  the page itself, with a volume slider and a sleep timer that fades it out.
- **Breathing exercise**: "Start a box breathing exercise." / "Do 4-7-8 breathing." A circle grows and shrinks
  with each breath, with the step and seconds written underneath. If your PC is set to reduce motion, you just
  get the words and the count.
- **Typing test**: "Give me a typing speed test." Type the sentence shown; you get words per minute and accuracy.
  New sentence for another go.
- **Picker wheel**: "Spin a wheel to choose between pizza, curry and tacos." Edit the options (one per line) and
  press Spin. Tell Alfred sends the result to Alfred so he can say it.
