# The web on the Alfred screen

Alfred can bring things from the internet onto his own screen as pop-up windows, so you never have to open the
web browser. Windows can be dragged, resized and closed; tapping a list item, tile or button sends a line to
Alfred just as if you had said it. All of these use free public services with no account, and Alfred only sends
a search word or a place name.

## News and reading
- **News to tap and read**: "Show me the BBC tech news on screen." A list of headlines pops up; tap one and
  Alfred reads that story inside a reader window.
- **Wikipedia article**: "Show me the Wikipedia article about Ada Lovelace." The summary and lead picture pop up,
  with a **Read full article** button.
- **Link preview**: "What's this link? https://example.com/story" A card with the page's title, description,
  picture and site, plus **Read it here** and **Save as a note** buttons.
- **Save a web page as a note**: "Save this page as a note: https://example.com/story" The page's words are
  saved as a Markdown note in Ideas (or the folder you name), and the note pops up.

## Pictures
- **Web picture**: "Show me this picture: https://example.com/cat.jpg" It pops up with a **Save to Ideas**
  button (say another folder to change it) that downloads it into your memory folder.
- **Dog pictures**: "Show me a dog." or "Show me a cocker spaniel." A random dog with an **Another one** button.
- **Cat pictures**: "Show me a cat picture." A random cat with **Another one**.

## Books
- **Book search**: "Find books by Terry Pratchett." A gallery of covers from Open Library pops up; tap a book to
  add it to your reading list.

## Maps and places
- **Map**: "Show me Whitby on a map." An OpenStreetMap map of the place pops up, with **Weather here** and
  **Rain radar** buttons.
- **Distance as the crow flies**: "How far is Leeds from Edinburgh as the crow flies?" Alfred says the miles and
  kilometres; a map of both places pops up with both sets of coordinates (the pin marks the first place).
- **Country facts**: "Tell me about Japan the country." The flag, capital, population, area, languages and
  currency pop up.
- **Rain radar**: "Show me the rain radar." The latest radar picture around your home city (or a place you
  name), drawn over a map, with a **Refresh** button.

## Radio and podcasts
- **Find a radio station**: "Find jazz radio stations." A player pops up with the stations; tap one to play it,
  and use Play/Stop and the volume slider.
- **Play a radio station**: "Play Radio Paradise." The best match starts playing in the player window.
- **Find a podcast**: "Find podcasts about history." A gallery of podcast covers; tap one to see its episodes.
- **Podcast episodes**: "Show me the latest episodes of The Rest Is History." The newest episodes appear in the
  player; tap one to play it.

## Recipes and drinks
- **Recipe search**: "Find me a recipe for lasagne." A gallery of dishes with pictures (TheMealDB); tap one for
  the full recipe.
- **Cocktails and mocktails**: "Find a mojito recipe." or "Show me some mocktails." Drinks with pictures from
  TheCocktailDB; tap one for the recipe.
- **Recipe from a web page**: "Show me the recipe on https://example.com/pancakes" The recipe pops up with an
  ingredients checklist to tick while you cook and numbered steps.
- **Save a recipe**: press **Save recipe** in the recipe window, or say "Save that recipe." It goes into your
  Recipes folder with your other recipes.
- **Ingredients to the shopping list**: press **Add ingredients to shopping list**, or say "Add those
  ingredients to my shopping list."

## Not included
- YouTube search needs an API key, so Alfred can't search YouTube. He can still play a YouTube link you give
  him ("Play this on screen: https://youtu.be/...").
- Driving or walking directions also need an account, so Alfred gives the distance as the crow flies instead.
