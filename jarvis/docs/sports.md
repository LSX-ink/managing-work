# Sports

Following your teams and keeping score. Nearly everything pops up in a window on the Alfred screen, and Alfred
says the headline in a sentence. Live football comes from TheSportsDB and Formula 1 from the Jolpica F1 API. Both
are free and need no sign-up. Only a league, a team name or a match number is ever sent. Your favourites, predictions and scores
stay in `sports-*.json` files in your memory folder. (For sport news headlines see [Live info](live-info.md). For
workouts and steps see [Health plus](health-plus.md).)

## Football and F1

- **League tables**: "Show me the Premier League table." Also the Championship, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1
  and Scottish Premiership, and past seasons ("the 2024-2025 La Liga table"). A table of position, played, won, drawn,
  lost, goal difference and points pops up.
- **A team's matches**: "When do Celtic play next?" / "What were Arsenal's last results?" A window pops up with club badges,
  the last five results (with W, D or L) and the next fixtures.
- **F1 standings**: "Show the F1 drivers' standings." / "the constructors' championship". A table pops up.
- **Last Grand Prix**: "Who won the last Grand Prix?" The full results table pops up.
- **Next Grand Prix**: "When's the next F1 race?" A live countdown to lights out pops up, with the circuit.

## My teams

- **Favourite teams**: "Save Arsenal as a favourite team." / "What are my favourite teams?" / "Remove Arsenal from my
  favourites." (Alfred asks you to confirm first.) A list pops up. Click a team to see how they did.
- **How did my team do?**: "How did my team do?" (all your favourites) or "How did Rangers do?" Their latest results
  pop up.
- **Sports summary**: "Give me my sports summary." Pops up each favourite's last result and next match, and the next
  F1 race.
- **Match reminders**: "Remind me an hour before Arsenal's next match." / "…15 minutes before the Chelsea game."
  Alfred sets an ordinary reminder, which he says aloud at that time.
- **Score predictions**: "I predict Spurs 1 Arsenal 2 for Arsenal's next game." Then later: "How are my predictions
  doing?" Alfred checks the real results: 3 points for the exact score, 1 for the right result. A table of your predictions pops up.

## Keeping score

- **Scoreboard for any game**: "Keep score for Scrabble with Amy, Ben and Cara." A scoreboard pops up with − and +
  buttons for each player (switch between steps of 1, 5 and 10), Undo, Reset and a history of changes. The leader is
  lit up.
- **Save the scores**: press **Save** on the scoreboard, or say "Save the Scrabble scores: Amy 312, Ben 290."
- **Past scores**: "Show my saved scores." / "Who wins most at Uno?" A table of past games pops up, and Alfred says who
  has won most.
- **Darts 501**: "Start a game of darts with Sam and Alex." (or "301"). A scoreboard pops up showing what each player has left,
  their three-dart average and last throws, and a checkout suggestion once they're on a finish. Type each visit in
  the box, or say "Sam scored 60." It knows about busts. When a leg is won, say "Next leg" (the other player starts)
  or "Undo that".
- **Checkouts**: "How do I check out 121?" Alfred gives the usual finish for any score from 2 to 170, ending on a double.
- **Tennis, badminton and table tennis**: "Score a tennis match between Sam and Alex, best of three." A scoreboard
  pops up with Point buttons for each player and Undo. It follows the real rules: love, 15, 30, 40, deuce and
  advantage, sets with tie-breaks at six all, and who serves next (● marks the server). Badminton is to 21 (capped at
  30). Table tennis is to 11, and service changes every two points. Say "Point to Alex" or click the buttons.

## Game night and the course

- **Golf courses**: "Save the course Hilltop with pars 4, 3, 5, 4, 4, 3, 4, 5, 4." (9 or 18 holes.)
- **Golf rounds**: "Start a round of golf at Hilltop with me and Dad." Then "I got a 5 on hole 3" or "Dad took 4 on
  the 2nd". Alfred says birdie, par or bogey and how you stand against par.
- **Scorecard**: "Show my golf scorecard." Every hole, with Out, In and Total, and how each player stands against par.
- **Pub quiz or game night league**: "Record tonight's quiz: Amy 30, Ben 25, Cara 28." Then "Show the quiz league table."
  A running table of nights played, wins, total points and average pops up.
- **Team picker**: "Split Tom, Jo, Sam, Ali, Kim and Lee into two teams." Add skill ratings to balance them:
  "Tom 5, Jo 3…". The teams pop up with a Shuffle again button.
- **Rules quick reference**: "Explain offside." / "What's LBW?" / "How does a tennis tie-break work?" Also darts,
  snooker points, badminton, table tennis and rugby points. The rule pops up with buttons for other sports.
