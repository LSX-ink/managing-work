# Health and fitness

Things to say to Alfred. Most of them pop a window up on the Alfred screen: a chart, a table, a checklist or a
live timer. Everything is saved in your memory folder on this PC (the `health-plus-*.json` files), nowhere else.

These are logs and tools only. Alfred never gives medical advice; if anything worries you, contact your GP or
NHS 111. Water, sleep, mood and medication are in the wellbeing log (see home-and-life.md); steps, gym workouts
and goals are in learning-and-goals.md; BMI is in the calculators.

## Logs

1. **Weight**: "I weigh 78.4 kilos." / "I'm 12 stone 4." / "Show my weight chart."
   Pops up a chart of your weigh-ins with a 7-day average line, and says how the average moved on the week before.
2. **Blood pressure and heart rate**: "Log blood pressure 128 over 82, pulse 70." / "Show my blood pressure."
   Pops up a table of readings, each with its NHS band (ideal, slightly raised, high, low) and the NHS guide
   figures for information. Press **Chart** for a chart of both numbers and your heart rate.
3. **Food and calories**: "Log porridge, 350 calories, 12 grams protein." / "How many calories today?" /
   "Show my calories this week." / "Set my calorie goal to 1,800."
   Pops up today's food table with the total against your goal, or a bar chart of the week.
4. **Caffeine**: "I've had a latte." / "Two cups of tea." / "How much caffeine today?"
   Pops up today's drinks with the total in mg and when you had your last coffee. "Show the caffeine in common
   drinks" pops up the table Alfred uses.
5. **Symptom diary**: "Log a headache, severity 6, after lots of screen time." / "Show my symptom diary."
   Pops up a history table. It's just a diary; it never diagnoses anything.
6. **Weekly summary**: "How has my health been this week?"
   Pops up a table of weight, blood pressure, heart rate, calories, runs, sleep, water and caffeine for the last
   7 days next to the week before (sleep and water come from the wellbeing log).
7. **Mistakes**: "Remove my last weight entry." Alfred reads it back and asks you to confirm first.

## Fitness

8. **Interval (HIIT) timer**: "Start an interval timer, 40 seconds work, 20 rest, 8 rounds, and save it as Tabata." /
   "Start my Tabata timer."
   Pops up a live timer that shows each phase, what's next and a progress bar, and beeps at each change (three
   short beeps before it). Pause and Restart are on the window.
9. **Workout plans**: "What workout plans are there?" / "Start the 5k plan." / "What's today's workout?" /
   "I've done the warm-up." / "Mark today's session done."
   Plans: beginner full body (sessions A and B), a 9-week Couch-to-5K style run plan, a stretching routine and desk
   exercises. Today's session pops up as a checklist; ticking a box tells Alfred.
10. **Stretch or desk break**: "Give me a desk break." / "Start a stretching routine, 40 seconds a move."
    Pops up the same live timer, one move at a time with a beep between moves.
11. **Running log**: "I ran 5k in 27 minutes 45." / "I ran 3 miles in 26:10 yesterday." / "Show my running."
    Alfred says your pace per km and per mile and spots new 5k and 10k personal bests. Pops up a chart of km per
    week for the last 8 weeks, with your personal bests, longest run and fastest pace.
12. **Heart-rate zones**: "What are my heart-rate zones? I'm 40 and my resting heart rate is 60."
    Pops up a table of the five zones using 220 minus your age, and the Karvonen method when you give a resting rate.
13. **Sleep cycles**: "When should I go to bed to wake at 7?" / "If I go to bed at 11, when should I wake up?"
    Pops up bedtimes (or wake times) for 3 to 6 ninety-minute sleep cycles, allowing 14 minutes to fall asleep.

## Records

14. **Appointments and check-ups**: "Add a dentist appointment on 12 November at 3 pm." / "My last eye test was on
    3 March 2025." / "What appointments have I got?"
    Pops up upcoming appointments with a countdown, plus when check-ups are due: the dentist every 6 months and an
    eye test every 2 years, counted from your last visit.
15. **Vaccinations and health records**: "Save my flu jab, 12 October, at Boots." / "Show my health records."
    Pops up a table. Removing one asks you to confirm first.
16. **Cycle tracker**: "My period started today." / "When's my next period due?"
    Pops up a two-month calendar marking period starts (•), the predicted next start (?) and today (<). The
    prediction is a simple average of your recent cycles, not exact. Private and kept only on this PC.

## Reminders

These use Alfred's ordinary reminders (said aloud with a chime). Reminders repeat every day, every weekday or every
week, so a schedule is set up as one repeating reminder for each time of day, at most 12 a day.

17. **Eye breaks and posture**: "Remind me to take eye breaks while I work." (20-20-20: every hour from 9 to 5 on
    weekdays, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds) / "Posture reminders every 2 hours from 10 to 4."
18. **Water**: "Remind me to drink water every 2 hours." (9 am to 9 pm every day unless you say other times)
    "Which health reminders are on?" pops up a list; "Stop my water reminders" asks you to confirm first.
