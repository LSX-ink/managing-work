# Career and job hunting

Things to say to Alfred. Everything is saved in your memory folder on this PC (files called `career-...`) and nothing
is sent anywhere. Most answers pop up a window on the Alfred screen. Cover letters are saved as ordinary files in
Documents/Letters.

Already elsewhere, so not repeated here: take-home pay ("What's my take-home on £35,000?", see calculators), a full
mock interview where Alfred asks the questions and marks your answers ("Practise a job interview", see modes), and
CV editing ("Put this in the skills section of my CV", see documents).

## Job applications

1. **Add an application**: "I applied to Acme for a nurse job, paying 32k." Pops up: the job board. A follow-up
   reminder is set for a week later unless you say otherwise.
2. **Move it along**: "Move Acme to interview." / "Acme rejected me." Stages: wishlist, applied, interview, offer,
   rejected. Pops up: the board.
3. **Kanban board**: "Show my job board." Pops up: a column per stage. Click a job to see it, or click its arrow to
   move it to the next stage.
4. **List**: "List my job applications." / "Which jobs are at interview?" Pops up: a table with stage, pay and follow-up date.
5. **One application**: "Show my Acme application." Pops up: stage, pay, link, history and research notes.
6. **Follow-ups due**: "Which applications need chasing?" Pops up: a list; clicking one tells Alfred you chased it.
7. **Set a follow-up**: "Remind me to chase Beta next week." (or "clear the follow-up for Beta")
8. **Chased them**: "I've followed up with Acme." The next nudge is set a week ahead.
9. **Company research notes**: "Add a note to Acme: they value kindness, new ward opening." Pops up: the notes.
10. **Show research**: "What did I note about Acme?" Pops up: the list of notes.
11. **Remove**: "Remove the Beta application." Asks you to confirm first.
12. **Weekly job-search stats**: "How's my job search going?" Pops up: a bar chart of applications per week for
    eight weeks; Alfred says how many reached interview, offers and rejections.

## Interview preparation

13. **Question bank**: "Show me competency interview questions." Types: behavioural, competency, strengths,
    situational and technical. Pops up: a list; tap a question to plan an answer.
14. **One question**: "Ask me a strengths question." Pops up: the question with **Plan a STAR answer** and **Another**.
15. **STAR answer planner**: "Plan a STAR answer for the time I led a team: the situation was a new project, I
    planned the rota, and it finished early." Pops up: four boxes, Situation, Task, Action and Result. Empty boxes
    can be tapped to fill in. Saved per question; say more later and it adds to the same answer.
16. **Show a saved answer**: "Show my STAR answer for leading a team."
17. **All saved answers**: "Show my STAR answers." Pops up: a list with how many parts are filled in.
18. **Salary negotiation script**: "Give me a negotiation script, I want 45k and they offered 40k." Pops up: a
    six-step script, using your brag-file wins as evidence.

## Paperwork

19. **Cover letter template**: "Save my cover letter template: Hi {company}, I'd like the {role} job..." You can use
    {name}, {company}, {role}, {profile}, {experience}, {skills} and {date}. Without one, a sensible default is used.
20. **Write a cover letter**: "Write a cover letter for Acme." Fills the template from your CV sections (read only)
    and the role from your tracked application, saves it in Documents/Letters and pops it up.
21. **Brag file**: "Add to my brag file: cut waiting times by 20 percent." Pops up: a table of wins, newest first.
22. **Show the brag file**: "Show my wins." / "What wins do I have about training?"
23. **Skills gap**: "For a data analyst job I need Excel, SQL, Python and Tableau; I know Tableau." Your CV skills
    count too. Pops up: a table of skills marked have or gap, with Learn buttons for gaps.
24. **Show the gap list**: "Show my skills gap for data analyst."
25. **Skill learned**: "I've learned Python for the data analyst role." Ticks it off.

## Offers, contacts and goals

26. **Save an offer**: "Acme offered 40k, 30 minutes commute, 25 days holiday, gym and private health." When you
    have two, they pop up side by side with a weighted score out of 10 and bars for each criterion.
27. **Compare offers**: "Compare my job offers." Pops up: the side-by-side table.
28. **What matters most**: "Pay matters most, 60 percent, commute 20, holiday 10, benefits 10." Re-scores the offers.
29. **Remove an offer**: "Remove the Beta offer." Asks you to confirm first.
30. **Networking contact**: "Add a networking contact, Priya Shah at Acme, met at the meetup, chase her in two
    weeks." (Contacts are kept by name only; your people notebook is separate.) Pops up: a table of contacts.
31. **Contacts to chase**: "Who should I follow up with?" Pops up: the table, with **Spoke to** buttons for those due.
32. **Followed up with a contact**: "I've followed up with Priya." The next reminder is set 30 days on.
33. **Career goal**: "Add a career goal: become team lead by June 2027." Pops up: a tick list of milestones.
34. **Add a milestone**: "Add a milestone: do a management course."
35. **Tick a milestone**: "I've finished the management course milestone." (or tick it in the window)
36. **Show a goal**: "How's my team lead goal going?"
