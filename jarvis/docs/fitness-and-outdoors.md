# Fitness and getting outdoors

Workouts, runs, walks and the great British outdoors. Most answers pop a window up on the Alfred screen: a plan, a
live timer, a chart or a checklist. Your logs are saved in one file, `active.json`, in your memory folder on this PC.

Only "Is it good weather for a run?" goes online: it sends just the city name to Open-Meteo, which is free and needs no
key. Alfred gives everyday guidance, never medical advice; if something worries you, see your GP or call NHS 111.

Already covered elsewhere, so not repeated here: Tabata and interval timers, the couch-to-5K plan, desk stretches,
the running log with 5k and 10k bests, heart-rate zones, weight, steps and water (health-plus.md, trackers.md and
learning-and-goals.md), and UV, pollen and air quality (live-info.md).

## Workouts

1. **Build a workout**: "Build me a 20 minute upper body workout with dumbbells." / "Give me a 30 minute bodyweight
   workout." Focus is full body, upper body, lower body, core or cardio; kit is bodyweight, dumbbell or gym.
   Pops up a table of warm-up, moves and cool-down. **Start timer** starts it.
2. **Guided workout timer**: "Start the timer for that workout." / "Start a 15 minute core workout timer."
   Pops up a big countdown with WORK, REST and GET READY phases, the exercise name, **Next:** the following move, the
   round, and beeps (three short ones before each change). Pause, Skip and Restart are on the window.
3. **EMOM timer**: "Start a 12 minute EMOM: 10 press-ups then 15 squats." A new move starts every minute on the
   minute; do the reps and rest for whatever time is left.
4. **Your own circuit**: "Circuit timer: star jumps, skipping, burpees, 45 seconds on, 15 off, 4 rounds."
5. **Stretch routines**: "Give me a morning stretch." / "After-run stretch." / "Pre-run warm-up." / "Lower back
   stretches." / "Evening stretch." Pops up step cards: press **Next** for each move, and **Start 30 seconds** for a
   beeping timer on that move.
6. **How to do an exercise**: "How do I do a press-up?" / "How do I deadlift?"
   Pops up how to do it, a common mistake and an easier version. About 30 exercises: squats, lunges, planks,
   burpees, dumbbell rows, bench press and more.
7. **List the exercises**: "What exercises can you show me?" / "List the gym exercises." Pops up a table.

## Activity log

8. **Log a run, walk, ride or hike**: "I walked 8 km in 1 hour 40." / "I cycled 20 miles in 1:30:00." / "Log a 5k
   run in 27:45." Alfred says your pace (or speed for rides) and tells you when it's your fastest.
9. **Weekly distance**: "Show my weekly distance." / "How far have I walked this week?" Pops up a bar chart of
   the last 8 weeks and this week's total.
10. **Personal bests**: "Show my personal bests." Pops up the longest, fastest and longest-time for each activity,
    and your biggest week.
11. **History**: "Show my recent activities." Pops up a table.
12. **Undo**: "Remove my last activity." Alfred says what it is and asks you to confirm first.
13. **Rest day**: "Should I rest today?" Looks at the last 7 days of activities and strength sessions, says whether to
    rest, go easy or train, and pops up a table of the week.

## Strength

14. **Log a lift**: "I squatted 3 sets of 5 at 100 kilos." Says your estimated one-rep max and spots personal bests.
15. **Strength progress**: "Show my squat progress." / "Show my lifts." Pops up a line chart of your estimated
    one-rep max, or a table of all your lifts.

## Fitness challenges

Each challenge has a daily target that grows. The plank, squats, press-ups and sit-ups plans have a rest day every
seventh day; a rest day keeps your streak going.

16. **Start a challenge**: "Start a 30 day plank challenge." / "Start a 30 day star jumps challenge, starting at 20
    and adding 2 a day."
17. **Tick off today**: "I did today's plank challenge." / "I did my plank yesterday." Says the streak and tomorrow's
    target.
18. **Challenge calendar**: "Show my plank challenge." Pops up a calendar: bright days done, dashed rest days,
    outlined today, dim missed days. Tap a day for its target. **Done today** is a button.
19. **Stop a challenge**: "Stop my plank challenge." Alfred asks you to confirm first.

## Calculators

20. **Pace and speed**: "What's my pace if I run 10k in 52 minutes?" Pops up pace per km and per mile, speed, and what
    that pace would give over 5k, 10k, a half marathon and a marathon.
21. **Finish time**: "How long is a half marathon at 5:45 per km?" / "10 miles at 6 mph?"
22. **Race predictor**: "I ran a 5k in 25:00. What could I do for 10k?" Pops up predicted times for 5k, 10k, half
    and marathon (estimates).
23. **Splits**: "Give me splits for a 50 minute 10k." / "...per mile." Pops up a table with each split and the running total.
24. **Calories burned**: "How many calories do I burn walking briskly for an hour at 70 kilos?" Pops up the total and
    a comparison with other activities for the same time (a rough MET-based estimate).
25. **MET table**: "Show the MET table." Pops up how hard each activity is.
26. **One-rep max**: "What's my one-rep max if I lift 80 kilos for 5?" Pops up training percentages.

## Outdoors

27. **Good weather for it?**: "Is it good weather for a run today?" / "...a bike ride in Leeds?" / "...a walk?" /
    "...a hike?" Scores every daylight hour left today for temperature, rain and wind and pops up a bar chart. Alfred
    names the best time and adds the day's UV.
28. **Hiking kit list**: "What do I need for a day walk?" / "Hill walking kit." / "Winter hill kit." / "Overnight trip kit."
    Pops up a tick list.
29. **The Countryside Code**: "What's the Countryside Code?" Pops up the points in plain words (England and Wales, with a
    note on Scotland).
30. **Hill-walking safety**: "Give me the hill walking safety checklist." Pops up a tick list, including how to call
    Mountain Rescue (dial 999 and ask for Police, then Mountain Rescue).
31. **Save a route**: "Save my route Ilkley Moor, 8 km, muddy after rain." Saving it again updates it.
32. **My routes**: "Show my walking routes." Pops up a table with distance and notes.
33. **Remove a route**: "Delete the Ilkley Moor route." Alfred asks you to confirm first.
