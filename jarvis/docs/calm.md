# Calm and mindfulness

Gentle tools for slowing down. None of this is medical advice. If you ever say something that sounds like self-harm or a
crisis, Alfred stops and points you to **Samaritans (116 123, free, any time)** and **NHS 111** (and 999 in an emergency).
Everything is saved only on your PC, in `calm-*.json` files in the memory folder. Sounds are made by the page itself.

Already elsewhere, so not repeated here: the plain breathing and ambient-noise widgets (see `widgets.md`) and adding
"three good things" (see `routines.md`); the look-back below reads those same notes.

## Breathing and meditation (`calm_exercises`)

1. **Box breathing**: "Start box breathing." A circle grows and shrinks (in 4, hold 4, out 4, hold 4) with an optional soft tone.
2. **4-7-8 breathing**: "Do 4-7-8 breathing for five minutes."
3. **Coherent breathing**: "Start coherent breathing." Slow and even, 5.5 seconds in and out.
4. **Physiological sigh**: "Do a physiological sigh." Two breaths in, one long sigh out.
5. **Your own rhythm**: "Breathe in for 4, hold 2, out for 6, silently." A circle with your own timings; `tone` off for silent.
6. **Breathing menu**: "What breathing exercises do you know?" A table with a button for each.
7. **Meditation timer**: "Meditate for 15 minutes with a bell every 5." A clock with a start bell, interval bells and an end bell.
8. **Body scan**: "Guide me through a body scan." Steps appear one by one with pauses; Pause, Back, Next, and an optional "Read aloud".
9. **5-4-3-2-1 grounding**: "Ground me with 5-4-3-2-1."
10. **Loving-kindness**: "Guide me through loving-kindness."
11. **Progressive muscle relaxation**: "Guide me through muscle relaxation."
12. **Sleepy wind-down script**: "Guide me through the sleepy wind-down."
13. **Guide menu**: "What guided calm do you have?" A list; tap one to start.
14. **Calm-down menu**: "I'm feeling anxious, what can I do?" (also angry, overwhelmed, restless, sad, tired, lonely). A list of quick ideas; with no feeling you get a list to pick from.
15. **Support numbers**: "I need to talk to someone." Samaritans and NHS 111 pop up.

## Mind (`calm_mind`)

16. **Daily affirmation**: "Give me today's affirmation." Same one all day; "Another" button for a different one.
17. **Add your own affirmation**: "Add an affirmation: I am doing enough."
18. **My affirmations**: "Show my affirmations." A list.
19. **Remove an affirmation**: "Remove my affirmation about enough." Alfred checks with you first.
20. **Kindness idea of the day**: "What's my kindness idea today?" With an "I did it" button.
21. **Log a kind act**: "I held the door for a stranger, log it as kindness."
22. **Grateful a month ago**: "What was I grateful for a month ago?" (or "a year ago"). A list from your good-things notes.
23. **Add a worry**: "Add a worry: the boiler might break." Parked until worry time.
24. **Worry list**: "Show my worry list." Tap a worry to let it go.
25. **Worry time**: "It's worry time." Goes through them one at a time: can you act, or let go?
26. **Set worry time**: "Set my worry time to 6pm."
27. **Let it go**: "Let go of the boiler worry." It is marked released, not deleted.
28. **Clear released worries**: "Clear my released worries." Deletes them for good, after you confirm.
29. **Calm check-in**: "I'm a 3 out of 5 calm right now."
30. **Calm chart**: "Show my calm check-ins." A line chart of the last two weeks.

## Mindful minutes (`calm_log`)

31. **Log minutes**: "I meditated for 10 minutes." Says today's total and your streak.
32. **Today**: "How many mindful minutes today?"
33. **Streak chart**: "Show my mindful minutes streak." A bar chart of 14 days.
34. **Summary**: "Mindful minutes this month?"
35. **History**: "Show my recent sessions." A table.
36. **Daily goal**: "Set my mindful goal to 10 minutes."
37. **Undo**: "Undo my last mindful entry." Alfred checks with you first.

The breathing and meditation pop-ups have a "Log it" button that logs the time for you.

## Evenings (`calm_evening`)

38. **Wind-down checklist**: "Show my wind-down." A tick list for the evening (phone on charge, lights down...). Tap to tick; it starts fresh each day.
39. **Tick a step**: "Tick off the warm drink."
40. **Add or remove a step**: "Add stretching to my wind-down." / "Remove tidy up." (removal asks first)
41. **When to start**: "I want to be in bed by 10:30, when should I start winding down?"
42. **Sleep tips**: "Give me some gentle sleep tips."
43. **Digital detox**: "Start a one hour digital detox." A countdown pops up; "I'm done" button.
44. **Detox status / end / history**: "How long is left on my detox?" / "End my detox." / "Show my detoxes."
