# Games and fun

Things to say to Alfred. Games and scores last until Alfred restarts.

- **Trivia quiz**: "Give me a trivia question." / "Ask me an easy history question." Then answer with the letter or the answer: "B." / "Canberra." Say "I give up" to hear the answer.
- **Guess the number**: "Let's play guess the number." Then say numbers; Alfred says higher or lower and counts your guesses.
- **Rock, paper, scissors**: "Rock, paper, scissors: I choose paper."
- **Scores**: "What's the score?"
- **Riddles**: "Tell me a riddle." Then guess, or say "What's the answer?"
- **Word scramble**: "Give me a word scramble." / "Give me a hint." / "Is it pirate?"
- **Magic 8-ball**: "Magic 8-ball, will it be sunny on Saturday?"
- **Would you rather**: "Ask me a would you rather."
- **Jokes**: "Tell me a joke." (no repeats until you've heard them all)
- **Motivation**: "Give me a motivational quote."
- **Fun fact**: "Tell me a fun fact."
- **This or that**: "Should I have pasta or a curry tonight?"

Trivia questions come from the free Open Trivia Database on the internet; everything else works offline.
