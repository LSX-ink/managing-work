# People: a notebook for keeping in touch

Things to say to Alfred about the people in your life. Everything is saved only in your memory folder on this PC
(files called `people-...json`) and is never sent to any website. Phone numbers, emails and addresses show on the
pop-up windows, but Alfred won't read them out loud unless you ask him to.

Removing anything (a person, a family link, a special date, a card-list entry, an invite list) always asks you to
confirm first. Birthdays stay with the birthdays ability ("Sam's birthday is 8 October") and gift ideas with the
gift ideas ability; the person card shows both.

## Who's who

1. **Add someone**: "Add Sam to my people, a school friend, phone 07700 900123."
   Saved quietly; add an email or address the same way.
2. **Change someone's details**: "Sam's new address is 4 Mill Lane, Leeds." / "Rename Sam to Samantha."
3. **Remove someone**: "Remove Jo from my people." Alfred checks with you first.
4. **List everyone**: "Show my people." Pops up: a table of names, how you know them, groups and when you were last in touch.
5. **Person card**: "Show Sam's card." Pops up: everything about Sam: how you know them, contact details, groups,
   last contact, birthday countdown, special dates, family, likes and dislikes, gift ideas, notes and recent
   conversations, with buttons for "I've been in touch", "Conversations" and "Family tree".
   Want the number read out? "What's Sam's phone number?"
6. **Notes about people**: "Remember Sam's daughter is called Mia and loves horses." / "Sam likes jazz." /
   "Sam doesn't like mushrooms." They appear on Sam's card.
7. **Search by any detail**: "Who loves horses?" / "Who lives in York?" / "Who did I talk to about the wedding?"
   Pops up: a list of matching people; click one to open their card.
8. **Groups and circles**: "Put Sam and Jo in Uni friends." / "Take Jo out of Uni friends." / "Show my groups." /
   "Who's in my Family group?" Pops up: a table of groups (with a button for each) or the people in one group.
9. **Family tree**: "Jean is Sam's mum." / "Mia is Sam's daughter." / "Alex is Sam's partner." /
   "Show Sam's family tree." Pops up: a tree with one row per generation, partners side by side and lines down to
   children. Click a name to open their card. People you haven't added yet are added for you.
10. **Call reminders**: "Remind me to call Mum on Sunday at 6, every week."
    Uses Alfred's normal reminders, so it's said out loud when it's due.

## Keeping in touch

11. **How often**: "I want to call Mum every 7 days." / "Stop the keep-in-touch goal for Mum."
12. **Log a catch-up**: "I called Mum." / "I saw Sam yesterday; we talked about her new job and the holiday."
13. **Who's overdue**: "Who haven't I been in touch with lately?"
    Pops up: a tick list of people you're due to contact; ticking one logs that you've been in touch.
14. **Conversation log**: "Show my conversations with Mum."
    Pops up: a table of every catch-up with the date, how (call, visit, text...), what you talked about and notes.
15. **Weekly social summary**: "How was my week with people?"
    Pops up: who you were in touch with this week, who's due a catch-up, birthdays and special dates in the next
    two weeks, thank-yous to send and promises due, with buttons to open each list.

## Occasions, thank-yous and promises

16. **Anniversaries and special dates**: "Sam and Alex's wedding anniversary is 20 June 2015." /
    "Show my special dates." Pops up: a table of dates with countdowns and how many years each marks.
17. **Thank-you tracker**: "Gran gave me a scarf." / "Sam did me a favour: a lift to the airport." /
    "I've sent Gran a thank-you." / "Show my thank-you list."
    Pops up: a tick list of gifts and favours; ticking one marks the thank-you as sent.
18. **Promises and favours**: "I promised Jo I'd lend him the ladder by Friday." / "Sam owes me a coffee." /
    "I've lent Jo the ladder." / "What have I promised people?"
    Pops up: a tick list of what you owe and what's owed to you, with due dates.

## Lists of people

19. **Holiday card list**: "Put my Family group and Jo on the Christmas card list." / "I've done Mum's card." /
    "Show my card list." Pops up: a tick list with each person's address; tick them off as you write them.
20. **Invite list builder**: "Make a birthday party invite list from Uni friends plus Mum." /
    "Take Jo off the party list." / "Show the birthday party list." / "Show my invite lists."
    Pops up: a table of the people invited, or a list of all your invite lists.
