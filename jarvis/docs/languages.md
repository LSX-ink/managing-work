# Language studio

Learn Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (European) or Japanese with Alfred. Everything pops up on the
Alfred screen; click things instead of typing. Words are read aloud in the right accent by your browser's voice, so
use the **Speak** buttons (and **Slow**) on any card. Your progress is saved in `languages.json` in the memory
folder.

Already in Alfred, and not repeated here: the travel phrasebook ("Show me the Japanese phrasebook", in
[travel](travel.md)), your own flashcard decks, phrase of the day and language quiz in
[learning and goals](learning-and-goals.md), the spoken conversation partner in [modes](modes.md), and the
vocabulary builder in [discover](discover.md).

## Choosing a language and decks

1. **List languages**: "Which languages can I learn?" A list pops up; click one to pick it.
2. **Set a language**: "I'm learning Spanish." Every later phrase can then leave the language out.
3. **See the decks**: "Show my Spanish decks." Greetings, food, travel, family, time, home and numbers, each with
   how many words are learned and due.

## Flashcards with spaced repetition

4. **Flip cards**: "Flashcards for the food deck." A card pops up; click it (or press space) to flip, then grade
   yourself Again, Hard, Good or Easy (keys 1 to 4). "Again" cards come round once more in the same round.
5. **Review what is due**: "Review my due cards." Only cards whose due date has come.
6. **Save the review**: click **Save my review** at the end and Alfred stores the new due dates (again = today,
   good = 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, then 60 days).
7. **Word of the day**: "What's my French word of the day?" One card with a Speak button and an "Add to my words"
   button.

## Your own words

8. **Add a word**: "Add the German word der Hund, meaning dog."
9. **See your words**: "Show my German words." A table with each word's review date.
10. **Remove a word**: "Remove der Hund from my words." Alfred asks first.
11. **Look a word up**: "How do I say milk in Spanish?" Searches the starter decks and your words.

## Hearing it and saying it

12. **Speak**: "Say 'Buenos días, ¿cómo estás?' in Spanish." Or "Let me hear the Japanese greetings." A list with
    Speak and Speak all.
13. **Pronunciation tips**: "Give me French pronunciation tips." Five short tips.

## Quizzes and drills

14. **Multiple-choice quiz**: "Quiz me on Italian travel words." Four buttons per question.
15. **Typing quiz**: "Typing quiz on the family words, from English." Accents are forgiven with a gentle note.
16. **Gender drill**: "Drill me on German genders." Masculine, feminine or neuter for each noun.
17. **Article drill**: "Drill me on French articles." Pick le or la, der, die or das, el or la.
18. **Numbers**: "Practise Spanish numbers up to 20." Number to word by default; ask for "word to number" for the other way.
19. **Telling the time**: "Practise telling the time in German." A clock face pops up and you pick or type how
    to say it (or the other way round).
20. **Save a score**: click **Save my score** at the end. Words you missed go back into your review queue.

## Verbs

21. **Conjugation table**: "Conjugate ir in Spanish." Present, past and future tabs with a Speak button on every
    line. Regular verbs and the key irregular ones for each language (Japanese shows polite and plain forms).
22. **Verb list**: "Which French verbs do you have?"
23. **Verb drill**: "Drill me on the past tense of être." Type or pick the right form.

## Role-play

24. **Scenes**: "What role-plays can I do in Portuguese?" Ordering in a café and asking the way to the station.
25. **Play one**: "Role-play the café in Italian." The other person speaks, you pick the right reply from three.
    Show or hide the English; press Speak on any line.

## Progress

26. **Log minutes**: "I studied Spanish for 15 minutes."
27. **Streak**: "What's my language streak?" Any day with minutes, reviews or a quiz counts.
28. **Daily goal**: "Set my language goal to 20 minutes."
29. **Dashboard**: "Show my Spanish progress." Streak, words learned, cards due, the goal bar, a 14-day minutes
    chart and a bar per deck.
30. **Minutes chart**: "Chart my study minutes for the last month."
31. **Deck progress**: "How am I doing on each Spanish deck?" A table.
32. **What is due**: "How many Spanish cards are due this week?" A bar chart for the next seven days.
33. **Quiz scores**: "Show my recent French quiz scores."
34. **Weak words**: "Which words do I keep getting wrong?"
35. **Start over**: "Reset my Spanish review schedule." Alfred asks first; your own words and study log stay.

The starter decks are small on purpose (ten words each, numbers to 20 then the tens). Add your own words to go
further.
