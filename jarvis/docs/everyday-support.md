# Everyday support

Gentle help for anyone who finds things hard: memory difficulties, low vision, anxiety, older people and their carers.
Everything is saved in `support.json` in your memory folder on this PC and never sent anywhere. Big text, contrast,
captions and the keyboard are in `accessibility.md`; reading web pages is in `reading-help.md`. Medicine and feeling
entries are logs only, never medical advice.

Already elsewhere, so not repeated here: "where I put things" ("My keys are in the blue bowl." / "Where are my keys?",
see `routines.md`), the plain medicine log ("I've just taken my ibuprofen", see `home-and-life.md`), the 5-4-3-2-1
grounding and breathing (see `calm.md`), general checklists (see `routines.md`) and the colour palette of a picture
(see `art-studio.md`).

## Today and simple mode (`support_day`)

1. **What day is it**: "What day is it?" A big window shows the weekday, date, morning, afternoon or evening, the time and today's plan.
2. **Add to the plan**: "Put the doctor at 2:30 on my plan." (or a day: "on Friday")
3. **Show the plan**: "What's my plan today?" A tick list pops up.
4. **Tick off the plan**: "I've done the doctor."
5. **Simple mode**: "Show me simple mode." Six huge buttons: time, weather, who to call, today's plan, where are my things, help. Press one and Alfred does it.
6. **Save a routine**: "My morning routine is: wash, dress, take my tablets, breakfast." (morning or bedtime)
7. **Show a routine**: "Show my bedtime routine." A tick list pops up, and Alfred says what is next.
8. **Tick a routine step**: "I've washed." The ticks are kept for today.
9. **Take a break**: "I'm overwhelmed." A calm card with buttons for grounding, breathing and a one minute timer.
10. **Say it slower**: "Say that again, slowly." Alfred repeats his last reply in short, simple sentences (uses the recent chat; if there is none he says so).

## People, guides and letters (`support_people`)

11. **Add an important person**: "Sam is my grandson, photo Personal/sam.jpg." (the photo is a picture already in the memory folders)
12. **Who is...?**: "Who is Sam?" or "Who is my daughter?" A big card shows the photo, name and relationship.
13. **Everyone**: "Show my important people." Photos and names pop up.
14. **Remove someone**: "Remove Sam from my people." (Alfred checks with you first.)
15. **Save a guide**: "Save a guide called washing machine: open the door, put the clothes in, add powder, press start."
16. **Show a guide**: "Show me the washing machine guide." One big step at a time with Back and Next buttons.
17. **List guides**: "What guides have I got?" Click one to open it.
18. **Delete a guide**: "Delete the washing machine guide." (Alfred checks first.)
19. **Explain a letter**: "Explain this letter: Dear customer, your account is overdue..." or "Explain the file bill.txt in Personal." Alfred explains in plain short sentences what it says, what to do and by when. Text files only; otherwise read or paste the words.

## Care and safety (`support_care`)

20. **Save a medicine**: "I take aspirin, 75mg, at 8 in the morning."
21. **I took it**: "I've taken my aspirin." Alfred logs the time, and checks twice: if it was already logged today he says stop and doesn't log it again unless you insist.
22. **Did I take it?**: "Did I take my aspirin?" Alfred answers from the log, and says not to double up if unsure.
23. **Medicines today**: "What medicines are due today?" A table shows each one and when it was taken.
24. **Remove a medicine**: "Stop tracking aspirin." (Alfred checks first.)
25. **Check-in**: "I'm feeling 4 out of 5, had a nice walk." (1 very low to 5 very good, with an optional note)
26. **Week chart for a carer**: "Show how I've felt this week." A bar chart of each day's average, with the notes.
27. **Handover note**: "Handover note: ate well, slept badly."
28. **Read handover notes**: "Show the carer handover notes." A table of the last few days.
29. **Emergency card**: "Save my emergency information: Ann Lee, 4 High Street, GP Dr Rao, allergic to penicillin, contact Jo."
30. **Show the emergency card**: "Show my emergency information." Big print name, address, GP, allergies and contact.
31. **Who to call**: "My daughter Jo is on 07700 900123." / "Who can I call?" The number is shown very big. Alfred does not dial.
32. **I'm lost**: "I'm lost, I need help." A big card with your name, home address, the person to call and 999.

## Seeing (`support_see`)

33. **Magnifier**: "Magnify the photo door.png in Personal." A window with plus, minus and Reset buttons; drag to move around, or use the mouse wheel.
34. **Colour names**: "What colours are in the photo shirt.jpg?" A table of plain names (dark blue, light green, grey) with how much of the picture each covers.
35. **Large-print shopping list**: "Show my shopping list in big print."

## Good to know

- Everything is stored in `support.json`. Delete that file to forget it all.
- Repeating a reply slowly needs the recent chat, which Alfred keeps in `.recent-chat.json`.
