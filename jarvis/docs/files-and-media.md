# Files, photos and media

Alfred can show, tidy and edit the files in your memory folders. Everything pops up in a window on the Alfred
screen, and nothing ever leaves the memory folders. Say the folder by name, e.g. "Personal" or "Personal/Holiday".

## Look at things

1. **Photo gallery**: "Show me the photos in Personal/Holiday."
   A grid of thumbnails pops up. Click one to see it big; then Prev / Next or the left and right arrow keys flick through.
2. **Slideshow**: "Play a slideshow of my holiday pictures."
   The pictures change every few seconds (say "every 10 seconds" to slow it down), with pause, back and next buttons.
3. **Music player**: "Play the music in my Music folder." (or "...on shuffle")
   A player pops up with the song list, play/pause, previous, next, shuffle and a volume slider.
4. **Video**: "Play the newest video in Work." / "Play the video called birthday."
   The video plays in a pop-up.

## Find things

5. **Recent files**: "What are my newest files?"
   The 20 newest files across all folders; click one to open it.
6. **Search by name**: "Find files called invoice."
   A list of matching files; click one to open it.
7. **Duplicates**: "Have I got any duplicate files?"
   Identical files listed in sets, and how much space they waste.
8. **Folder sizes**: "How much space does each folder use?"
   A bar chart of each memory folder's size.
9. **File info**: "How big is beach.jpg in Personal?"
   A table: size, type, dates, and the picture's size in pixels or the PDF's page count.
10. **Photo details**: "When was sunset.jpg taken, and on what camera?"
    The date taken and the camera, if the photo remembers them.

## Tidy things

11. **Rename**: "Rename beach.jpg in Personal to Cornwall beach."
12. **Move**: "Move the payslip PDF from Ideas to Work."
13. **Copy**: "Make a copy of my CV in Work." (or "copy it to Personal")
14. **Delete**: "Delete old-notes.txt from Ideas." Alfred asks you to confirm first.
15. **Zip a folder**: "Zip up my Holiday folder." A list of what went in pops up.
16. **What's in a zip**: "What's inside Holiday.zip?"
17. **Unzip**: "Unzip Holiday.zip." It unpacks into a new folder next to it. Alfred refuses zips that would unpack
    outside that folder, or to more than 500 MB or 2000 files.

## Edit pictures

Each of these saves a new copy next to the original and pops it up; the original is never changed.

18. **Resize**: "Make beach.jpg 800 pixels wide." / "Shrink beach.jpg to half size."
19. **Rotate**: "Rotate sunset.jpg 90 degrees."
20. **Convert**: "Convert the screenshot to JPG." (PNG, JPG or WebP)
21. **Black and white**: "Make beach.jpg black and white."
22. **PDF from pictures**: "Make a PDF of the pictures in Work/Receipts."
23. **GIF**: "Make a GIF from my Holiday pictures."
24. **Contact sheet**: "Make a contact sheet of Personal/Holiday." One picture with a thumbnail of each photo.

Pictures from an iPhone saved as HEIC can't be opened; set the iPhone camera to "Most Compatible" (JPG) or convert them first.
