# Films, TV and books: your watchlist

Alfred keeps a watchlist of films and shows, follows the episodes you are on, tracks the books you are reading and
helps with film nights and your book club. Everything is saved in `watchlist.json` in your memory folder. It is
separate from the older, simpler "reading list" and "watch list" abilities (see learning-and-goals.md); those are
left alone. Pop-ups use the black-and-white poster style: click a tile or a row and Alfred hears it as if you said it.

The only internet use is TVmaze (shows, episodes, cast, next airing date) and Open Library (series order). Both are
free and need no key, and only the show, series or author words you say are sent.

## Films and TV

1. **Add to the watchlist**: "Add Heat to my watchlist, it's a 170 minute thriller, tense mood." (Say "kids-safe" to flag it.)
2. **Watchlist gallery**: "Show my watchlist." Poster-style tiles pop up (a picture if there is one, else the title). Also
   "show my kids-safe films" or "show my horror films".
3. **Mark watched**: "I watched Heat, five stars, cracking shootout." Saves the 1 to 5 stars and a one-line review.
4. **What should we watch tonight**: "What should we watch tonight? Something cosy, under 100 minutes, me and the kids."
   Pops up the pick and a few alternatives, from your watchlist only. Mentioning kids shows only kids-safe items.
5. **Watched history**: "Show my watching history." Numbers, a chart by month, a chart by genre and your average rating.
6. **Top picks by genre**: "What are my top rated films by genre?" A table of your best-rated items per genre.
7. **Recommendations**: "Recommend something like my favourites." Alfred looks at your top-rated films, shows and
   books and suggests similar ones in his reply. Say "books" or "films" to narrow it.
8. **Rewatch list**: "I want to rewatch Heat." / "What's on my rewatch list?" Tiles pop up.
9. **Kids-safe flag**: "Mark Paddington as kids-safe." (or "not for kids")
10. **Remove**: "Remove Alien from my watchlist." Alfred asks you to confirm first.

## TV shows (TVmaze)

11. **Set where you are**: "I'm on season 2 episode 3 of Slow Horses."
12. **Watched the next one**: "I watched the next episode of Slow Horses." Moves on one episode (and to the next
    season if you have looked up the show's episodes).
13. **Next up**: "What am I watching and what's next?" Tiles and a table of every show you follow; tap a tile to log
    the next episode.
14. **Find a show**: "Search TV for Slow Horses." Tiles with posters; tap one to add it.
15. **Add a show**: "Add the TV show Slow Horses to my watchlist." Saves its genre, length and poster.
16. **Episode list**: "List the episodes of Slow Horses season 1." A table with titles and air dates.
17. **Next episode date**: "When is the next episode of Slow Horses?" Alfred says the date and time; a small card pops up.
18. **Cast**: "Who's in the cast of Slow Horses?" A table of actors and characters.

## Books

19. **Add a book**: "Add Dune by Frank Herbert, 600 pages, to my reading list."
20. **Progress**: "I'm on page 150 of Dune." A progress bar pops up (page 150 of 600, 25%).
21. **Finished**: "I finished Dune, five stars, epic." Rating and one-line review saved.
22. **Reading now**: "What am I reading?" Progress bars for each book.
23. **Yearly goal**: "My reading goal is 24 books this year."
24. **Goal ring**: "How's my reading goal?" A ring of books finished against the goal, with the list of books.
25. **Log pages**: "I read 20 pages today."
26. **Reading streak**: "What's my reading streak?" Streak, longest streak and a pages-per-day chart.
27. **Book notes**: "Note on Dune page 10: spice is the whole economy."
28. **Favourite quotes**: "Save this quote from Dune: fear is the mind-killer."
29. **Show notes and quotes**: "Show my notes on Dune." (or "read me a favourite book quote")
30. **Series order**: "What order do the Slow Horses books go in?" A table from Open Library in order of first
    publication; Alfred flags anything that looks wrong.

## With other people

31. **Lend something**: "I lent Dune to Sam." (book, DVD, anything)
32. **It came back**: "Sam gave Dune back."
33. **What's lent out**: "Who has my books?" A table with how long each has been out (over 30 days is marked overdue).
34. **Book club**: "Our book club is reading Rebecca, meeting Friday 9 October at Jo's." The next book, date and place pop up.
35. **Discussion questions**: "Write discussion questions for the book club." Alfred writes them and they are saved
    on the book club card.
36. **Film night**: "Plan a film night on Saturday at 8 with Sam and Priya, popcorn and pizza." / "When's film night?"
    A card with the film, time, who is coming and snacks; tap a snack to add it to your shopping list.
