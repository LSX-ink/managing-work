# Kids

Everyday help for children: a sticker chart with rewards, pocket money jars, reading, growth and health logs,
milestones, bedtime routines, a turn taker, a big visual timer, packed lunches, bedtime stories, activity ideas,
jokes and a drawing pad. Nothing goes online; everything is kept in `kids-*.json` files in your memory folder,
per child. Say a child's first name; with only one child saved you can leave the name out. The health log is a
diary only — Alfred never suggests doses or diagnoses, and points to NHS 111 or the GP for worries.

## Sticker chart and rewards

- **Chart goals**: "Mia's sticker chart goals are brushing teeth and tidying up." A goal picked up the first time
  a star is given for it is added automatically too.
- **Give a star**: "Give Mia a star for brushing teeth." / "Take a star off Mia." The week's sticker chart pops up,
  with a column per day; tap a square to add a star for that goal and day.
- **Sticker chart**: "Show Mia's sticker chart." Pops up the week's grid and her balance of stars to spend.
- **Set a reward**: "Mia can have a trip to the park at 10 stars." A progress bar pops up.
- **Star rewards**: "Show Mia's star rewards." Progress bars towards each reward still open.
- **Claim a reward**: "Mia is claiming the trip to the park." Spends the stars needed; claimed rewards drop off.

## Pocket money jars

- **Set weekly pocket money**: "Mia gets 5 pounds a week." Paid into spend, save and give jars every Monday by
  itself (50/40/10 split unless you say otherwise, e.g. "60, 30, 10 split").
- **Money in**: "Add 20 pounds to Mia's jars for her birthday." Split across the jars like a normal payday, or say
  a jar to put it straight in one, e.g. "Put £5 in Mia's save jar."
- **Money out**: "Take 5 pounds out of Mia's spend jar for sweets."
- **Jars**: "Show Mia's pocket money." / "Show the pocket money jars." Each child's jars pop up as filling glasses,
  with the last few changes underneath.

## Reading, growth and health logs

- **Reading log**: "Mia read The Gruffalo for 15 minutes and finished it." A certificate pops up automatically every
  so many books finished (10 by default; say "certificates every 5 books for Mia" to change it).
- **Reading log pop-up**: "Show Mia's reading log." Minutes this week, books finished and a table of recent reading.
- **Reading certificate**: "Show Mia's reading certificate." Pops up early if one's due, otherwise says how many
  books are left.
- **Growth chart**: "Log Mia at 112 centimetres and 19.5 kilos." / "Show Mia's height chart." A line chart of height
  or weight over time pops up, with a button to switch between them. Convert feet/inches or stone to metric first.
- **Milestones and firsts**: "Mia lost her first tooth today." / "Show Mia's milestones." A table of firsts pops up,
  for one child or the whole family.
- **Temperature and medicine log**: "Mia's temperature is 39.2, gave Calpol 5ml." A log only — Alfred flags a high
  temperature (38°C or over) and always adds "call NHS 111 or your GP; in an emergency, call 999" for worries.
  "Show Mia's temperature and medicine log" pops up the last three days.
- **Undo a mistake**: "Undo Mia's last reading entry." (or growth, milestone, health) Alfred asks you to confirm
  before removing it.

## Bedtime, turns and timers

- **Bedtime routine**: "Show Mia's bedtime." A checklist pops up (bath, pyjamas, teeth, toilet, story, cuddle and
  lights out by default); tick steps as they're done, or say "Mia's done her teeth for bedtime."
- **Change the steps**: "Mia's bedtime routine is bath, pyjamas, story."
- **Whose turn**: "Whose turn is it on the Xbox, Mia and Jack?" Remembers who went first last time and gives the
  other child a go first next time.
- **Visual timer**: "Start a 5 minute green timer." / "Start a 5 minute turns timer for Mia and Jack." A big
  shrinking coloured circle pops up with a countdown and a chime at the end; with more than one person it moves on
  to the next child's turn each time it runs out.

## Packed lunches

- **Plan a lunch**: "Put a cheese sandwich in Mia's packed lunch for Monday." Lunches are for school days
  (Monday to Friday) only; leave the day out for the next empty slot.
- **This week's lunches**: "Show this week's packed lunches." A table pops up, one column per child (or "Everyone").
- **Fill the gaps**: "Fill the empty packed lunch days with ideas." Picks fresh ideas from the built-in list plus
  any you've added.
- **Lunch ideas**: "Give me some packed lunch ideas." / "Add pasta salad to the lunch ideas." Tap one to put it in
  the next empty day.

## Family week

- **The week at a glance**: "Show the family's week." One table of every child's stars, reading, bedtimes done,
  pocket money and milestones this week.

## Fun

- **Bedtime story**: "Make up a bedtime story for Mia about a dragon who's brave." Alfred picks a hero, place and
  theme (or uses what you gave) and tells it there and then from the plan that pops up.
- **Stories told**: "Which bedtime stories have we had?" A table of past story titles pops up.
- **Activity idea**: "Give me an indoor activity for a 6 year old that costs nothing." One idea pops up with a
  button for another, or say "show all" for the full list that fits.
- **Jokes and tongue twisters**: "Tell the kids a joke." / "Give us a tongue twister." Each one doesn't repeat until
  every one in the list has been heard.
- **Drawing pad**: "Open the drawing pad." A big-button colouring pad pops up with paint colours, a pen and stamps
  (star, heart, circle, square, flower, smiley), undo and clear. **Save** saves the picture as a PNG into a memory
  folder (Ideas by default).
