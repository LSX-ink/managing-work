# Art studio

Alfred can help you make pictures, play with colours and add effects to your photos. Everything is done on your
PC with Pillow; nothing is uploaded anywhere. New pictures are saved in a memory folder (**Ideas** unless you say
another one) and pop up on the Alfred screen. Edits to a photo are always saved as a **new copy** next to the
original, so the original is never changed.

Say the folder by name, e.g. "Personal" or "Personal/Holiday". iPhone HEIC photos can't be opened: on the iPhone,
Settings > Camera > Formats > Most Compatible saves new photos as JPEG.

## Colours

1. **Colour palette**: "Make a triadic palette from teal." (or complementary, analogous, tetradic,
   split complementary, monochrome; give a colour name or a hex code like #3fa9ff)
   Swatches pop up with their hex codes and names. Click a swatch to copy its hex code, or **Copy all**; the
   buttons underneath make the other kinds of palette.
2. **Palette from a picture**: "What colours are in sunset.jpg in Personal?"
   The six main colours of the picture pop up as swatches, with how much of the picture each one covers.
3. **Colour name**: "What colour is #3fa9ff?" / "What's the hex code for cornflower blue?"
   Alfred says the nearest of the 140 or so named web colours, and a swatch of each pops up.

## Photo effects (saved as a new copy)

4. **Filters**: "Make beach.jpg in Personal/Holiday look vintage."
   Filters: sepia, vintage, blur, sharpen, invert, posterize, pixelate, vignette, and noir (high-contrast black
   and white). The new picture pops up, e.g. "beach vintage.jpg".
5. **Brightness and contrast**: "Brighten beach.jpg a bit." / "Give it more contrast."
   Say how much if you like ("by 50 percent").
6. **Border or frame**: "Put a white polaroid frame round beach.jpg."
   Styles: plain, polaroid (thicker at the bottom) or double; any colour and thickness.
7. **Rounded corners**: "Round the corners of my profile picture."
   Saved as a PNG so the corners are see-through.
8. **Watermark**: "Put '© 2026 My Photos' in the corner of beach.jpg."
   White text in the corner (or the middle), half see-through unless you say otherwise.
9. **Crop to a shape**: "Crop beach.jpg to a square." / "Crop it to 16:9." / "Make a 9:16 phone wallpaper
   from it." The crop is taken from the middle.
10. **Meme maker**: "Make a meme of cat.jpg with 'Monday morning' on top and 'again' on the bottom."
    Big white letters with a black outline, sized to fit.

## Make something new

11. **Pixel art editor**: "Open the pixel art editor." (or "a 32 by 32 pixel art grid")
    A grid pops up with a colour palette, pen, fill bucket, eraser, undo and clear. Name it and press **Save**:
    a scaled-up PNG goes into Ideas (or the folder you asked for) and pops up. Tick **See-through background**
    off for a white background.
12. **Collage**: "Make a collage from Personal/Holiday." (2 to 9 pictures; "with a black background", "with no
    gaps", or name the pictures)
    The pictures are laid out in a neat grid and the collage pops up.
13. **Wallpaper**: "Make me a 1920 by 1080 starfield wallpaper." / "Make a phone wallpaper, 1170 by 2532, as a
    gradient from navy to black."
    Starfields are black with white stars, like Alfred's hud-stars look (say "with coloured stars" for a few
    tinted ones).
14. **ASCII art**: "Turn beach.jpg into ASCII art."
    The picture drawn in text characters pops up, with a Copy button and text size buttons. "Save it as a note"
    keeps a copy in Ideas.
15. **Mood board**: "Make a mood board from Ideas/Kitchen."
    The folder's pictures (up to 12) pop up in a grid with a row of their main colours underneath. Click a
    picture to see it big, or a colour to copy it.
16. **QR code**: "Make a QR code for https://example.com."
    Made on your PC (no website involved), saved as a PNG and popped up, ready to print or scan. Up to about
    200 characters.
17. **Drawing prompts**: "Give me something to draw." (or "three drawing ideas")
    A prompt from a list of 60 pops up, with buttons for another one, the pixel art editor and the sketch pad.
