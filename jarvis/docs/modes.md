# Conversation modes and practice sessions

Alfred can change how he talks for a while: a patient tutor, a tough interviewer, a quiz master, a calm
listener, or short answers only. Pick a mode and he stays in it until you say **"normal mode"** (or "stop", or
pick another mode). While a mode is on, a small badge pops up on the Alfred screen showing which one, how long
it's been on, and a **Back to normal** button.

Everything is saved as small files in your memory folder, named `modes-*.json`. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Switching modes

- **Switch mode**: "Be my coach." / "Tutor mode." / "Explain like I'm five." / "Concise mode." / "Be chatty." /
  "Play devil's advocate about my new business idea." / "Tell me a story about a dragon." / "Just listen, I need
  to vent." / "Rubber duck mode." Pops up the mode badge.
  The built-in modes are: tutor, coach, debate partner, interviewer, language partner, storyteller, quiz master,
  brainstorm, rubber duck (for code), calm listener, devil's advocate, explain simply (ELI5), concise and chatty.
- **Back to normal**: "Normal mode." / "Stop the mode." Closes the badge.
- **Which mode**: "What mode are you in?" Alfred says, and the badge pops up again.
- **Mode history**: "Which modes did I use this week?" A bar chart of minutes in each mode since Monday.
- **Your own modes**: "Make a mode called Pirate where you talk like a pirate." Then "Pirate mode." to use it.
  "List my modes." pops up a list (click one to switch to it). "Delete my Pirate mode." asks you to confirm first.
- **Rubber duck checklist**: "Show the rubber duck checklist." A tick-box list of debugging steps, with a button
  to start talking it through.

## Daily check-in

- **Check in**: "Start my daily check-in." Alfred asks three quick questions (energy, mood, focus, each 1 to 5),
  saves them and goes back to normal. Your mood also goes into your wellbeing mood log, so "How has my mood been
  this week?" still works.
- **Check-in chart**: "Show my check-ins." A chart of energy, mood and focus over the last two weeks.

## Job interview practice

- **Practise an interview**: "Practise a job interview for a senior nurse role." / "Give me situational interview
  questions." Alfred asks one question at a time from a bank of 60 (behavioural, strengths, situational and
  general technical), listens to your answer and gives feedback. Each question pops up, with **STAR tips**,
  **Next question** and **End practice** buttons.
- **STAR method**: "Remind me of the STAR method." Pops up Situation, Task, Action, Result.
- **Interview log**: "Show my interview practice." A table of each question, your answer, Alfred's feedback and
  score from your last practice.

## Presentation rehearsal

- **Time a talk**: "Time my talk." / "Time my best man speech." A running clock pops up with a Stop button.
- **Stop and pace**: "Stop the talk timer." Alfred says how long it took. Paste or say the talk's text as well
  ("stop the timer, here's the text...") and he works out words per minute. A table of your past rehearsals pops up.
- **Filler words**: "Count the filler words in this transcript: ..." Counts um, uh, er, erm, like, you know,
  basically, actually, sort of and kind of, with a bar chart. (Every "like" is counted, even the proper ones.)

## Role-play

- **Scenarios**: "What role-plays can we do?" A list: ordering at a restaurant, returning an item, asking for a
  raise, calling the doctor's surgery, small talk at a party, complaining to a landlord, checking in at a hotel,
  saying no to extra work. Click one to start.
- **Start one**: "Let's role-play asking for a raise, make it hard." Alfred plays the other person; your goals
  pop up. When you finish he steps out of character and tells you how you did.

## Language conversation partner

- **Practise a language**: "Practise Spanish with me at B1 about travel." Levels are A1 (beginner) to C1
  (advanced). Alfred speaks mostly in that language at your level and corrects you gently. The level and topic
  pop up.
- **Topics**: "Show me A2 conversation topics." A list for that level; click one to switch.
- **New words**: Alfred saves new words as you go, or say "Save the word el billete, it means ticket." Then
  "Show my Spanish words." pops up the list.

## Socratic tutor

- **Tutor me**: "Tutor me in fractions at year 6 level." Alfred asks guiding questions instead of giving answers,
  and keeps track of the questions asked and the concepts you've got.
- **Progress**: "How am I doing in fractions?" A list of concepts understood (ticked) and recent questions.
  Next time you pick up where you left off.

## Quiz master

- **Quiz me**: "Be my quiz master on the Romans, 10 questions." Alfred writes the questions; the score is kept
  for him. A scoreboard pops up and updates after every answer.
- **Scoreboard**: "What's the score?" / "End the quiz." The final score and your recent quizzes.

## Debate

- **Motions**: "Show me debate motions." A list of 40; click one.
- **Debate**: "Let's debate motion 3, I'll argue against." / "Debate me on whether zoos should be abolished."
  Alfred takes the other side, round by round.
- **Summary**: "Sum up the debate." Pops up both sides' arguments, round by round, with Next round and Finish buttons.

## Brainstorm board

- **Brainstorm**: "Let's brainstorm birthday party ideas." Brainstorm mode starts and good ideas are saved to a
  board that pops up and fills as you go. You can also say "Add bake-off to the board."
- **Star and remove**: click ☆ to star an idea or × to remove it, or say "Star idea 2."
- **Export**: "Export my brainstorm ideas to a note." Saves a Markdown note in Ideas (starred ideas first) and
  pops it up.
