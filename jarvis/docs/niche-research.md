# Niche research

Choose and test a niche or business idea before you spend money. You type your own ratings, notes and prices; Alfred keeps them
in `nicheresearch.json` in the memory folder and turns them into pop-ups and files on the Alfred screen.

Please be realistic. Scores, SWOTs and "gaps" are a thinking aid built from what you told Alfred. They are not market data, and
nothing here can promise that an idea will sell or earn. Test with real people first, and only risk money you can afford to lose.
Nothing is posted, sent, built or scraped, and no website is visited. Any UK tax point is general guidance: check GOV.UK.

Where you name a niche, you can leave it out when you only have one.

## Score and compare niches (nicheresearch_score)

| Say | What pops up |
|---|---|
| "Add a niche called sourdough baking" | Saves it |
| "Show my niches" | Tap a niche to see its radar |
| "Rate sourdough: demand 4, competition 3, passion 5, skill 3, profit 4, evergreen 4" | Saves ratings and speaks the score out of 100 |
| "Make profit count 5 in my niche scoring" | Changes a weight (or "reset the weights") |
| "Rank my niches" | Table, best score first, with all six ratings |
| "Show the radar for sourdough and chess" | Radar chart, up to four niches overlaid |
| "Compare sourdough and chess" | Side-by-side table including how much research each has |
| "How do I rate a niche?" | What each 1-5 rating means (5 is always the good end) |
| "What are the weak spots of sourdough?" | Lowest ratings with a thing to try for each |
| "Rename niche 1 to sourdough bread" | Renames or changes the note |
| "Remove the chess niche" | Asks first; deletes everything saved under it |

## Audience and ideas (nicheresearch_audience)

| Say | What pops up |
|---|---|
| "Save a persona: busy Beth, wants fresh bread, frustrated by flat loaves" | Saves the audience persona |
| "Show my persona" | Persona sheet |
| "Give me customer interview questions" | Ten questions to ask real people |
| "Where can I find my audience?" | Six places to read and listen |
| "Add a pain point: loaves come out flat, hurts 5, happens often 4" | Saves to the pain bank |
| "Show pain points" | List, worst first; tap one for solution ideas |
| "Change pain 2 to hurts 4" | Updates it |
| "Remove pain point 2" | Asks first |
| "Solution ideas for my top pain point" | Seven prompts (checklist, template, challenge and so on) |
| "Draft a survey for sourdough" | Survey questions built from your pain points |
| "Brainstorm keywords for sourdough, rye" | List of word combinations. Clearly not search volumes |
| "Find content gaps: they cover starters, ovens; I cover starters" | Topics they cover and you don't, shared, and yours only |
| "Add scoring loaves to my topic list" | Saves your own topics |

## Competitors, prices, SWOT, interest (nicheresearch_market)

| Say | What pops up |
|---|---|
| "Add competitor Bake Club: strengths big list, weaknesses dull videos, prices 19 a month" | Saves your notes |
| "Update Bake Club's topics: starters, ovens" | Edits any field |
| "Show my competitors" | Table |
| "Compare my competitors" | Strengths, weaknesses and openings to aim at |
| "Remove Bake Club" | Asks first |
| "I saw a starter course for 49 pounds on site A" | Logs a price you saw |
| "Show prices I've seen" | Table, cheapest first |
| "Price stats" | Bar chart, lowest, middle, average and highest |
| "Where would 30 pounds sit?" | Budget, middle or premium end of what you logged |
| "Remove the ebook price" | Asks first |
| "Add a strength: I bake daily" | Fills a SWOT box (strength, weakness, opportunity, threat) |
| "Show my SWOT" | Four-box pop-up; tap a box for prompts |
| "Give me SWOT prompts" | Questions for each box |
| "Clear my SWOT" | Asks first |
| "Is interest in sourdough rising?" | Line chart of Wikipedia page views for that word |

The interest check is the only one that uses the internet. It sends only the topic word to the free Wikimedia page views service
(no key, no account). Wikipedia views are a rough interest signal only: not search volume, not demand.

## Plan, test and decide (nicheresearch_plan)

| Say | What pops up |
|---|---|
| "What should I do next for sourdough?" | Progress checklist of eight research steps; tap one to start |
| "Write my positioning statement" | "For (who) who struggle with (problem)..." filled from your answers and saved |
| "Give me angle ideas for busy parents" | Eight angles; tap one to save |
| "Set my lean canvas revenue to workshops" | Saves boxes (also value, customers, solution, channels, costs, metrics, advantage) |
| "Show my lean canvas" | Nine boxes; problem, customers and value fill from your research |
| "Save the lean canvas as a file" | Markdown file in the Niche research folder |
| "Make my one-page business plan" | Markdown plan file with score, audience, competitors, prices, tests, verdict, next steps |
| "Add an experiment: parents will join a waitlist, test a mock page, success 4 of 20" | Saves hypothesis, test and success number |
| "Record the result of experiment 1: 6 of 20, keep going" | Result and decision (waiting, keep, change, stop) |
| "Show my experiments" | Board by decision; tap one to update it |
| "How do I write an experiment?" | Template with an example |
| "Remove experiment 2" | Asks first |
| "Smoke test ideas" | Eight checklist steps (paper landing page, waitlist plan, pre-order plan, ask ten people and so on). You do them; Alfred builds and posts nothing |
| "Tick smoke test step 2" | Ticks or unticks a step |
| "Show the go no-go checklist" | Ten yes/no questions with a suggestion once all are answered |
| "Answer go no-go question 3 no" | Yes, no or unsure |
| "Save my verdict: go, six of twenty said yes" | Saves go, no-go, park or pivot with a reason |
| "Show my verdict" | Verdict pop-up |
| "Add a niche lesson: ask about the past, not the future" | Saves to the niche journal |
| "Show my niche journal" | Lessons, newest first |
| "Remove journal lesson 1" | Asks first |

Files are saved in the **Niche research** folder. Client work, proposals, day rates and time tracking are in the freelance
abilities; money-research files for video accounts are in the TikTok abilities.
