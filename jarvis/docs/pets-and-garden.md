# Pets and garden

Pets are kept in `petsgarden.json` in your memory folder, along with your harvests, seed packets and raised bed plans.
None of it is sent anywhere. The guides (sowing, plant care, toxic foods) are built in and work offline; only the
frost warning and dog breed pictures go online, and they send just a city or a breed word.

Vet, vaccination and flea dates, the pet weight chart, watering reminders for houseplants and the general sitter sheet
are already in [Household plus](household-plus.md) and [Home and life](home-and-life.md), so they aren't repeated here.
Bird and tree ID and foraging are in [Sky and nature](sky-and-nature.md).

## Pets

1. **Pet profile**: "Add Biscuit, a golden retriever, born 1 March 2022, weighing 29 kilos." Saves name, species, breed,
   birthday, weight and notes. Say it again to update.
2. **Show a pet**: "Show Biscuit's profile." / "Show my pets." Pops up: a table of facts, or all your pets.
3. **Age in human years**: "How old is Biscuit in human years?" Works for dogs (by size) and cats.
4. **Remove a pet**: "Remove Biscuit." Asks you to confirm first.
5. **Feeding schedule**: "Biscuit is fed at 8am and 5:30pm, one cup."
6. **Log a feed**: "Mia has fed Biscuit." Notes the time and who.
7. **Did anyone feed the dog?**: "Has Biscuit been fed today?" Pops up: today's meals, who fed, and a note if a
   scheduled meal looks missed.
8. **Log a walk**: "Biscuit had a 45 minute walk, 2 miles."
9. **Walk chart**: "Show Biscuit's walks this week." Pops up: a bar chart of minutes per day.
10. **Training tricks**: "Biscuit is learning roll over." / "Biscuit has mastered sit."
11. **Tricks list**: "What tricks does Biscuit know?" Pops up: a tick list; click one to move it up a level.
12. **Pet sitter sheet**: "Make a pet sitter sheet, back on Sunday." Saves `petsgarden-sitter-sheet.md` in your memory
    folder (meals, walks, tricks, notes) and shows it.
13. **Toxic foods**: "Can my dog eat grapes?" / "Is a lily safe for my cat?" / "Show foods that are toxic to pets."
    Pops up: a table of dog and cat safety with the reason.
14. **Pet first-aid kit**: "Show the pet first-aid kit." Pops up: a tick list of what to keep at home.
15. **Pet first aid steps**: "What do I do if my dog has heatstroke?" Steps for bleeding, choking, heatstroke,
    poisoning, stings, seizures, burns, broken bones and snake bites. It always says to phone the vet.
16. **Dog breed facts**: "Tell me about labradors." Pops up: size, weight, life span and exercise with a picture from
    dog.ceo.

## Garden

17. **What to sow this month**: "What can I sow in March?" / "What herbs can I sow now?" Pops up: a table of about 55
    veg, herbs and flowers for that month (sow indoors, sow outdoors, plant out, harvest).
18. **One crop's calendar**: "When do I plant out tomatoes?" Pops up: its sowing and harvest months.
19. **Jobs this month**: "What should I do in the garden this month?" Pops up: a tick list.
20. **Plant care cards**: "How do I care for a monstera?" Pops up: water, light and feed for about 30 houseplants and
    garden plants. "List the plant care cards" gives a list to tap.
21. **Companion planting**: "What grows well with tomatoes?" / "Show the companion planting table."
22. **Lawn care calendar**: "What should I do with the lawn?" Pops up: a table for every month.
23. **Compost do's and don'ts**: "What can I put in the compost?" Pops up: a list of yes, no and tips.
24. **Frost warning**: "Will there be a frost this week?" Uses your home city, or say "in Leeds". Pops up: a table of
    the coming nights' lows with advice.
25. **Log a harvest**: "I picked 1.5 kilos of courgettes." / "Log 12 apples picked." (say each for counts)
26. **Harvest totals**: "Show my harvest for this year." Pops up: a bar chart of totals by crop.
27. **Add seeds**: "I've got two packets of sweet peas, sow by March 2027."
28. **Seed stock**: "What seeds have I got?" Pops up: a table sorted by sow-by date with a countdown and "expired".
29. **Remove seeds**: "Remove the carrot seeds." Asks you to confirm first.
30. **Plan a raised bed**: "Plan a raised bed, 4 rows by 3 columns." Sets the grid size.
31. **Plant a square**: "Plant carrots in row 2, column 3 of the raised bed."
32. **Raised bed planner**: "Show my raised bed." Pops up: a grid of squares. Type a plant in the box, then click
    squares to plant them; leave the box empty and click to clear one.
33. **Remove a bed plan**: "Remove the raised bed plan." Asks you to confirm first.
