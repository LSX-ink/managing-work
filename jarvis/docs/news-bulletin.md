# News bulletin

Say **"Play today's news"** and Alfred plays the latest radio news bulletin in the presenters' own voices, not his.

- Default station: BBC World Service (the Global News Podcast, the newest edition).
- It waits until Alfred has finished speaking, then plays the opening headlines (the main three stories) and pauses.
  Press **Play** or **Full bulletin** for the rest.
- The top three BBC News headlines are also shown as text in the pop-up, for anyone who can't hear it.
- Other stations: "Play the BBC Minute" (a 60-second bulletin) or "Play the NPR news" (US, hourly).

Alfred finds the bulletin through the keyless iTunes podcast search; only the bulletin's name is sent.
