# Screen games

Games that pop up in a window on the Alfred screen. Play by clicking, or just say your move. Games and scores last
until Alfred restarts. (For voice-only games such as trivia and riddles, see [Games and fun](fun.md).)

## Against Alfred

- **Noughts and crosses**: "Let's play noughts and crosses." / "Play noughts and crosses on easy." Then click a
  square or say "Top left." Pops up a 3 by 3 board; you're X. On hard, Alfred can't be beaten.
- **Connect Four**: "Let's play Connect Four." Click a column or say "Column 4." Pops up a 7 by 6 board.
- **Battleships**: "Let's play battleships." Click a square on Alfred's waters or say "Fire at B7." Pops up two
  8 by 8 grids: Alfred's waters (his ships stay hidden) and your fleet, which he fires back at.

## Word games and quizzes

- **Hangman**: "Let's play hangman." Click letters or say "Is there an E?" / "Is it penguin?" / "Give me a hint."
  Pops up the drawing, the word so far and a row of letter buttons.
- **Wordle**: "Let's play wordle." Type a five-letter word in the box, or say "Is it crane?" Pops up coloured tiles:
  filled in means the right letter in the right place, dashed means it's in the word somewhere else.
- **Capital cities quiz**: "Give me a capitals quiz." Ten questions; click an answer or say it. Pops up the question
  with four answer buttons.
- **Flags quiz**: "Give me a flags quiz." Pops up a flag with four country buttons.

## Cards

- **Higher or lower**: "Let's play higher or lower." Click Higher or Lower, or say it. Pops up the last card and
  this one. Aces are high; see how long a streak you can build.
- **Blackjack**: "Deal me a hand of blackjack." Click Hit or Stand, or say "Hit me." Alfred is the dealer; his second
  card stays face down until you stand.
- **Bingo caller**: "Let's play bingo." Then "Next number." Pops up the last number (with its call, like "Legs
  eleven") and a board of all 90 with the called numbers lit.

## Games that play in the window

These run in the pop-up itself, and tell Alfred your score when you finish. Keys only work while the game's window
is on top, and never while you're typing in a box.

- **Sudoku**: "Give me an easy sudoku." / "a medium sudoku". Type digits into the squares and press Check. Say
  "Give me a sudoku hint" (or press Hint) to fill one in.
- **Memory pairs**: "Let's play memory." Turn over two cards at a time to find matching shapes.
- **Minesweeper**: "Let's play minesweeper." A 9 by 9 board with 10 mines; right-click (or Flag mode) marks a mine.
- **Snake**: "Let's play snake." Arrow keys (or W A S D) to steer, space to pause. It pauses by itself when you
  switch to another window.
- **2048**: "Let's play 2048." Arrow keys slide the tiles; matching tiles join.
- **Maths sprint**: "Start a maths sprint." / "a hard maths sprint". Press Start and answer as many sums as you can
  in 60 seconds.

## Scores

- **Scores table**: "Show me the game scores." Pops up a table of every screen game played this session: played,
  won, lost, drawn and best score.

Everything works offline except the flags quiz, whose pictures come from flagcdn.com. Nothing about you is sent.
