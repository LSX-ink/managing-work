# Side hustles

Find, plan and run a side hustle with honest numbers. Everything is saved in your memory folder on this PC (files called
`sidehustle-...`) and nothing is ever sent, posted or paid anywhere; no internet is needed. Money is in pounds (GBP).

Alfred never promises income. The idea list is general information written into Alfred (typical costs, times and risks,
not live data), and every calculation only uses the numbers you give. UK tax, licence and insurance notes are general:
check GOV.UK for the current rules.

Not repeated here: brand deals, invoices and creator income (creator-business.md), general budgets (money-plus.md), jobs
and CVs (career.md). Digital products, income stream tracking with UK tax reminders and marketplace fee calculators are
separate abilities.

## Finding an idea (sidehustle_ideas)

1. **List ideas**: "Show me side hustle ideas." Pops up: about 40 ideas with start cost, time to first pound, hours, skills and risk.
2. **Filter**: "List cheap side hustles" or "List care side hustles." Groups: selling, services, online, creative, local, care.
3. **Match to me**: "Match side hustles to my skills: driving and cooking, 5 hours a week, £100." Pops up: the best fits and why.
4. **One idea**: "Tell me about dog walking as a side hustle." Pops up: cost, time to first pound, hours, skills, risk and a UK note.
5. **Compare**: "Compare dog walking, tutoring and cleaning." Pops up: a side-by-side table (two to four ideas).
6. **Save my profile**: "Remember my side hustle skills are writing and design, 6 hours a week, £50 budget."
7. **Show my profile**: "What do you have on my side hustle profile?" Pops up: skills, hours, budget and shortlist.
8. **Shortlist add**: "Add tutoring to my side hustle shortlist."
9. **Show shortlist**: "Show my side hustle shortlist." Pops up: a list; click one for details.
10. **Shortlist remove**: "Take tutoring off my shortlist."
11. **Surprise me**: "Surprise me with a side hustle idea." Pops up: one idea.
12. **Quick and cheap**: "Which side hustles are quickest and cheapest to start?" Pops up: the eight quickest.
13. **Groups**: "What kinds of side hustle are there?" Pops up: the groups; click one to list it.

## Planning and running (sidehustle_plan)

14. **Start one**: "Start a dog walking side hustle." Pops up: a 30-day launch plan checklist with due dates.
15. **Show the plan**: "Show my dog walking launch plan." Pops up: the checklist by week; click a step to tick it.
16. **Tick a step**: "Tick off task 3 on my dog walking plan."
17. **Add a step**: "Add a step to my dog walking plan: buy poo bags, day 2."
18. **What's next**: "What are my next side hustle tasks?" Pops up: a list of the next steps across your hustles.
19. **My hustles**: "List my side hustles." Pops up: a table with status, plan progress, hours and profit.
20. **Pause or stop**: "Pause my dog walking side hustle." Logged numbers are kept.
21. **Remove a hustle**: "Remove my dog walking side hustle." Asks you to confirm first.
22. **Set a goal**: "I want to earn an extra £300 a month."
23. **Goal progress**: "How am I doing on my side hustle goal?" Pops up: a meter, this month's profit, days left, and a pace estimate (not a promise).
24. **Monthly review**: "Do my side hustle review for dog walking." Pops up: the month's numbers, a keep going / change / stop prompt, and questions to ask yourself. It is a prompt from your own numbers, never advice.
25. **Save a decision**: "Record my dog walking review: keep going."
26. **Past reviews**: "Show my past side hustle reviews." Pops up: a table.
27. **Add an order**: "New order: Jo wants 12 cupcakes for £30, due 2026-10-10." Pops up: the order table.
28. **Show orders**: "Show my side hustle orders." Pops up: a table with status and what is waiting to be paid.
29. **Update an order**: "Mark Jo's order as paid." Paid orders log the income once.
30. **Customers**: "Show my side hustle customers." Pops up: orders, paid totals and repeat customers.
31. **Customer note**: "Note on Jo: nut allergy in the family."
32. **Remove an order**: "Remove order 2." Asks you to confirm first.

## Honest numbers (sidehustle_money)

33. **Effective hourly rate**: "I made £300, spent £100 and worked 20 hours. What's my hourly rate?" Pops up: the rate and a note if it is below the National Living Wage.
34. **Break-even**: "I've spent £100 to start and make £10 per £15 sale. When do I break even?" Pops up: sales needed.
35. **Startup costs**: "Startup costs: mower £100 and gloves £10, budget £200." Pops up: the total with a 10% spare.
36. **Cost-plus price**: "What should I charge? Materials £5, one hour at £10, 10% platform fee." Pops up: the lowest sensible price.
37. **Target-hourly price**: "I want £15 an hour and a job takes 2 hours plus 1 hour travel. What do I charge?" Pops up: the price and what you'd need if it runs 25% long.
38. **Log hours**: "I did 3 hours of dog walking." Says the week's total.
39. **Log income**: "I earned £60 from Sam for dog walking."
40. **Log a cost**: "I spent £10 on dog leads."
41. **Hours this week**: "How many side hustle hours did I do this week?" Pops up: bars per hustle for four weeks and your weekly aim.
42. **Real hourly rate**: "What's my real hourly rate for dog walking?" Pops up: income, costs, profit, hours and rate.
43. **Compare my hustles**: "Which of my side hustles pays best per hour?" Pops up: a ranked table.
44. **Sales needed**: "How many £20 sales with £5 costs to reach £150?" Pops up: the number.
45. **Log entries**: "Show my side hustle log." Pops up: the latest entries.

## Scam and red-flag help (sidehustle_safety)

46. **Check an offer**: "Check this offer: guaranteed £500 a day, pay a small fee for your starter kit..." Pops up: a risk score and each warning sign in plain words. Read on this PC only; a quick check, not proof either way.
47. **Red-flag guide**: "What are the red flags in get-rich offers?" Pops up: the list.
48. **Common scams**: "What are the common get-rich scams?" Pops up: a list; click one to hear more.
49. **Explain a scam**: "Explain MLM schemes." Also upfront-fee schemes, crypto giveaways, fake cheques, task scams, trading bots and get-rich courses.
50. **Before you pay**: "Give me the before-you-pay checklist." Pops up: a checklist.
51. **If I've been scammed**: "What do I do if I've been scammed?" Pops up: the steps (bank, 159, reporting, 7726).
52. **MLM maths**: "They want £200 to join and £80 a month, and say I'll earn £60 a month. What does that come to?" Pops up: your position after a year.
53. **Check a company**: "How do I check a company out?" Pops up: a checklist (Companies House, FCA register).
54. **Genuine signs**: "What do genuine opportunities look like?" Pops up: a list.
