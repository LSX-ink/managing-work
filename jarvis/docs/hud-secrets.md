# HUD secrets

Hidden and handy things on the Alfred screen itself. Some you do with the keyboard or mouse, and most you can
also ask Alfred for. Letter shortcuts only work when you are not typing in a box. Press **?** to see them all.

## Head only

The screen starts with nothing on it but Alfred's floating head (the dotted wolf; the orb on the classic look) on a
plain dark background. Everything still works underneath: he listens, answers out loud, reads out notifications, and
any pop-up you ask for (a folder, now playing, a password check, an approval tick or cross) still appears.

- **H** brings back every panel (the top bar, system, clock, weather, notifications, chat); **H** again, or **Esc**, hides them. The choice is remembered in this browser.
- While only the head shows, just start typing (or press **/**) and a typing box opens at the bottom; it hides again once your message is sent. **Esc** closes it.
- Click the head to start or pause listening, as before.

## Things to say

- **Tidy the windows**: "Tidy my windows." All the pop-up windows line up in a neat grid.
- **Close the windows**: "Close all the windows." Every pop-up closes except the ones you pinned.
- **Shrink the windows**: "Minimise all the windows." Each pop-up shrinks to its title bar; click the – to open one again.
- **Focus mode**: "Turn on focus mode." / "Focus mode off." Everything dims except the chat and the pop-ups. Key: **F**.
- **Screensaver**: "Start the screensaver." A big clock and a slowly drifting wolf fill the screen; press any key or move the mouse to come back. "Turn the screensaver off" stops it coming on by itself, and "Start the screensaver after 5 minutes" sets how long the screen must be idle (it's 10 minutes to begin with). Key: **S**.
- **Change the colours**: "Switch to the gold theme." / "Swap the theme." / "Go back to the stars theme." The HUD changes colour straight away and remembers it. Choices: stars (black and white), gold, blue, purple, red, green.
- **Bigger or smaller**: "Make the HUD bigger." / "Make the HUD smaller." / "Reset the HUD size." The side panels and chat grow or shrink. Keys: **Ctrl + =**, **Ctrl + -**, **Ctrl + 0**.
- **Clean screen**: "Hide the side panels." / "Show the side panels." (Key **H** now shows or hides everything but the head; see above.)
- **Keyboard shortcuts**: "Show the HUD keyboard shortcuts." A list of every shortcut pops up. Key: **?**.
- **Command palette**: "Open the command palette." A search box with about 40 example requests. Key: **Ctrl + K**, or double-click the wolf.
- **What can you do?**: "Show me what you can do." A pop-up list of 20 things to try; click one to ask it.
- **Add a sticky note**: "Add a sticky note: buy milk." / "Stick a pink note saying ring Mum." A note appears on the HUD.
- **List sticky notes**: "What sticky notes have I got?" Alfred reads the ones he added, and a pop-up lists every note on the screen.
- **Remove a sticky note**: "Remove the note about milk." Alfred asks you to confirm first.
- **Clear sticky notes**: "Clear all my sticky notes." Alfred asks you to confirm first.

## Things to do with the mouse and keyboard

- **Command palette** (**Ctrl + K** or double-click the wolf): type to search the examples, use the arrow keys to pick one, and press Enter to send it to Alfred. Or type anything and press Enter.
- **Sticky notes** (**N**, or the NOTE + button at the top): type straight into the note, drag it by its top strip, click ● to change its colour and × to delete it. Notes stay put when you reload the page.
- **Pin a window**: click ⊙ on a pop-up's title bar. A pinned window stays on top and isn't closed by "close all the windows".
- **Maximise a window**: double-click its title bar; double-click again to put it back.
- **Jump to a window**: **Alt + 1** to **Alt + 9** bring that pop-up to the front. **T** tidies them all.
- **Drop files onto the HUD**: drag files from File Explorer anywhere onto the screen, pick a memory folder, and each file is saved there and pops up.
- **Paste**: press **Ctrl + V** when you're not typing in a box. Text pops up with **Save to Ideas** and **Summarise** buttons; a picture pops up with **Save to Ideas** (saves it into your Ideas folder) and **Summarise** (Alfred looks at it and tells you what's in it).
- **Find the mouse**: tap **Ctrl** twice quickly and a ring shows where the pointer is.
- **Space facts**: click an empty patch of sky for a little space fact.
- **A secret**: try the old arcade code ↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A.

Notes you make on the screen are kept in this browser. Notes Alfred adds are also saved in your memory folder as
`sticky-notes.json`. Everything here works offline, and nothing is sent to the internet. If your computer is set to
reduce motion, the animations stay still.
