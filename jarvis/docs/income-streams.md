# Income streams

One overview of ALL your extra income: TikTok creator fund, brand deals, affiliate, digital products, reselling,
freelance, a room to rent, interest, anything. You register each **stream**, tell Alfred what came in each month, and
he shows a dashboard, which streams are best, how much rests on one of them, and the UK side-income basics.

Everything is saved in `incomestreams.json` in your memory folder (money in GBP). Nothing is sent, filed or posted. Alfred
only looks back at numbers you logged and never promises income. The UK notes are general information, not tax advice:
check GOV.UK for the current rules. A stream can also be **linked** to read (never change) creator income from
`creatorbiz-ledger.json` or a `sales` list in `digitalproducts.json`; don't also type that same money by hand.

Not repeated here: creator invoices, deals and expenses (creator-business.md), digital product making and listing
(digital-products.md), general budgets (money-plus.md). Marketplace fee calculators and hustle planning belong to other sets.

## Streams and money in (income_streams)

1. **Add a stream**: "Add an income stream called Etsy prints, mostly passive." Types: active, passive (passive-ish) or mixed;
   tax kinds: trading, property, interest, other.
2. **List streams**: "Show my income streams." Pops up: a table with this month and all-time totals.
3. **One stream**: "Show my Etsy prints stream." Pops up: totals, tax year, costs, hours and money per hour.
4. **Edit a stream**: "Rename Etsy prints to Etsy shop" or "make freelance active".
5. **Pause or end a stream**: "Mark reselling as paused." History is kept.
6. **Remove a stream**: "Remove the reselling stream." Asks you to confirm first; removes what was logged for it too.
7. **Stream ideas**: "What kinds of extra income are there?" Pops up: kinds with active/passive-ish and honest notes.
8. **Log money in**: "I earned 250 from freelance this month, 20 costs." Say a month ("in August") or a date.
9. **List money in**: "Show what came in from affiliate." Pops up: a table of entries.
10. **Edit an entry**: "Change entry 4 to 275 pounds."
11. **Remove an entry**: "Delete entry 4." Asks you to confirm first.
12. **Log hours**: "I spent 6 hours on reselling this week."
13. **Hours per stream**: "Show my hours per stream." Pops up: hours, money and per-hour figures.
14. **What can be linked**: "Can you read my creator income into a stream?" Pops up: totals found in the creator and product files.

## Dashboard and analysis (income_insights)

15. **Income dashboard**: "Show my income dashboard." Pops up: stacked bars by stream, month by month, with a legend
    (passive-ish streams are hatched) and a summary. "Last 12 months" works.
16. **Income by month**: "Show income by stream for each month." Pops up: a table.
17. **Monthly chart**: "Chart my extra income this year." Pops up: a line chart of the monthly total.
18. **Passive vs active share**: "How much of my income is passive?" Pops up: bars (part-active streams also counted as half).
19. **Share by stream**: "Which stream is biggest?" Pops up: bars; click one to open it.
20. **Best and worst**: "Which income stream is best and worst?" Pops up: best, lowest earner and streams with nothing logged.
21. **Growth trends**: "Which streams are growing?" Pops up: the last 3 full months against the 3 before.
22. **Money per hour**: "Which stream pays best per hour?" Pops up: ranked hourly figures (needs logged hours).
23. **Diversification score**: "How diversified is my income?" Pops up: a 0 to 100 score with each stream's share.
24. **Too reliant warning**: "Am I too reliant on one stream?" Pops up: a warning if one stream is 60% or more (you can pick the line).
25. **Compare months**: "Compare this month with last month." Pops up: a table with differences.
26. **Run rate**: "What's my average month lately?" A look back at the last 3 full months, not a forecast.
27. **Calendar year**: "Summarise 2026 income." Pops up: in, costs and left per stream.
28. **After costs**: "What did I keep after costs?" Pops up: money in, costs and left per stream.
29. **Gaps in my log**: "Which months have I not logged?" Pops up: a list; click a stream to log it.

## Goals (income_goals)

30. **Set a goal**: "My goal is 500 pounds a month extra." Also yearly, tax-year, or for one stream. Pops up: progress bars.
31. **Goal progress**: "How am I doing on my income goal?" Pops up: a bar per goal.
32. **Goal pace**: "How much more do I need this month?" Pops up: amount left, days left and per day. A target, not a promise.
33. **Goal history**: "How many months did I hit my goal?" Pops up: a bar per month for a monthly goal.
34. **Remove a goal**: "Delete my income goal." Asks you to confirm first.
35. **Log nudge**: "Which streams haven't I logged this month?" Pops up: a list; click one to log it.

## UK side-income helpers (income_tax_uk)

All of these end with a "check GOV.UK" note. Alfred is not an accountant.

36. **Trading allowance tracker**: "How much of my 1,000 pound allowance have I used?" Pops up: bars for trading and property income.
37. **Allowance explained**: "Explain the trading allowance." Pops up: plain-words points.
38. **Allowance or real costs**: "Should I use the allowance or claim costs? I made 3,000 with 500 costs." Pops up: both taxable figures.
39. **Deadlines**: "What are my tax deadlines?" Pops up: 5 October register, 31 October paper return, 31 January file and pay, 31 July second payment on account, with days to go.
40. **Registering**: "How do I register for Self Assessment?" Pops up: the 5 October deadline in plain words.
41. **Tax year dates**: "When does the tax year start and end?" Pops up: 6 April to 5 April.
42. **Set-aside percentage**: "Set my tax pot to 25 percent." You pick it; it is a rough guide.
43. **Tax pot**: "How much should I put aside for tax?" Pops up: a rough pot and what you've put aside.
44. **Log a pot payment**: "I moved 100 pounds into my tax pot."
45. **Tax year summary**: "Summarise this tax year." Pops up: per stream gross, costs and left, plus the allowance.
46. **CSV for an accountant**: "Export my income for my accountant." Saved in the `Income streams` folder; pops up the file.
47. **Record-keeping checklist**: "Show my record-keeping checklist." Pops up: 10 ticks; clicking one ticks it.
48. **Tick records**: "Tick items 1 and 3 on the records checklist."
49. **Add a receipt**: "Log a receipt: ring light, 25 pounds, for TikTok, kept in the drawer."
50. **Receipts log**: "Show my receipts." Pops up: a table, filter by stream or tax year.
51. **Remove a receipt**: "Delete receipt 3." Asks you to confirm first.
52. **What counts as income**: "What counts as side income?" Pops up: plain points.
53. **Costs you can deduct**: "What costs can I claim?" Pops up: plain points.
54. **Payments on account**: "What are payments on account?" Pops up: plain points.
55. **Where to check on GOV.UK**: "Where do I check the tax rules?" Pops up: what to search for on GOV.UK.
