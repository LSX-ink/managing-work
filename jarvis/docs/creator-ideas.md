# Creator ideas

Writing and planning help for short-video accounts (TikTok, Reels, Shorts). Everything is planning and notes: nothing is
posted anywhere, nothing is looked up online, and your data stays in `creatorideas.json` in the memory folder. Alfred
writes the ideas, hooks and captions himself; the tools store, score and organise them.

Platform rules change, so the risky-words and safe-zone checks are general guidance: check the platform's current
community guidelines and monetisation rules. Alfred never promises income.

## Ideas and pillars (creator_ideas)

1. Add pillars: "Mindglitch's pillars are psychology and dark history."
2. List pillars: "What are my content pillars?" (table)
3. Remove a pillar: "Drop dark history from mindglitch."
4. Add an idea: "Save an idea: why we sigh, tag psychology."
5. Save picked ideas: "Save the first three."
6. List the idea bank: "Show my ready ideas." (table)
7. Search ideas: "Find ideas about ghosts."
8. Change status: "Idea 4 is scripted." (idea, scripted, ready, used)
9. Remove an idea: "Delete idea 4." (asks first)
10. Mark a winner: "Idea 2 did well, mark it a winner."
11. Generate ideas: "Give me ten video ideas for lowkey.lore." Alfred uses your pillars and past winners.
12. Idea board: "Show my content board." (three columns; tap an idea to move it along)

## Hooks and checks (creator_hooks)

13. Hook templates: "Show me question hooks." (about 60 templates, six types)
14. Fill hooks: "Write hooks about sleep." (list; tap one to score it)
15. Hook tester: "Score this hook: Nobody tells you this about sleep." (table out of 100)
16. Save a hook: "Save that hook."
17. Saved hooks: "Show my saved hooks."
18. Call to action ideas: "Give me follow calls to action." (table)
19. Add your own call to action: "Add a save line: Save it for later."
20. Text and safe zones: "Check this on-screen text fits." (phone frame with the shaded areas TikTok covers)
21. Risky words list: "What words should I watch for?"
22. Risky words check: "Check this script for risky words." (table)

## Captions, hashtags and scripts (creator_scripts)

23. Caption help: "Write a caption about why we yawn." Uses your saved hashtags.
24. Save hashtags: "Save psychology tags: fyp broad, mindtricks niche, mindglitch branded."
25. Show hashtag sets: "Show my hashtag sets." (table)
26. Hashtag mix check: "Check these hashtags: #fyp #mindtricks." (broad, niche, branded)
27. Remove a hashtag set: "Delete my psychology hashtags." (asks first)
28. Script outline: "Give me a listicle outline for 45 seconds." (beats, seconds and words)
29. Outline choices: "What script outlines do you have?" (list)
30. Pacing: "How many words fit 60 seconds?" or "How long is this script read aloud?"
31. Script check: "Check this script's length." (length, longest sentence)

## Series, trends and replies (creator_plan)

32. New series: "Plan a Birth Months series." (also letters, zodiac signs, weekdays or your own episodes)
33. Show a series: "Show my Birth Months series." (tick list)
34. List series: "What series am I running?"
35. Tick off an episode: "I finished episode 3 of Birth Months."
36. Remove a series: "Delete the Zodiac series." (asks first)
37. Trend note: "Note the slow zoom format." Notes you type in expire after 14 days.
38. Trend notes: "What trend notes do I have?"
39. Remove a trend note: "Remove trend note 2."
40. Trend angles: "Brainstorm angles from the words sleep and dreams." Nothing is fetched, so nothing is called trending.
41. Swipe file add: "Save this example: ghost ship story, strong hook."
42. Swipe file list: "Show my swipe file." (table)
43. Swipe file remove: "Remove swipe file example 2." (asks first)
44. Comment replies: "Help me reply to: is this real?" Three reply options and a reply-video idea; you copy it yourself.

## Skipped

Live trending sounds and hashtag popularity are skipped: they need platform data, and the rules here allow no
logins or platform APIs. Posting, scheduling, the calendar and stats belong to other themes.
