# Home and life trackers

Things to say to Alfred. Everything is saved in your memory folder on this PC, nowhere else.

## Kitchen

- **Meal plan**: "Put lasagne down for Wednesday dinner." / "What are we eating this week?" / "Clear the meal plan."
- **Recipes**: "Save a recipe for pancakes: flour, eggs and milk; whisk, then fry." / "What recipes have I got?" /
  "Read me the pancake recipe." They're Markdown files in the `Recipes` folder.
- **Recipe to shopping list**: "Add the pancake ingredients to my shopping list."
- **Pantry and fridge**: "Add milk to the fridge, use by Friday." / "I've used up the yoghurt." /
  "What's in the pantry?" / "What's going off soon?"

## Household

- **Bin days**: "The blue bin goes out every other Thursday, next one is the 8th of October." / "Which bin is it this week?"
- **Bills**: "Rent is 900 pounds on the 1st of each month." / "What bills are due this week?" /
  "How much are my bills a month?"
- **Subscriptions**: "Add Netflix at 10.99 a month." / "Remove Netflix." / "How much do my subscriptions cost?"
- **Car and home dates**: "My MOT is due on the 10th of November." / "What's coming up in the next couple of months?"
- **Packing lists**: "Make a packing list for Spain: passport, charger, sun cream." / "I've packed the passport." /
  "What's left to pack for Spain?"
- **Gift ideas**: "Gift idea for Mum: a scarf." / "What gift ideas have I got for Mum?"
- **Plants**: "The fern needs watering every 3 days." / "I've watered the fern." / "Which plants need water today?"

## Wellbeing

This is a log only. Alfred doesn't give medical advice.

- **Medication**: "I've just taken my ibuprofen." / "Did I take my ibuprofen today?" / "What have I taken today?"
- **Water**: "I've had two glasses of water." / "How much water have I had today?" (the goal is 8 unless you change it)
- **Sleep**: "I slept seven and a half hours." / "How have I been sleeping this week?"
- **Mood**: "Log my mood as 4, nice walk today." / "How has my mood been this week?"

## Diary

- **Write**: "Add to my diary: had a lovely day at the beach." One Markdown file per day in the `Diary` folder.
- **Read**: "Read me yesterday's diary."
