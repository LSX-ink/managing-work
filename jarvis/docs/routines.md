# Routines, shortcuts and briefings

Things to say to Alfred. Everything is saved in your memory folder on this PC, nowhere else. Most of these pop a
window up on the Alfred screen; Alfred just says a short line about it.

## Routines and shortcuts

A routine is a name for a few requests you'd normally say one by one.

- **Make a routine**: "When I say movie night: turn on dark mode, close all windows, set volume to 30."
- **Run a routine**: "Movie night." / "Run my movie night routine." Alfred does each step in order, and a checklist
  of the steps pops up.
- **List routines**: "What routines have I got?" A list pops up; click one to run it.
- **Change a routine**: "Change movie night to: dim the lights, open Netflix."
- **Delete a routine**: "Delete the movie night routine." (Alfred checks with you first.)
- **Run a routine at a time**: "Run my morning routine every weekday at 7:30." Alfred reminds you at that time;
  say "go ahead" and he runs it. Cancel it like any reminder.
- **Make a shortcut**: "When I say 'lights', I mean 'open the smart home app'."
- **Use a shortcut**: "Lights."
- **List or remove shortcuts**: "What shortcuts have I got?" (pops up; click one to use it) / "Remove the lights shortcut."

## Briefings

- **Morning briefing**: "Give me my morning briefing." A card pops up with the date, today's weather, calendar,
  how many to-dos are open, reminders later today and habits not done yet.
- **Evening wrap-up**: "Wrap up my day." Shows to-dos ticked off today (counted from this morning's briefing), habits
  done, money spent today, steps and water, and tomorrow's calendar and reminders.
- **Week planner**: "Plan my week." A table of the next 7 days: calendar, reminders, bills due and birthdays.
- **What's next**: "What's next today?" Your next calendar event or reminder, with a countdown on screen.
- **What can you do?**: "What can you do?" A list of topics pops up; click one to see things to say, and click a
  line to say it.
- **Thought of the day**: "Give me a thought for the day." A reflective question for your diary (a new one each day).

## Capture and remember

- **Quick note**: "Note that I need a new bike light." Saved with the time in `Inbox.md` in the Ideas folder.
- **Read the inbox**: "Read my inbox." The notes pop up as a list.
- **Clear the inbox**: "Clear my inbox." (Alfred checks with you first.)
- **Where I put things**: "My passport is in the top drawer."
- **Find something**: "Where's my passport?"
- **Everything I've put away**: "Where have I put things?" (a table pops up) / "Forget where the passport is."
- **Three good things**: "Three good things today: a sunny walk, lunch with Sam, finished the report."
- **Good things this month**: "Show my good things this month." A table of each day's good things.

## Decisions, checklists and loans

- **Pros and cons**: "Help me decide about moving to Leeds: cheaper rent is a big pro, being far from friends is a con."
  Give a weight from 1 to 5 if you like. A table pops up with the score.
- **Look at a decision**: "Show my pros and cons for moving to Leeds." / "What decisions am I weighing up?" /
  "Delete the Leeds decision."
- **Save a checklist**: "Save a checklist called leaving the house: keys, wallet, hob off, windows shut."
- **Start a checklist**: "Start my leaving the house checklist." It pops up with tick boxes.
- **List checklists**: "What checklists have I got?" (click one to start it) / "Delete the leaving the house checklist."
- **Lend something**: "I lent Sam my drill." / "I borrowed a ladder from Dad."
- **Got it back**: "Sam gave the drill back."
- **What's out on loan**: "Who has my stuff?" A table pops up with who has what and for how many days.
