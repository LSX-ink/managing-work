# Time and dates

Things to say to Alfred:

- **World clock**: "What time is it in Tokyo?"
- **Time-zone conversion**: "What's 3pm London time in Johannesburg?"
- **Days until or since**: "How many days until Christmas?"
- **Age or exact duration**: "How old is someone born on 12 April 1990?"
- **Add or subtract time**: "What date is 90 days from today?"
- **Day of the week**: "What day of the week was 25 December 2000?"
- **Week number and day of the year**: "What week number is it?"
- **Countdowns**: "Start a countdown to my holiday on 18 December." / "Show my countdowns." / "Remove the holiday countdown."
- **Birthdays**: "Mum's birthday is 3 October 1965." / "Whose birthday is next?" / "Remove Sam's birthday."
  Birthdays stay on this PC in `birthdays.json` in the memory folder.
- **UK bank holidays**: "When's the next bank holiday?" (add "in Scotland" or "in Northern Ireland" if you like)
- **Sunrise and sunset**: "What time is sunset today?" (your home city unless you name another)
- **Moon phase**: "What's the moon like tonight?"
- **Stopwatch**: "Start the stopwatch." / "Lap." / "Stop the stopwatch." / "How long has the stopwatch been going?"
- **Focus sessions**: "Start a focus session." (25 minutes, with a chime) / "How long left on my focus session?"
- **Today**: "What's today?" (date, week number, days left in the year, next bank holiday)

Removing a countdown or birthday always asks you to confirm first.
