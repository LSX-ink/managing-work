# Live info: what to say to Alfred

Alfred can read live public feeds for you. None of them need an account, and Alfred only sends a city,
a topic or a coin name. Where a city matters, Alfred uses your home city (JARVIS_CITY) unless you name one.

## News
- **BBC headlines**: "What's in the news?" or "Give me the BBC tech headlines." (sections: top, world, UK,
  technology, business, sport, science, entertainment, health)
- **Hacker News**: "What's top on Hacker News?"

## Outdoors
- **Air quality**: "How's the air quality today?"
- **Pollen**: "What's the pollen count in Leeds?" (Europe only)
- **UV**: "Do I need sun cream today?"
- **Rain soon**: "Do I need an umbrella this afternoon?"
- **Wind and washing**: "Is it a good day to dry the washing outside?"
- **Northern lights**: "Any chance of seeing the aurora tonight?"
- **Green electricity**: "Is now a good time to run the washing machine?" (UK grid carbon)

## The world
- **Crypto prices**: "What's Bitcoin at?" or "How are Ethereum and Solana doing?"
- **Earthquakes**: "Any big earthquakes this week?"
- **Space station**: "Where's the ISS right now?"
- **People in space**: "Who's in space at the moment?"
- **Wikipedia**: "Tell me about Ada Lovelace."
- **On this day**: "What happened on this day in history?"
- **Something random**: "Tell me something random."
