# Study and exams

Help for GCSE, A-level, university and work-certificate study. Everything pops up on the Alfred screen and is saved in
`study.json` in your memory folder, on your PC only. Nothing goes online.

Already in Alfred, and built on rather than repeated: flashcard decks with spaced repetition and "I studied maths for
90 minutes" ([learning and goals](learning-and-goals.md)), the 25-minute focus timer ([time and dates](time-and-dates.md)),
mind maps and your notes ([knowledge](knowledge.md)), exams saved with the [discover](discover.md) study planner (they
show up in your courses and timetable too), language study ([languages](languages.md)), the tutor and quiz-master modes
([modes](modes.md)) and driving theory ([car and commute](car-and-commute.md)). Study breathing exercises are in
[calm](calm.md).

## Courses, timetable and deadlines
1. **Add a course**: "Add GCSE Maths, exam on 12 October 2026, aiming for a 7." Saves the subject, level, date and target.
2. **Exams and countdowns**: "How long until my exams?" A table of courses with days left.
3. **Remove a course**: "Remove my Biology course." Alfred asks first.
4. **Revision timetable**: "Make me a revision timetable." A week grid pops up, one column per day and a coloured, named
   block per session. It works back from your exam dates, gives nearer exams and red topics more sessions, and keeps the
   day before an exam for that subject. Click **Next week** for the rest.
5. **Today's plan**: "What should I study today?" A list of today's sessions and deadlines due soon.
6. **Add a deadline**: "Add a deadline: history essay due 15 October, high priority."
7. **See deadlines**: "What deadlines have I got?" A table sorted by date; overdue ones say OVERDUE.
8. **Tick one off**: "Mark the history essay as done."
9. **Exam day checklist**: "Show my Maths exam day checklist." Tick boxes for the night before, the morning and the room.

## Topics and confidence (red, amber, green)
Every topic shows a letter (R, A, G) and a word as well as its colour, and the heatmap uses patterns, so it works if you
can't tell red from green.

10. **Add topics**: "Add topics to Maths: algebra, geometry and number."
11. **Rate a topic**: "I'm amber on algebra in Maths." Red comes back for review tomorrow, amber in 3 days, green in 10.
12. **Topic checklist**: "Show my Maths topics." A list; click a topic to move it red, amber, green.
13. **Heatmap**: "Show my topic heatmap." Every subject as coloured squares with letters; click one to change it.
14. **What next**: "What should I revise next?" The weakest topics first, each with a start-a-Pomodoro button.
15. **Due for review**: "Which topics are due for review?" Topics whose review date has come, with a quiz button.
16. **Remove a topic**: "Remove geometry from Maths." Alfred asks first.

## Formula sheet
17. **Add a formula**: "Add to my Maths formulas: area of a circle, A equals pi r squared."
18. **Show it big**: "Show my Maths formula sheet." Big monospaced text with A- and A+ buttons.
19. **Remove one**: "Remove Pythagoras from my formulas." Alfred asks first.

## Sessions, papers and grades
20. **Pomodoro for a subject**: "Start a pomodoro for Biology." A 25-minute countdown pops up.
21. **Log a session**: "Log my study session." (25 minutes after a Pomodoro) or "I did 40 minutes of Maths."
22. **Weekly hours chart**: "Chart my study hours this week." Bars per subject; "for the last 8 weeks" gives a line.
23. **Log a past paper**: "I got 62 out of 80 on Maths Paper 1 2024." Says the percentage, grade and trend.
24. **Past papers table**: "Show my Maths past papers."
25. **Paper trend**: "Am I improving in Maths papers?" A line chart of your percentages.
26. **Enter grade boundaries**: "Maths boundaries: grade 4 from 40 percent, 7 from 72, 9 from 85." They are your own
    numbers, so copy them from the exam board.
27. **Show boundaries**: "Show my Maths grade boundaries."
28. **Mark to grade**: "What grade is 36 out of 50 in Maths?"
29. **Set a component**: "Maths coursework is 40 percent and I got 80." / "The final exam is 60 percent."
30. **What do I need**: "What do I need in the final to get a 7 in Maths?" A table of parts, and the mark you need.

## Writing and recall
31. **Essay plan**: "Plan an analysis essay on Macbeth's ambition, 1000 words." Alfred fills a five-part structure with
    points and shows it; you write the essay.
32. **Citation**: "Harvard reference for Smith, John, 2020, Learning to Learn, Pearson, London." Also APA, and books,
    websites and journal articles. It is worked out on your PC from what you say; check it against your school's guide.
33. **Quiz me on my notes**: "Quiz me on my Photosynthesis note." Alfred asks questions from a `.md` or `.txt` note one at
    a time and gives a score at the end.
34. **Study break**: "Give me a 10 minute break idea." Three ideas plus a calm breathing exercise and a break timer.
