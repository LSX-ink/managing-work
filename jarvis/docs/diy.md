# DIY and home projects

UK sizes, metric first. Things to say to Alfred. Room measurements, project steps and your tool list are saved in
your memory folder on this PC (`diy-rooms.json`, `diy-projects.json`, `diy-tools.json`), nowhere else. The guides and
reference tables are built in, and nothing here goes online. Removing anything asks you to confirm first.

Materials for a project (with costs) are the "DIY projects" in [household-plus.md](household-plus.md); this page adds
steps, sums, plans and guides.

## Materials calculators

Give a room you've saved ("for the lounge"), or the sizes ("4 by 3 metres, 2.4 high, one door, one window").
Each answer pops up a small table of what to buy, with the working. Coverage figures are typical; check the tin or pack.

1. **Paint**: "How much paint for the lounge, two coats?" (litres, and which tins to buy; add "and the ceiling")
2. **Wallpaper**: "How many wallpaper rolls for a 4 by 3 room, 2.4 high?" (add a pattern repeat if it has one)
3. **Floor tiles**: "How many 300 millimetre tiles for 10 square metres?" (10% wastage, plus adhesive)
4. **Laminate**: "How many packs of laminate for a 5 by 4 room?" (7% wastage, plus underlay)
5. **Skirting board**: "How much skirting for the bedroom?" (less the doors, in 2.4 m lengths)
6. **Fence**: "How many fence panels and posts for 9 metres?" (plus gravel boards and postmix)
7. **Concrete**: "How much concrete for a 2 by 2 metre base, 100 mm deep?" (cubic metres and bags of postmix)
8. **Decking**: "How many decking boards for 4 by 3 metres?" (boards, joists and screws)
9. **Grout**: "How much grout for 10 square metres of 300 millimetre tiles?"
10. **Plasterboard**: "How many plasterboard sheets for the spare room walls?"
11. **Gravel**: "How many tonnes of gravel for 20 square metres, 50 mm deep?" (and bulk bags)
12. **Turf**: "How many rolls of turf for 20 square metres?"
13. **Bricks**: "How many bricks for a 10 square metre wall?" (plus mortar)

## Rooms and floor plans

14. **Save a room**: "Add the lounge, 5 metres by 4, 2.4 high." (centimetres, millimetres, feet and inches work too)
15. **Add a door or window**: "The lounge has a door on the south wall." / "A 1.5 metre window on the north wall."
    North is the top of the plan.
16. **Add furniture**: "Put a 1.5 by 2 metre bed in the north east corner of the bedroom."
    Pops up: the room drawn to scale with doors, windows and furniture.
17. **Show the floor plan**: "Show the floor plan of the lounge." / "Sketch a 3 by 2.5 metre room."
18. **Measurements book**: "Show my room measurements book."
    Pops up: a table of every room with its floor, distance round, wall area and volume.
19. **Room details**: "What are the details of the kitchen?" Pops up: the sums for the room and what's in it.
20. **Remove a room or furniture**: "Remove the bed from the bedroom." (asks first)

## How-to guides

Each guide pops up as a checklist you tick off as you go, with a safety box, what you'll need, how long it takes and
when to call a professional.

21. **How-to guide**: "How do I bleed a radiator?" / "Change a fuse." / "Unblock a sink." / "Fix a dripping tap." /
    "Hang a picture level." / "Reset a tripped RCD." Also: turning off the water at the stopcock, patching plasterboard,
    resealing a bath, painting a room, hanging a shelf and unblocking a toilet. "What DIY guides have you got?" lists them.
22. **Safety reminders**: "What should I check before I drill into a wall?" (studs, pipes and cables) Also electrics,
    gas, water, ladders, asbestos, lead paint, dust, power tools and lifting.

## Reference tables

23. **Drill bits**: "Which drill bit for brick?" / "For tiles?"
24. **Screw sizes**: "What pilot hole for a number 8 screw?" (clearance and pilot holes by gauge)
25. **Wall fixings**: "What fixing for plasterboard?" (plugs, toggles and studs by type of wall)
26. **Paint finishes**: "Which paint finish for a bathroom?" (matt to gloss, and where to use each)
27. **Wood stain and finishes**: "What's the difference between decking oil and stain?"

## Project steps and tools

28. **Start a project**: "Start a DIY project for the shed: base, walls, roof, paint." Saying it again adds more steps.
29. **Tick off a step**: "I've done step 2 of the shed project." / "Undo step 2."
30. **Show a project**: "Show the shed project." Pops up: a tick list with progress; ticking a step tells Alfred.
31. **All projects**: "How are my DIY projects going?" Pops up: a table of progress and each next step.
32. **Remove a project**: "Remove the shed project." (asks first)
33. **Add a tool**: "I've got a cordless drill in the garage."
34. **Lend a tool**: "Sam has borrowed my drill."
35. **Get a tool back**: "Sam brought the drill back."
36. **Tool inventory**: "What tools do I have?" / "Which tools are lent out?" Pops up: a table with where each is kept,
    who has it and for how long.
37. **Remove a tool**: "Remove the hammer from my tools." (asks first)

Alfred only gives general guidance. For gas work, a new electrical circuit or anything you're unsure of, use a
registered professional.
