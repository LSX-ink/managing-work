# Calculators

Alfred works these out exactly instead of guessing. Things to say:

- **Sums**: "What's 17.5 percent of 240 times 3?" or "What's the square root of 2,209?"
- **Unit conversion**: "How many kilometres is 26.2 miles?"
- **Temperature**: "What's 350 Fahrenheit in Celsius?"
- **Cooking**: "How many grams is a cup of flour?" / "What's gas mark 5 in Celsius?"
- **Percentages**: "What's 15% of 80?" / "What percent is 30 of 120?" / "What's the percentage change from 80 to 100?"
- **Tip and split the bill**: "Add a 12.5% tip to £86.40 and split it three ways."
- **Loan or mortgage**: "What's the monthly repayment on a £200,000 mortgage at 4.5% over 25 years?"
- **Savings growth**: "If I save £100 a month at 4% for 5 years, how much will I have?"
- **Savings goal**: "How long to save £5,000 putting away £200 a month?"
- **Take-home pay**: "What's my take-home on £35,000 with a 5% pension and a student loan?"
  (An estimate for England in the 2026/27 tax year, with a Plan 2 student loan.)
- **VAT**: "Add VAT to £250." / "Take the VAT off £120."
- **BMI**: "What's my BMI? I'm 5 foot 9 and 11 stone." (gives the NHS category)
- **Better value**: "Which is better value, 500g for £2.10 or 750g for £2.90?"
- **Fuel cost**: "How much fuel for 120 miles at 45 mpg with petrol at 145.9p a litre?"
- **Coin, dice and random numbers**: "Flip a coin." / "Roll 2d6." / "Pick a number from 1 to 50." /
  "Pick one: pizza, curry or tacos."
- **Password**: "Make me a 20-character password." (shown on screen, not read aloud unless you ask)
- **Roman numerals**: "What's 1987 in Roman numerals?" / "What number is MMXXVI?"

Nothing here goes online or is saved.
