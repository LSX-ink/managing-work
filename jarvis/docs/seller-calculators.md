# Seller calculators

Work out what you'll really keep when you sell online or resell things: fees, postage, profit, stock and returns. Everything
is saved in your memory folder on this PC (files called `sellercalc-...`). Nothing is posted, no marketplace is contacted
and no internet is needed. Money is in pounds (GBP).

**Fees change.** The fee table is approximate and dated (last looked over **2026-09**). Every result says "fees change, check
the site". You can override any rate and Alfred remembers it. UK tax and rules notes are general: check GOV.UK.

Not repeated here: hourly rate, break-even and order log (side-hustles.md), digital products (digital-products.md), brand
deals and creator income (creator-business.md), general budgets (money-plus.md), unit and percentage maths (calculators.md),
the home inventory (home-admin-and-shopping.md). Income tracking with tax-pot reminders is a separate ability.

## Fees and profit (sellercalc_fees)

1. **Fee table**: "Show the selling fee table." Pops up: every platform's rate, the date it was checked, and yours marked; click one for details.
2. **Change a rate**: "Set my Etsy fee to 9 percent plus 20p." Saved and used from then on.
3. **Reset a rate**: "Put the Etsy fee back to normal." (or platform "all")
4. **Profit on a sale**: "What do I make selling on Etsy for £20, cost £5, £3 postage?" Pops up: fees, costs, profit, margin and return.
5. **Compare platforms**: "Compare my £25 item across every platform, it cost £8." Pops up: net profit side by side, best first; click one to look closer.
6. **Price for a target**: "What price gets me £5 profit on eBay, cost £6?" Pops up: the lowest price. Leave out the platform for a table of all of them.
7. **Price for a margin**: "What price gives me a 30 percent margin?" Same solver, aiming at margin.
8. **Lowest price**: "What's the lowest I can sell this for on Depop, cost £10?" Pops up: the break-even price.
9. **Check an offer**: "Someone offered £20 on my £30 Depop item, it cost £10." Pops up: profit at the offer and a counter suggestion.
10. **Discount check**: "What if I take 20 percent off my £25 Etsy item?" Pops up: profit lost per sale and how many more sales you'd need.
11. **Bundle offer**: "Bundle these two eBay items with 10 percent off." Pops up: bundle profit against selling separately.
12. **How Vinted works**: "How do Vinted fees work?" Pops up: the seller pays nothing, the buyer pays a protection fee and postage.
13. **Amazon FBA or FBM**: "Rough FBA against FBM for a £20 item costing £5." Pops up: both profits, with caveats.
14. **Print-on-demand price**: "Base cost £12, what should I sell my print-on-demand mug for?" Pops up: the price after fees. Say a markup percent for a Redbubble-style site.
15. **Own shop month**: "My shop plan is £25 and I get 20 orders at £30." Pops up: profit per order, the month, orders to cover the plan.
16. **Postage bands**: "Show my postage prices." Pops up: Royal Mail and Evri size bands (approximate).
17. **Edit postage**: "Set Evri small parcel to £3.10." Saved. New services need a price and top weight.
18. **Postage suggestion**: "Cheapest postage for a 600 gram parcel?" Pops up: the cheapest band that fits.
19. **Reset postage**: "Reset my postage table." Asks you to confirm first.

## Stock and results (sellercalc_stock)

20. **Add an item**: "Bought a denim jacket from a charity shop for £6, category clothes." Logged with a number.
21. **List it**: "Listed the jacket on Vinted for £25." Tells you the profit after fees.
22. **Mark sold**: "Sold the jacket for £25 on eBay, postage £3." Works out fees from the table unless you say the real ones; gives profit and days to sell.
23. **Change an item**: "Change the jacket's category to coats." Also names, sources, notes and prices.
24. **Show stock**: "Show my reselling stock." Pops up: a table (filter by status, platform or category).
25. **One item**: "Show item 3." Pops up: cost, fees, postage, profit, days.
26. **Remove an item**: "Remove item 3." Asks you to confirm first.
27. **Stock value**: "What is my reselling stock worth?" Pops up: unsold cost by category and listed asking prices.
28. **Ageing**: "How old is my unsold stock?" Pops up: bars by age band.
29. **Sell-through**: "What's my sell-through rate?" Pops up: items sold out of items bought, by category.
30. **Best categories**: "What are my best-selling categories?" Pops up: bars by profit with days to sell.
31. **Monthly profit**: "Chart my reselling profit for six months." Pops up: a bar chart.
32. **Summary**: "Give me my reselling summary." Pops up: profit, this month, sold, unsold, sell-through, average days.
33. **Slow movers**: "What hasn't sold in 60 days?" Pops up: a list; click one to see it.
34. **Platform results**: "Which platform makes me the most?" Pops up: a table of sales, fees, profit and average days.
35. **Days to sell**: "How quickly do things sell?" Pops up: average days by platform or category.
36. **Export**: "Export my stock to a spreadsheet." Saves a CSV in the Selling folder and shows it.
37. **Log a return**: "Dress came back, too small, refund £18, return postage £2.50."
38. **Returns**: "Show my returns." Pops up: a table with cost and return rate.
39. **Close a return**: "Close return 1, restocked."

## Guides and sourcing (sellercalc_guide)

51. **HMRC and online sellers**: "Will HMRC be told about my Vinted sales?" Pops up: platforms report sellers with 30 or more sales, or about £1,700 a year; being reported doesn't mean you owe tax; check GOV.UK.
52. **Sourcing tips**: "Tips for car boot sales." Pops up: tips for car boots, charity shops, house clearances and online auctions.
53. **Sourcing note**: "Note: Sunday car boot, spent £20, hope to sell for £60, arrive early." Saved.
54. **Sourcing notes**: "Show my sourcing notes." Pops up: a table; says which place looks best on your own guesses.
55. **Remove a note**: "Remove sourcing note 2." Asks you to confirm first.
56. **Most to pay**: "What's the most I should pay for something I can sell for £30 on Vinted and make £10?" Pops up: the ceiling price.
57. **Seasonal ideas**: "What sells at this time of year?" Pops up: this month and next month.
58. **Seller scams**: "Scams to watch for when selling." Pops up: overpayment, fake payment emails and others.
59. **Records to keep**: "What records should I keep as a seller?" Pops up: a list.
60. **Returns rules**: "What are my returns duties as a seller?" Pops up: private against business sellers (general).
61. **Platform summary**: "Tell me about selling on Depop." Pops up: what it is good for, fees, what to watch.
62. **Glossary**: "What's the difference between margin and markup?" Pops up: plain definitions.
