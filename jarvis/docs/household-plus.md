# Household plus: running the home and family

Things to say to Alfred. Everything is saved in your memory folder on this PC (files called `household-...`),
nowhere else. Most answers also pop up a window on the Alfred screen: a table, a chart or a tick list. Ticking a box
in a pop-up tells Alfred it's done, just as if you'd said it.

Removing anything (a chore, a project, a pet, a contact, an event) always asks you to confirm first.

## Chores and upkeep

- **Chores rota**: "The rota is Sam, Alex and Jo." / "Add hoovering, bins and dishes to the rota." /
  "Whose turn is it to do the bins?" / "The hoovering's done." / "Show this week's chores."
  Pops up: a table of each chore, whose turn it is and whether it's done. Chores move to the next person every Monday.
- **Cleaning schedule**: "Clean the bathroom every 7 days." / "What cleaning is due today?" / "I've cleaned the bathroom."
  Pops up: today's cleaning as a tick list; "Show my whole cleaning schedule" gives a table.
- **Home maintenance**: "Test the smoke alarms every month." / "Clear the gutters every 12 months, last done in March." /
  "What maintenance is due this month?" / "I've tested the smoke alarms."
  Pops up: a table of every job with its next due date, marking what's overdue or due this month.
- **Laundry symbols**: "What does a triangle with two lines on a label mean?" / "Show me the laundry symbols."
  Pops up: a table of the matching care symbols (about 27 in the full chart).
- **Laundry timer**: "Start a laundry timer for 45 minutes." Alfred tells you when it's done.
  Pops up: a live countdown with a Cancel button.

## Things in the house

- **Home inventory**: "Add the TV to the lounge, worth 499 pounds, warranty until 1 November 2026." /
  "Show my home inventory."
  Pops up: a table of items with room, value, purchase date and warranty end.
- **Warranties ending soon**: "Which warranties run out soon?" Pops up: a table with a countdown for each.
- **Value by room**: "How much is the stuff in each room worth?" Pops up: a bar chart of total value by room.
- **Freezer**: "I've put a chilli in the freezer." / "I've taken the chilli out." / "What's in the freezer?"
  Pops up: a table, oldest first, with how long each thing has been in.
- **Grocery price book**: "Milk is 1.45 at Tesco." / "Where is milk cheapest?" / "Show the price history for milk."
  Pops up: a table of the latest price at each shop, or a line chart of prices over time.
- **DIY projects**: "Start a DIY project for shelves: brackets, screws, oak board." / "The oak board costs 25 pounds." /
  "I've bought the screws." / "Show the shelves project."
  Pops up: a materials tick list with the total cost; "Show my DIY projects" gives a table.

## Meters and the car

- **Meter readings**: "My electric meter reads 12,345." / "Show my gas use."
  Works for electric, gas and water. Pops up: a line chart of units used per day between readings.
- **Energy cost estimate**: "Electricity is 24.5p a unit." / "What will my energy cost this month?"
  Uses your recent readings and the unit price you gave.
- **Car fuel log**: "I filled up at 10,400 miles, 40 litres, 58 pounds." / "How's my car's mpg?"
  Fill the tank to full each time for accurate mpg. Pops up: a chart of mpg per fill-up, with the average and the
  cost per mile.
- **Where I parked**: "I parked on level 3, bay 42." / "Where did I park?" / "I've got the car."
  Pops up: the note and what time you parked, with a Got the car button.

## Family and pets

- **Pet care**: "Add Biscuit, our dog." / "I've fed Biscuit." / "Has Biscuit been fed?" /
  "Biscuit's flea treatment is due on 5 October." / "What's due for the pets?" / "Biscuit weighs 13 kilos." /
  "Show Biscuit's weight."
  Pops up: a table of vet, vaccination and flea dates, or a line chart of weight.
- **Party planner**: "Plan Mia's party on 10 October, budget 150 pounds." / "Invite Ava, Leo and Zara." /
  "Ava said yes." / "Gran is bringing the cake." / "We've got the cake." / "I spent 40 pounds on the hall." /
  "Show Mia's party." / "Show the to-bring list."
  Pops up: the guest list with each RSVP and a summary of budget and things still to bring, or the to-bring tick list.
- **Guest counts**: "How many have said yes to Mia's party?" Pops up: a bar chart of yes, no, maybe and not replied.
- **Sitter info sheet**: "For the sitter, allergies: Mia is allergic to peanuts." / "Bedtimes: Mia at half seven." /
  "Show the sitter sheet."
  Pops up: the sheet, ready to read out or leave on screen. It's also saved as `household-sitter-sheet.md`.
- **Emergency contacts**: "Add Gran to emergency contacts, 07700 900123." / "Show my emergency contacts."
  Pops up: a table of names and numbers. They're kept only in your memory folder, never sent to any website, and
  Alfred doesn't read them back aloud; only the pop-up shows them.
- **School and family dates**: "Half term is 26 to 30 October." / "Add an inset day on 20 November." /
  "What school dates are coming up?"
  Pops up: a table of dates with a countdown, showing "now" for anything happening today.
