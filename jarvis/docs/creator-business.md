# Creator business

The business side of being a creator. Everything is saved in your memory folder on this PC (files called
`creatorbiz-...`) and nothing is ever sent, posted or paid anywhere. Drafts, invoices and your media kit are saved as
ordinary files in the `Creator business` folder. Money is in pounds (GBP).

Alfred never promises income. Rate cards and quotes are rough estimates from numbers you type in. UK tax and advertising
notes are general information only: check GOV.UK, and the ASA and CMA guidance, for the current rules.

Not repeated here: ideas, hooks, scripts, captions, hashtags, posting calendars and view or follower stats (see the
creator ideas and stats docs), and general invoices in documents.md (this page tracks creator invoices as paid, unpaid or overdue).

## Brand deals (creator_deals)

1. **Add a deal**: "I've got a lead with Glow Skincare for a summer campaign, £500." Pops up: the pipeline board.
2. **Move it along**: "Move Glow to negotiating." Stages: lead, pitched, negotiating, agreed, delivered, invoiced, paid, declined.
3. **Pipeline board**: "Show my brand deal pipeline." Pops up: a column per stage; click a deal or its arrow.
4. **List deals**: "List my brand deals." Pops up: a table with stage, fee and follow-up date.
5. **One deal**: "Show my Glow deal." Pops up: stage, fee, terms, deliverables and history.
6. **Update terms**: "Glow wants 3 months of paid ads usage and pays in 30 days."
7. **Follow-ups due**: "Which brands do I need to chase?" Pops up: a list; clicking one says you followed up.
8. **Set a follow-up**: "Remind me to chase Glow next week."
9. **Followed up**: "I've followed up with Glow." Next nudge in a week.
10. **Add a note**: "Note on Glow: they want a call first."
11. **Show notes**: "What did I note about Glow?" Pops up: the notes.
12. **Pipeline value**: "What are my brand deals worth?" Pops up: fees by stage; agreed-but-unpaid total (not guaranteed).
13. **Remove a deal**: "Remove the Glow deal." Asks you to confirm first.

## Deliverables, gifts and affiliates (creator_work)

14. **Add a deliverable**: "Glow needs one Instagram reel by Friday." (a date like 2026-10-09)
15. **All deliverables**: "What do I owe brands?" Pops up: a table by due date.
16. **Due soon**: "What brand work is due this week?" Pops up: a list; click one to tick it off.
17. **Tick off**: "I've finished the reel for Glow."
18. **Reopen**: "Reopen the Glow reel."
19. **Remove a deliverable**: "Remove the story set from Glow."
20. **Log a gift**: "Lush sent me a face mask worth £12." Pops up: the gift table, with a reminder about labelling.
21. **List gifts**: "What gifted products have I logged?" Pops up: a table.
22. **Gift status**: "I posted the Lush mask and labelled it as gifted." Statuses: received, posted, not posting, returned.
23. **Gift value**: "How much gifted stuff have I had?" Says the total and that gifts can count as income.
24. **Add an affiliate code**: "Add Amazon affiliate, code ME10, 4% commission." Pops up: the affiliate log.
25. **Affiliate log**: "Show my affiliate programmes."
26. **Affiliate earnings**: "I earned £7.50 from Amazon affiliate." Also added to your income.

## Money (creator_money)

27. **Rate card estimate**: "Work out a rate card, I get about 10,000 views a video." Pops up: a price range per format, plus
    extras for usage, exclusivity and rush. Views and per-1,000-view rates are yours to change.
28. **Save my rate numbers**: "Save my hourly rate as £40 and 5 hours per video." A time-based minimum is used too.
29. **Package quote**: "Quote two videos and a story set with usage rights, 10% off." Pops up: a table with a total range.
30. **Make an invoice**: "Invoice Glow £500 for one reel, 20% VAT." Saved as a Markdown file with the next number; the deal moves to invoiced.
31. **Invoice list**: "Show my invoices." Pops up: paid, unpaid or overdue with a mark-paid button; can filter by status.
32. **Show an invoice**: "Show invoice INV-2026-001."
33. **Mark paid**: "Glow paid invoice 1." Adds the money to your income and moves the deal to paid.
34. **Overdue**: "Which invoices are overdue?" Pops up: the late ones.
35. **Chase draft**: "Draft a firm chase for the Zed invoice." Three tones; a draft only.
36. **Due date**: "If I invoice today on 30-day terms, when is it due?"
37. **Remove an invoice**: "Delete invoice 2." Asks you to confirm first; the file stays.
38. **Log income**: "I got £250 from Glow as a deposit."
39. **Log an expense**: "I spent £40 on a ring light." Categories such as equipment, software, travel.
40. **Expenses**: "Show my creator expenses." Pops up: a bar chart by category.
41. **Deal summary**: "How did the Glow deal work out?" Pops up: fee, invoiced, received, expenses and what is left.
42. **Month summary**: "Creator money this month." Pops up: income minus expenses for six months.
43. **Tax year summary**: "Creator income for this tax year." Pops up: a general summary; check GOV.UK or an accountant.
