# Photography and video

Alfred can be your camera bag companion: sums for exposure and lenses, shot lists you tick off on screen, a gear
list, and ideas for when you are stuck. Nothing goes online. Your shot lists, projects and gear are kept in small
files in the memory folder (`photo-shots.json`, `photo-projects.json`, `photo-gear.json`).

Shutter speeds can be said as "one over one twenty-five" (1/125) or in seconds. Sensors: full frame, APS-C, micro
four thirds, 1 inch, medium format or phone; if you don't say, Alfred assumes full frame.

## Calculators

Each answer pops up a table, or a drawn diagram where noted.

1. **Equivalent exposure**: "I shot f/2.8 at 1/125 and ISO 100. What shutter if I go to f/8?" A table of the matching
   settings. Leave out the change and you get a ladder of aperture and shutter pairs.
2. **Exposure value**: "What EV is f/8 at 1/125?" or "What shutter for EV 15 at f/16?" A table with a typical scene.
3. **Sunny 16**: "What's the sunny 16 rule at ISO 200?" A table of apertures for sun, cloud and shade.
4. **Slowest handheld shutter**: "How slow can I go handheld at 200 mm on APS-C?" A table with and without stabilisation.
5. **Depth of field**: "Depth of field at 50 mm, f/1.8, focused at 2 metres?" A drawn diagram of the sharp zone.
6. **Hyperfocal distance**: "Hyperfocal distance for 24 mm at f/8?" A table: where to focus for everything sharp.
7. **Field of view**: "What does 50 mm on APS-C see at 10 metres?" A drawn diagram of the angle, and the
   full-frame equivalent.
8. **Star shots**: "How long can I expose the stars at 14 mm f/2.8 on 24 megapixels?" A table with the 500 rule and
   the sharper NPF rule.
9. **ND filters**: "1/125 with a 10 stop ND filter?" or "What ND to get 30 seconds?" A table of times for common filters.
10. **Flash guide number**: "Guide number 40, how far at f/4?" A table (or work out the aperture from a distance).
11. **Print size and pixels**: "What can I print from 6000 by 4000?" or "How many pixels for a 30 by 20 cm print?" A table.
12. **Aspect ratio crop**: "Crop 6000 by 4000 to 16:9." A drawn diagram of the crop. Leave out the ratio for a table of
    all of them.
13. **Video bitrate and recording time**: "How long can I record 4K30 on a 128 GB card?" or "How much space for 30
    minutes of iPhone 4K60?" A table.
14. **Photos per card**: "How many 24 megapixel raw photos fit on 64 GB?" A table, with video time too.
15. **Timelapse planner**: "Timelapse of two hours, one shot every 5 seconds?" A table of frames and clip length. Give
    any two of interval, duration and clip length.

## Shot lists and projects

16. **New shot list**: "Make a wedding shot list called Sam's wedding." A pop-up with a tick box for each shot.
    Say a type (portrait, landscape, wedding, street, night, macro, wildlife, product, sports, family, travel,
    event, property) or give your own shots.
17. **Add shots**: "Add bouquet toss to Sam's wedding."
18. **Show a shot list**: "Show my wedding shot list." Tick the boxes on the pop-up as you shoot; each tick is saved.
19. **Tick off a shot**: "Tick off the rings shot." Say "untick" to undo.
20. **Remove a shot**: "Remove the cake shot from the wedding list."
21. **Clear the ticks**: "Clear the ticks on my wedding shot list."
22. **My shot lists**: "What shot lists have I got?" A table with progress.
23. **Starter ideas**: "What shots should I get at a street shoot?" A preview pop-up with a Save this list button.
24. **Delete a shot list**: "Delete the wedding shot list." Alfred asks you to confirm first.
25. **Start a photo project**: "Start a 365 project called 365 of 2026." or "Start a 100 portraits count project."
26. **Log today's photo**: "Log today's photo for my 365, it was a sunrise." A pop-up grid of squares that fills in.
27. **Project progress**: "How's my 365 going?" The grid, with day, missed days and streak.
28. **My projects**: "What photo projects have I got?" A table.
29. **Delete a project**: "Delete the 365 project." Asks you to confirm first.

## Gear

30. **Add gear**: "Add my Sony A6400, an APS-C camera." or "Add a 56 mm f/1.4 lens called Sigma 56."
31. **Gear list**: "What's in my camera bag?" A table (say "just my lenses" for one type).
32. **Lens details**: "Tell me about my Sigma 56." A table with the focal length, angle of view and full-frame equivalent.
33. **Update gear**: "Note that my Sigma 56 is sharp wide open."
34. **Remove gear**: "Remove my old tripod." Asks you to confirm first.
35. **What to pack**: "What should I pack for a landscape shoot?" A tick list that notes which items you own.
36. **Which lens**: "Which of my lenses for portraits?" A table of your lenses that fit. Leave out the subject for a
    guide to every kind of photo.

## Ideas and learning

37. **Composition tip**: "Show me the rule of thirds." A picture with the guide lines drawn on. Also leading lines,
    symmetry, framing, diagonals, golden ratio, golden spiral, negative space, fill the frame, layers, odd numbers and
    patterns.
38. **Composition list**: "What composition tips are there?" A list you can tap.
39. **Challenge of the day**: "What's today's photo challenge?" A card with a prompt and a tip.
40. **Random challenge**: "Give me a random photo challenge."
41. **Photo walk idea**: "Give me a 45 minute photo walk idea for the seaside." A tick list with a theme, a rule and a
    bonus. Places: town, park, seaside, countryside or home.
42. **Starting settings**: "What settings for waterfalls?" A table. Say nothing for a table of every scene.
43. **Photo words**: "What does bokeh mean?" A short explanation. Ask for the list to tap through them.
