# Words

Things to say to Alfred:

- **Definitions**: "What does ubiquitous mean?"
- **Synonyms**: "Give me another word for happy."
- **Opposites**: "What's the opposite of generous?"
- **Rhymes**: "What rhymes with orange?"
- **Sounds like**: "What words sound like 'nite'?" (handy when you're not sure of a spelling)
- **Spelling**: "How do you spell necessary?"
- **Counting**: "How many words are in this: ..." (Alfred gives words and characters)
- **Word of the day**: "What's the word of the day?"

Definitions come from the free dictionaryapi.dev and word lists from Datamuse; only the word you ask about is sent.
