# Freelancing

Running freelance or gig work for clients (design, editing, writing, video editing, virtual assistant work and similar).
Everything is saved in your memory folder on this PC (files called `freelance-...`). Proposals, case studies and drafts are
saved as ordinary files in the `Freelance` folder. Nothing is ever sent, posted or paid anywhere: emails and messages are
drafts for you to copy. Money is in pounds (GBP).

Alfred never promises income. Rates and prices are estimates from the numbers you give him. UK tax and legal notes are
general guidance only: check GOV.UK for the current rules and rates.

Not repeated here: brand deals, creator invoices and contract checklists (creator-business.md), side-hustle pricing and
order logs (side-hustles.md), income streams and UK tax helpers (income-streams.md), general invoices (documents.md), work
projects and the kanban board (work.md), and job hunting (career.md). This page is for your own clients.

## Clients and leads (freelance_clients)

1. **Add a client**: "Add a client, Acme Ltd, contact sam@acme.example, they need logos." Pops up: the client table.
2. **Client note**: "Note on Acme: they pay within a week." Also updates the contact.
3. **List clients**: "Show my freelance clients." Pops up: a table.
4. **One client**: "Tell me about Acme." Pops up: contact, projects, leads, hours and recent notes.
5. **Remove a client**: "Remove Acme from my clients." Asks you to confirm first; projects and time stay.
6. **Client value**: "Which client is worth the most?" Pops up: fees against hours and fee per hour.
7. **Add a lead**: "New lead: Zed wants website copy, about £400, from a referral." Pops up: the pipeline board.
8. **Move a lead**: "Move lead 1 to proposal sent." Stages: enquiry, proposal sent, won, lost. Sending a proposal sets a
   follow-up for a week; winning adds the client to your list; losing can record a reason.
9. **Pipeline**: "Show my freelance pipeline." Pops up: a column per stage; click a lead to move it on.
10. **Win rate**: "What's my win rate?" Pops up: won against lost, by source, average job and lost reasons. Small numbers
    swing a lot, so it is a rough guide.
11. **Leads to chase**: "Which leads do I need to chase?" Pops up: a list.
12. **Followed up**: "I've followed up with Zed." Next nudge in a week.
13. **Onboarding checklist**: "Show the onboarding checklist for Acme." / "Tick step 3 for Acme." Pops up: ticks you can click.
14. **Offboarding checklist**: "Show the offboarding checklist for Acme." Same idea, for wrapping a job up.
15. **Feedback and testimonial requests**: "Draft a testimonial request for Acme about the logo." Kinds: feedback, testimonial,
    referral, review. Pops up: the draft, also saved as a file.
16. **Save a testimonial**: "Save a testimonial from Acme: 'Brilliant and quick.' They said I can use it."
17. **Testimonial bank**: "Show my testimonials." / "...the ones I can use." Pops up: a table. Only publish ones with permission.

29. **Log time by hand**: "Log 90 minutes of admin for Zed, not billable." (hours are decimals, 1.5)

## Rates, planning and late payment (freelance_money)

41. **Save my numbers**: "Save my freelance target as £40,000 with 5 weeks holiday and 60% billable." Anything you don't give uses
    stated defaults (5 days a week, 7.5 hours a day, 5 weeks holiday, 8 bank holidays, 5 sick days, 60% billable).
42. **Day rate**: "What day rate do I need to earn £40,000 with £2,000 overheads?" Pops up: day and hourly rate, working days
    and billable days. It is before tax, and only billable days count.
43. **Project price**: "Price a 10 hour job at £40 an hour with a 15% buffer." Pops up: the price, rounded up to £5.
44. **Price options**: "Give me three price options around £400." Pops up: lower, middle and higher.
45. **Payment schedule**: "Split £1,000 into a 30% deposit, a milestone and a final payment." Pops up: amounts and dates.
46. **Real rate**: "I charged £500 for 20 hours with £50 costs; what did I really earn an hour?" Compared to your target.
47. **Rate rise**: "Put my rate up 10% from £40 with four weeks notice." Pops up: the new rate and a notice draft.
48. **Billable percentage**: "How billable was my week?" Pops up: a bar of billable and total hours.
49. **Capacity**: "How many 10 hour projects can I fit in the next month?" Pops up: free hours after a buffer.
50. **Availability**: "How busy am I over the next four weeks?" Pops up: a bar per week, from each project's remaining hours.
51. **Late payment interest**: "Work out interest on a £1,000 invoice 73 days late, base rate 4%." Pops up: 8% plus base rate
    interest and the fixed cost (£40, £70 or £100 by size). You give the base rate; check GOV.UK for the one that applies.
52. **Late payment explainer**: "Explain late payment interest for freelancers." Pops up: plain words on who it covers, interest,
    fixed costs, terms and sensible steps.
53. **Chase drafts**: "Draft a firm reminder to Zed for invoice 7, £250, due 1 September." Tones: polite, firm, final. The final
    one mentions statutory interest. Saved as a file; a draft only.

## Paperwork and profiles (freelance_docs)

54. **Proposal or quote**: "Build a proposal for Acme, Brand refresh, scope logo and colours, deliverables logo files, options
    Basic £400 and Full £900." Saved as `Proposal - ...` in Markdown, or HTML if you ask. Pops up: the file.
55. **Case study**: "Write a case study, Cafe rebrand: the menu was confusing, I interviewed staff and sketched, orders went up."
    Problem, process, result, optional numbers and quote. Use only real figures and ask the client first.
56. **Terms**: "Draft my freelance terms with a 25% deposit." General wording, not legal advice.
57. **Kickoff questions**: "Kickoff questions for a video editing job." Services: design, editing, writing, video editing,
    virtual assistant, other.
58. **Welcome message**: "Draft a welcome message for Acme starting 12 October."
59. **Proposal follow-up**: "Draft a follow-up for the Acme proposal."
60. **Profile bio**: "Write me a headline and bio for video editing for YouTubers."
61. **Platform checklist**: "Show my Fiverr profile checklist." Also Upwork, PeoplePerHour, Freelancer or general. Pops up:
    ticks you can click. Plain advice, no site connection; sites change rules, so read their help pages.
62. **Tick a step**: "Tick step 2 of my Upwork checklist."
63. **Platform fee**: "I'll charge £100 on a site that takes 20%, what do I keep?" You give the fee percentage.
64. **Freelance files**: "Show my freelance files." Pops up: a list; click one to open it.
