# Investing basics

Learning about investing and long-term money, in plain words. This is **education and calculators only**: Alfred never says
what to buy or sell, uses no live prices and no internet for any of it. Every calculator result is an **illustration**:
returns are not guaranteed, capital is at risk, and it is not financial advice. You pick the growth rate; results show a
low, middle and high case. UK notes are general information: rules and limits change, so check GOV.UK.

Your progress, quiz answers, practice portfolio and goals are saved in `investlearn.json` in your memory folder (GBP).

Not repeated here: budgets, savings pots, debts, net worth and tax-year summaries (money-plus.md), income streams
(income-streams.md), loan and simple savings calculators (calculators.md), study goals (learning-and-goals.md).

## Growth calculators (invest_calc)

1. **Compound growth**: "If I put in 1,000 and 100 a month for 20 years at 5%, what could it be?" Pops up: a line chart with low,
   middle and high cases, an inflation-adjusted line and the money paid in.
2. **Cost of waiting**: "What does waiting 5 years to start saving 100 a month cost me?" Pops up: bars for starting now or later.
3. **Saving to a target**: "How much a month to reach 20,000 in 10 years?" Pops up: bars at 0%, low, middle and high.
4. **Years to a target**: "How long to reach 50,000 saving 300 a month at 5%?" Pops up: a growth chart.
5. **Fees drag**: "Show how 0.2% fees compare with 1% over 30 years." Pops up: two lines and the difference.
6. **Inflation effect**: "What will 1,000 pounds be worth in 20 years at 3% inflation?" Pops up: a falling buying-power line.
7. **Real return**: "What is 6% growth after 3% inflation?" Pops up: bars.
8. **Rule of 72**: "How fast does money double at 6%?" or "What rate doubles money in 9 years?" Pops up: a table.
9. **Average yearly growth**: "1,000 became 2,000 in 10 years, what was the yearly growth?" Pops up: a steady path chart.
10. **Loss and recovery**: "I lose 50%, what gain gets it back?" Pops up: a table of falls and recoveries.
11. **Saving a bit more**: "What does an extra 50 a month add?" Pops up: two lines.
12. **Compare growth rates**: "Compare 2, 4 and 6% on 100 a month for 20 years." Pops up: bars.
13. **Pound-cost averaging with made-up prices**: "Pound-cost average 100 a month over prices 10, 8, 6, 9, 12." Pops up: a table of units bought.

## Learning (invest_learn)

14. **Learning path**: "Show me the investing learning path." Pops up: a list of ten lessons with progress; tap to open one.
15. **A lesson**: "Teach me lesson 5" or "teach me about fees." Pops up: the lesson text.
16. **Quiz**: "Quiz me on fees." Pops up: a question with A, B and C to tap.
17. **Answer a question**: "B for question 1." Says if you are right and why, with a Next button.
18. **Progress**: "How far through the investing path am I?" Pops up: bars per lesson (read and quiz score). Saved.
19. **Glossary**: "What does OCF mean?" or "Show the investing glossary." About 70 words; unknown words get suggestions.
20. **Flash cards**: "Give me 10 glossary flash cards." Pops up: tap-to-flip cards.
21. **Jargon buster**: "Explain this: the ETF has a low OCF and pays a dividend." Finds the jargon and gives plain meanings.
22. **Myth vs fact**: "Investing myths." Pops up: tap-to-flip cards.
23. **Scam warnings**: "How do I spot an investment scam?" Covers the FCA Register, clone firms, guaranteed returns.
24. **Scam check**: "Run the scam checklist." Six yes or no questions, then a count of red flags.
25. **UK wrappers in plain words**: "Explain the Lifetime ISA." Also stocks and shares ISA, cash ISA, junior ISA, workplace pension,
    SIPP, state pension, general investment account, premium bonds. Always with "check GOV.UK".
26. **Risk categories quiz**: "Start the risk quiz." Six questions; it only explains what cautious, balanced or adventurous mean and
    never picks products.
27. **Explainers**: "Explain pound-cost averaging", "the 4% rule and its caveats", diversification, risk and reward, compound growth,
    inflation, fees, emergency fund first, time horizon, pension basics.
28. **Reset progress**: "Reset my investing progress." Asks you to confirm first.

## UK sums, FIRE and goals (invest_plan)

29. **Emergency fund first**: "Do I have enough emergency savings? Essentials 1,500, savings 3,000." Pops up: bars against your target.
30. **FIRE number**: "How much for financial independence spending 20,000 a year?" Pops up: a table at 3, 3.5, 4 and 5% with the 4% rule caveats.
31. **Years to financial independence**: "Years to FI with 50,000 saved and 1,000 a month?" Pops up: a chart in today's money.
32. **Drawdown longevity**: "How long would 300,000 last taking 15,000 a year?" Pops up: low, middle and high lines.
33. **Workplace pension match**: "If I pay 5% instead of 3% and my employer matches, what do I gain?" Pops up: a table.
34. **Pension tax relief**: "I pay 800 into a pension as a higher-rate taxpayer." Pops up: the sums at general rates.
35. **Lifetime ISA bonus and penalty**: "What is the LISA bonus on 4,000, and the penalty for taking it out early?" Pops up: a table.
36. **ISA allowance left**: "I've used 5,000 of my ISA allowance." Pops up: how much is left before 5 April.
37. **Add a goal**: "New goal: house deposit, 20,000, 2,000 saved, 300 a month, by 2030." Kinds: house deposit, retirement, other.
38. **Show goals**: "Show my investing goals." All goals as bars; one goal as a low, middle and high projection chart with the target.
39. **Update a goal**: "Add 500 to my house deposit goal."
40. **Remove a goal**: "Remove the retirement goal." Asks you to confirm first.
41. **Milestones**: "Milestones for my house deposit." Pops up: 25, 50, 75 and 100 percent with estimated months.
42. **House deposit planner**: "Deposit planner for a 250,000 house, 5,000 saved, 400 a month." Pops up: 5 to 20 percent deposits and when.
43. **Retirement planner**: "I'm 30, retire at 67, want 20,000 a year, 10,000 saved, 300 a month." Pops up: chart in today's money.

## Practice portfolio (invest_practice)

Paper only: you type made-up holdings and prices. No live prices, no real money, clearly marked as practice.

44. **Add a holding**: "Practice: 10 units of Example Fund at 5 pounds." Adding again averages the cost.
45. **Set a price**: "Set Example Fund to 9 pounds." (You type the price; nothing is looked up.)
46. **Show the practice portfolio**: "Show my practice portfolio." Pops up: a table with value and gain or loss.
47. **Allocation**: "Show my practice allocation." Pops up: bars, warns when one holding is over half.
48. **What if it falls**: "What if my practice portfolio fell 20 percent?" Pops up: before and after, and the gain needed to recover.
49. **Practice sale**: "Sell 5 units of Example Fund at 8 pounds." Logs a made-up gain or loss.
50. **Trade log**: "Show my practice trades."
51. **Diversification check**: "Is my practice portfolio diversified?" A plain explanation, not advice.
52. **Rebalancing**: "Rebalance to 60 and 40." Pops up: how far each holding drifted from the split you typed.
53. **Remove a holding** and 54. **Clear the practice portfolio**: both ask you to confirm first.

Left out: live prices, product picks, and anything that says what to buy. Marketplace fee calculators belong to another set.
