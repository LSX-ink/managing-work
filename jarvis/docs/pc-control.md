# PC control (Windows)

These work when PC control is on (`JARVIS_ENABLE_PC=true`, the default) and Alfred runs on Windows.
Before shutting down, restarting, sleeping, emptying the Recycle Bin or closing an app, Alfred asks you first.

1. Read the clipboard: "What have I copied?"
2. Copy to the clipboard: "Copy my address to the clipboard."
3. Check brightness: "What's my screen brightness?"
4. Set brightness: "Set brightness to 60 percent." (laptop screens; desktop monitors use their own buttons)
5. Wi-Fi: "Which Wi-Fi am I on and how strong is it?"
6. IP and internet: "What's my IP address, and is the internet working?"
7. Dark mode: "Turn on dark mode."
8. Light mode: "Switch apps to light mode."
9. Screenshot: "Take a screenshot." (saved in the Screenshots memory folder; or "Screenshot into Work.")
10. Open windows: "What windows are open?"
11. Switch window: "Switch to Chrome."
12. Show the desktop: "Minimise everything."
13. Downloads: "What's taking up space in my Downloads?"
14. Recycle Bin: "Empty the Recycle Bin."
15. Sleep: "Put the PC to sleep."
16. Restart: "Restart the PC." (60 seconds' warning)
17. Shut down: "Shut down the PC." (60 seconds' warning)
18. Cancel: "Cancel the shutdown."
19. Settings pages: "Open Bluetooth settings." (display, sound, bluetooth, wifi, updates, apps, storage, notifications, power)
20. Sound device: "Which sound device am I using?"
21. Uptime: "How long has my PC been on?"
22. Busy apps: "What's using the most memory and CPU?"
23. Close an app: "Close Spotify." (Alfred won't close Windows itself or his own program)
