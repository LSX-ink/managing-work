# Events and occasions

Things to say to Alfred when you are planning a real-life occasion: a birthday, a kids' party, a wedding, Christmas
or a family gathering. Everything is saved in `events.json` in your memory folder on this PC. The only thing that
ever goes to a website is a city name, when you ask for the weather on the day.

Say which event you mean ("Mia's party"). If you leave it out, Alfred uses your only event, or the next one coming
up. Removing an event or a guest always asks you to confirm first.

This sits beside the games and quiz host ([Party host](party-host.md)), the simple party planner in
[Household plus](household-plus.md), your birthdays and countdowns ([Time and dates](time-and-dates.md)), the
Christmas card list and invite lists in [People](people.md), and gift ideas in [Home and life](home-and-life.md).
Events adds dietary needs, plus-ones, seating, suppliers, a budget by category and timelines on top.

## Your events

1. **Create an event**: "Plan Mia's party on 10 November at the village hall, budget 300 pounds, it's a kids' party."
   Saves it and adds the standard timeline for that kind (party, birthday, kids, wedding, christmas, gathering).
2. **Christmas**: "Plan Christmas." With no date it uses the next 25 December and adds the Christmas steps.
3. **My events**: "What events have I got?" Pops up a table with dates and countdowns.
4. **Event dashboard**: "Show Mia's party." Pops up a big countdown, a budget bar, tasks done, guests coming and the
   next jobs, with buttons for the timeline, guests, budget and seating.
5. **Change an event**: "Move Mia's party to 17 November." / "The budget is 350 pounds." / "Rename it to Mia's 7th."
6. **Remove an event**: "Remove Mia's party." Alfred checks with you first.

## Tasks, timeline and checklists

7. **Timeline**: "Show the timeline for Mia's party." Pops up steps working back from the date (8 weeks before:
   send invites), with ticks. Late steps are marked; tap a step to tick it off.
8. **Add a task**: "Add 'book the bouncy castle' 4 weeks before Mia's party."
9. **Tick a task**: "Tick off the invitations." (or its number)
10. **Standard steps**: "Add the wedding checklist to our wedding." For a UK wedding it starts a year out and
    includes giving notice of marriage at your register office (at least 29 days before the wedding).
11. **Setup-day checklist**: "Show the setup list for the party." Pops up a tick list (chairs, decorations, music,
    ice...). "Add a cake knife to the setup list." / "Tick off balloons."

## Guests and seating

12. **Add guests**: "Invite Ava, Leo and Zara to Mia's party." / "Add Ben, he's coming with a plus one and has a nut
    allergy." Guests can have an RSVP, dietary needs and plus-ones.
13. **RSVP**: "Ava is coming." / "Leo can't come." / "Zara is a maybe."
14. **Guest list**: "Show the guest list." Pops up a table with RSVP, plus-ones, dietary needs and tables.
15. **Counts**: "How many are coming?" Pops up a chart of yes, maybe, no and waiting, and Alfred lists dietary needs.
16. **Remove a guest**: "Take Zara off the list." (asks first)
17. **Set up tables**: "Set up 5 tables of 8." (Each guest takes one seat; add a plus-one as their own guest to seat them.)
18. **Seating plan**: "Show the seating plan." Pops up your tables with seats. Tap a guest, then tap a seat, and Alfred
    seats them. Tap a filled seat to take that guest off it.
19. **Seat someone**: "Put Ben at table 2, seat 3." Leave the seat out for the first free one; "Unseat Ben."
20. **Invitation wording**: "Write an invitation for Mia's party, fun style, 2pm, RSVP by 1 November." Alfred writes
    the wording from the event details.
21. **Thank-you cards**: "Show my thank-you cards for the party." Pops up a tick list of everyone who came.
22. **Tick a thank-you**: "I've sent Ava a thank-you." / "Ava gave Mia a book." (notes the gift)

## Money and suppliers

23. **Plan a budget line**: "Plan 100 pounds for the venue and 40 for the cake."
24. **Log spending**: "I spent 30 pounds on the cake." Alfred says how much of the budget is used, or how far over.
25. **Budget chart**: "Show the budget for Mia's party." Pops up planned against spent for each category as bars;
    over-budget ones are striped.
26. **Suppliers and bookings**: "Book the village hall as the venue, 200 pounds, 50 deposit due 1 October, balance
    due 1 November, phone 01234 567890." Also the cake, DJ or photographer.
27. **Mark a payment**: "I've paid the venue deposit." Adds it to your spending too.
28. **Supplier list**: "Show my suppliers." Pops up a table with cost, deposit and balance status and due dates;
    Alfred warns about anything overdue.

## Food and menu

29. **Menu planner**: "Mains for Mia's party: pizza and salad." / "Show the menu." Pops up the courses, plus the
    guests' dietary needs.
30. **How much food**: "How much food for 30 adults and 10 children?" (or leave the numbers out to use the guests
    coming) Pops up sandwiches, pastry bites, cake slices, soft drinks, wine, beer, tea and ice.

## Special days

31. **Christmas dinner timetable**: "Make a Christmas dinner timetable for 2pm, 5 kilo turkey." Pops up a timeline
    working back from serving time: turkey in, roast potatoes, gravy, carving.
32. **Kids' party timeline**: "Plan a party for a 7 year old starting at 3pm." Pops up a timed running order with
    ideas for that age.
33. **Party bags**: "Make a party bag list for 12 children." A tick list.
34. **Weather on the day**: "What will the weather be for the garden lunch?" Uses the free Open-Meteo forecast and
    works only within 14 days. Only the city goes out.
35. **Memory note**: "Save a memory of Mia's party: she loved the treasure hunt." Saved as a note in your
    Personal folder and shown on screen.
