# Home admin and shopping

The paperwork of running a home, and buying smarter. Say these to Alfred. Everything is saved in your memory folder
on this PC in `homeadmin.json` (price notes go into the grocery price book), nothing is sent anywhere. Answers pop up
as a table or a tick list; tapping a list item or button tells Alfred, just as if you'd said it. Removing anything
asks you to confirm first. Files (receipts, manuals, photos) are ones already in your memory folders; give their path,
like `Home/Receipts/tv.pdf`.

Already there and not repeated here: bills, budgets, renewal countdowns, the shopping list, meter readings, bin days,
the interval-based maintenance jobs, the home inventory with room totals and gift *ideas*.

## Shop smarter

1. **Price per unit**: "Which is better value: 500 grams for 2.10 or 1 kilo for 3.80?" Pops up: a table with each
   option's price per 100g (or 100ml, or each) and which is cheapest.
2. **Multi-buy check**: "Is 3 for 2 worth it on 1.50 pasta sauce?" / "Two for 5 pounds, single price 3." Pops up: single
   against deal, what you save.
3. **Note a price**: "I paid 1.35 for milk at Aldi." Saved to the grocery price book.
4. **Is this a good price?**: "Is 1.45 a good price for milk?" Judged against what you noted before. Pops up: a table of
   your last and lowest price at each shop.
5. **Gift budget**: "My Christmas gift budget is 300 pounds."
6. **Add a gift**: "Christmas gift for Mum: a scarf, 20 pounds." / "I've bought the scarf." Says how much budget is left.
7. **Show gifts**: "Show my Christmas gifts." Pops up: a table by person with cost, bought or to buy, total and budget
   left. Works for birthdays too: "Show Mia's birthday gifts."
8. **Remove a gift**: "Remove the scarf from Christmas." (asks first)

## Paperwork

9. **Add a return**: "I need to return the boots to Next by 3 October, 45 pounds." 
10. **Show returns**: "What returns have I got?" Pops up: a table of items, refund, return-by date and countdown.
11. **Return progress**: "I've sent the boots back." / "The boots refund has arrived."
12. **Remove a return**: "Remove the boots return." (asks first)
13. **Add a warranty**: "The TV has a 2 year warranty from 1 November 2024, receipt is Home/Receipts/tv.pdf."
14. **Warranties ending soon**: "Which warranties are running out?" Pops up: a table with a countdown; buttons open the
    receipts.
15. **Remove a warranty**: "Remove the kettle warranty." (asks first)
16. **Add to the subscription audit**: "Netflix is 10.99 a month, cancel by 5 October, last used in June."
17. **Used a subscription**: "I used Netflix today."
18. **Subscription audit**: "Audit my subscriptions." Pops up: cost per month and year, last used, and anything unused
    for 60 days or with a cancel-by date coming.
19. **Remove a subscription**: "Remove Netflix from the audit." (asks first)
20. **Save an important document**: "My passport expires 1 December 2026." / "Council tax is band D."
21. **Important documents**: "Show my important documents." Pops up: a table with renewal countdowns. With none saved,
    a list of usual ones (passport, driving licence, TV licence...) to tap and add.
22. **Remove a document**: "Remove the passport." (asks first)

## The house

23. **Log a service**: "The boiler was serviced today, 90 pounds, every 12 months, manual is Home/Manuals/boiler.pdf."
24. **Service log**: "Show the boiler service log." / "Show my appliance services." Pops up: a table, with an Open manual button.
25. **Services due**: "What needs servicing?" Pops up: a list; tap one to say it's done.
26. **Photo for insurance**: "The photo of the TV is Photos/tv.jpg." Attaches to an item in your home inventory.
27. **Insurance check**: "Check my contents for insurance." Pops up: a table of items, value and photos by room.
28. **Maintenance calendar**: "What home jobs are due this month?" Pops up: a tick list for the month (gutters, smoke
    alarm test, boiler service, bleeding radiators...); "Show the whole year" gives a table.
29. **Tick a job**: "Tick off bleed the radiators."
30. **Add a job**: "Add wash the car to October."
31. **What an appliance costs**: "What does a 2000 watt kettle cost to run for half an hour a day at 25p a unit?"
    Pops up: energy and cost per day, week, month and year. Uses the electricity price from your meters if you set it,
    or 25p if not.
32. **Save an appliance**: "Save the tumble dryer at 2500 watts, 1 hour a day."
33. **Appliance ranking**: "Which appliances cost most to run?" Pops up: a table, dearest first, with a total.
34. **Remove an appliance**: "Remove the lamp from running costs." (asks first)

## Checklists and the emergency card

35. **Moving house**: "Show my moving house checklist." / "Tick off book the removal firm." / "Add change the locks."
    Pops up: a tick list of 18 jobs.
36. **Switching utilities**: "Show my utility switch checklist." Pops up: a tick list for changing energy supplier.
37. **Emergency info**: "The stopcock is under the kitchen sink." / "The fuse box is in the hall cupboard."
38. **Emergency card**: "Show my emergency card." Pops up: the card with 999, 101, 111, 105 (power cut) and the gas
    emergency number, plus where your stopcock, fuse box and meters are. It's saved as `Home emergency card.md` in
    your memory folder, ready to print.
