# Motoring: your car and getting around the UK

Things to say to Alfred. Your car details, dates and logs are saved in `motoring.json` in your memory folder on this
PC. Nothing goes online, and your registration is only shown on the screen: Alfred never speaks it back or looks it up.
Answers with numbers or lists pop up a window on the Alfred screen.

The car fuel log ("I filled up at 10,400 miles...") and "where I parked" already live in Household plus, so they are
not repeated here.

## Your car

1. **Car profile**: "My car is a 2018 Ford Focus, petrol, 48 mpg, registration AB12 CDE." / "Show my car."
   Pops up: a table of the car (the registration appears only here).
2. **Set a car date**: "My MOT is due on 10 November." / "Car tax is due 1 December." / "Insurance renews on 3 March."
   Alfred sets a reminder for 2 weeks before.
3. **Car dates countdown**: "When are my car dates due?" Pops up: a table of MOT, tax, insurance and service with days
   left, marking what is due soon or overdue.
4. **Remove a car date**: "Remove the insurance date." Alfred asks you to confirm first.
5. **Log a job**: "I had an oil change at 41,000 miles for 89.99 pounds, next in 10,000 miles or 12 months."
6. **Maintenance log**: "Show my car maintenance." / "When did I last do the brakes?"
   Pops up: a table of jobs, dates, miles, cost and what is next due.
7. **Tyre notes**: "Front left tyre is 32 psi and 3.5 mm tread." / "All tyres 33 psi." Alfred warns below 3 mm and at the
   1.6 mm legal limit.
8. **Show tyres**: "Show my tyre notes." Pops up: a table of pressure and tread for each tyre.
9. **Parking timer**: "Start a 2 hour parking timer, level 2." Pops up: a live countdown, and a reminder 5 minutes
   before it runs out.

## Costs and logs

10. **Trip fuel cost**: "How much for 120 miles with petrol at 145.9p a litre?" / "Return trip to Bath, 60 miles each way."
    Uses your car's mpg if you saved it. Pops up: distance, litres, cost and pence per mile.
11. **EV charging cost**: "What does 100 miles cost at 25p a kWh?" / "Charging from 20 to 80 percent on a 60 kWh battery
    at 10p." Pops up: kWh, cost and range gained.
12. **Log work mileage**: "I drove 45 miles from Leeds to York for a client visit." Also motorbike or bicycle.
13. **Mileage claim table**: "Show my work mileage." Pops up: this tax year's trips with the claim at HMRC rates
    (45p a mile for the first 10,000, then 25p; motorbike 24p; bicycle 20p).
14. **Export mileage to CSV**: "Export my mileage to a spreadsheet." Saves a CSV in your Personal folder and shows it.
15. **Car-share split**: "Split 60 pounds of petrol between 4." / "100 miles each way at 20p a mile between 3 of us."
16. **Speed, distance and time**: "How long is 150 miles at 50 mph?" / "I did 90 miles in 90 minutes, how fast was that?"
17. **Log a commute**: "My commute took 35 minutes." / "Yesterday's drive home took 50 minutes."
18. **Commute chart**: "Show my commute times." / "Show my recent commutes." Pops up: a bar chart of the average
    journey time by weekday, or a line of the last 30 journeys.

## Rules of the road

19. **Speed limits**: "What are the UK speed limits?" Pops up: the table by vehicle and road type.
20. **Stopping distances**: "What's the stopping distance at 50 mph?" Pops up: thinking, braking and total distance
    at each speed, in metres and car lengths.
21. **Warning lights**: "What does the oil light mean?" / "Show the dashboard warning lights." Pops up: coloured
    lights; click one for what it means and what to do.
22. **Congestion and ULEZ**: "Tell me about the ULEZ." Pops up: a table of London's charges and clean air zones. These are
    plain facts, so check tfl.gov.uk for current prices.
23. **Breakdown checklist**: "What do I do if I break down?" Pops up: a list of steps.
24. **After an accident**: "What do I do after a crash?" Pops up: a list of steps.
25. **Winter car kit**: "What should be in my car for winter?" Pops up: a kit list.
26. **Road trip packing**: "Road trip packing list." Pops up: a packing list.
27. **Used car checklist**: "I'm looking at a used car; what should I check?" Pops up: a buying checklist.

## Learning to drive

28. **Theory question**: "Give me a theory test question." Pops up: a multiple-choice card; click an answer.
29. **Answer**: "Theory answer B." Alfred says if you were right and why, then asks the next question.
30. **Theory score**: "What's my theory score?" Pops up: right, asked and percentage (the real test pass mark is 43 of 50).
