# Travel

Things to say to Alfred when you're planning a trip or away on one. Most of them pop a window up on the Alfred
screen. Your trips, bookings, documents and lists are saved in your memory folder on this PC (the `travel-*.json`
files), and trip journals are Markdown files in the **Travel** folder. Only a place name ever goes online: to
Open-Meteo for a trip's weather, or to find the time zone of a city Alfred doesn't already know.

Already elsewhere (not repeated here): packing lists (home-and-life.md), currency and trip budgets (money-plus.md),
maps, distances and country facts (web-in-alfred.md), world clocks and time zones (time-and-dates.md).

When you don't name a trip, Alfred uses your next one.

## Trips

1. **Add a trip**: "I'm going to Barcelona from 5 to 9 October." / "Add a trip called Mum's 60th to Lisbon, 1 to
   4 May." Alfred says how many days to go.
2. **List trips**: "What trips have I got?" Pops up a table of your trips with a countdown in days; press a trip
   for its summary.
3. **Delete a trip**: "Delete the Tokyo trip." Alfred checks with you first, because its plan and bookings go too.

## Itinerary

4. **Add to the plan**: "On day two of Barcelona, 3 pm, Sagrada Familia on Carrer de Mallorca."
5. **Show the plan**: "What's the plan for Monday in Barcelona?" / "Show my itinerary." Pops up a table for that
   day (or the whole trip), with a button for each day.
6. **Move or remove**: "Move number 3 to Wednesday at 11." / "Take number 2 off the plan." (Removing is confirmed.)

## Bookings

7. **Save a booking**: "Save my flight BA478, Heathrow to Barcelona, 5 October at 7:15." / "Add the hotel, Hotel
   Arts, reference HX99, Carrer de la Marina 19."
8. **Ask about one**: "What's my flight number?" / "Which hotel am I in?" / "What's the train reference?"
9. **All bookings**: "Show my bookings." Pops up a table with references, times and addresses. Removing one is
   confirmed.

## Documents and leave

10. **Travel documents**: "My passport expires on 1 May 2031." / "My GHIC runs out on 31 January." / "Show my
    travel documents." Pops up a table; anything expired or within 6 months of expiring is flagged.
11. **Holiday allowance**: "I get 25 days' leave a year." / "Book 5 to 11 October as leave." / "How much leave
    have I got left?" Pops up bars for allowance, taken, booked and left. Weekdays are counted for you; say "2 days"
    for anything else.

## Places

12. **Places wishlist**: "Add Kyoto to places I want to visit, for the cherry blossom." / "I've visited Lisbon." /
    "Show my places list." Pops up a list with tick boxes; ticking one tells Alfred you've been.
13. **Where have I been?**: "Where have I been?" Pops up a table of ticked-off places counted by country.

## On the trip

14. **Trip journal**: "Add to my trip journal: tapas by the sea." / "Read me my Barcelona journal." Entries are
    dated and saved as `<trip> journal.md` in the Travel folder; reading pops the journal up.
15. **Gifts and souvenirs**: "Add turrón and a fridge magnet for Mum to the gift list." / "I've got the magnet." /
    "Show my gift list." Pops up a list with tick boxes.

## Phrasebook and country facts

16. **Phrasebook**: "Show me the Japanese phrasebook." / "How do I say thank you in Greek?" Pops up 20 essential
    phrases (hello, please, the bill, where is the toilet, I need a doctor...) with a **Speak** button on each.
    Languages: French, Spanish, Italian, German, Portuguese, Dutch, Greek, Turkish, Japanese and Mandarin.
17. **Plugs and voltage**: "What plugs do they use in the USA?" / "Do I need an adapter for Malta?" / "Show plugs
    for every country." Pops up the plug type and voltage, and whether your UK plugs will fit.
18. **Driving, emergencies and tipping**: "Which side do they drive on in Japan?" / "What's the emergency number
    in Thailand?" / "Should I tip in Italy?" Pops up a small table (or the whole table of about 80 countries).
    These are the usual figures; check anything important before you go.

## Time zones

19. **Jet lag planner**: "Plan my jet lag for Tokyo." / "Help me adjust for New York over 2 days, I go to bed at
    10:30." Pops up a table moving your bedtime and wake time an hour a day before you fly, with light tips.
20. **Calling home**: "When's a good time to call home from Sydney?" Says the time difference and pops up a
    table of times there and at home, marking the hours when you're both usually awake (8 am to 10 pm).

## Weather and summary

21. **Trip weather**: "What's the weather going to be in Barcelona for my trip?" Pops up a chart of the daily highs
    with each day's conditions. The forecast only reaches 16 days ahead; before then Alfred says when to ask.
22. **Trip summary**: "Give me a summary of my Barcelona trip." Pops up one window with the countdown, the start of
    the plan, bookings, document warnings (like a passport with under 6 months left) and the weather.

## Travel checklists

23. **Checklists**: "Start my before you go checklist." / "Show the airport day checklist." / "I've cancelled the
    milk." / "Reset the airport day checklist." Pops up a list to tick: *before you go* (locks, plants, bins,
    cancel the milk...), *airport day* and *coming home*.
