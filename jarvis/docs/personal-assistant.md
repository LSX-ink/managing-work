# Personal assistant: the proactive helper

Things to say to Alfred so he keeps you organised. Everything is saved in `assistant.json` in your memory folder on
this PC and never sent anywhere (the briefing only fetches the public BBC headlines, and the weather as before).
Most answers pop up a window on the Alfred screen; Alfred just says a short line.

This builds on what you already have: your to-do list, reminders, calendar, birthdays, habits, the people
notebook (including "promises"), meeting notes and the inbox from "Note that...". Those keep working as before;
the assistant reads them and puts them together. Removing anything asks you to confirm first.

## Your day

1. **Morning briefing**: "Brief me." / "What's my day like?" Pops up: today's calendar and weather, what to start
   with, follow-ups to chase, reminders, birthdays and renewals coming up, things you keep forgetting, open counts,
   how many news headlines there are, and buttons to plan the day.
2. **Evening wrap-up**: "Wrap up my day." Pops up: what got done, what is still open, habits missed and tomorrow's
   first thing.
3. **What next**: "What should I do next?" / "I've got 10 minutes, what can I do?" Picks from your to-dos, follow-ups
   that are due and promises, by priority, the time you have and your energy. Pops up with the pick and what's after it.
4. **Time-block my day**: "Plan my day." / "Time-block tomorrow." A timeline pops up: calendar events (drawn an hour
   long), reminders, lunch, and your best jobs in the gaps. Your preferences apply ("no calls before 10").
5. **Prepare for a meeting**: "Prepare me for my next meeting." Pops up: when and where, what you know about the
   people in it, notes from the last similar meeting, what's open with them and decisions so far. Read-only.
6. **Weekly review**: "Do my weekly review." Pops up: wins, closed items, decisions, focus time, habits, what has
   been stuck over a week, and next week's dates.
7. **Start focus mode**: "Focus for 30 minutes on the report, no calls." A countdown pops up with your do-not-disturb
   note, and Alfred keeps replies short until it ends.
8. **Stop focus mode**: "Stop focus mode." Alfred tells you how long you focused; it counts in the weekly review.
9. **Focus status**: "Am I in focus mode?"
10. **Energy**: "I'm tired today." / "I've got loads of energy." Low energy picks quick jobs (calls, emails); high
    energy picks the deep ones (writing, planning).

## Things to chase

11. **Follow-up**: "Remind me to chase Sam if he hasn't replied by Friday." Saved, with a 9am reminder that day.
12. **Waiting for**: "I'm waiting for the plumber's quote." Add a person or a date if you like.
13. **Delegated**: "I asked Priya to book the venue by Thursday."
14. **What's outstanding**: "What am I chasing?" / "What's due to chase today?" Pops up the three lists, overdue
    ones highlighted; click one to tick it off.
15. **Tick one off**: "Sam replied." / "The plumber's quote came." (also ticks a promise to someone)
16. **Promises I made**: "What have I promised people?" Pops up the promises from your people notebook
    ("I promised Mum I'd visit on Sunday" adds one there).
17. **Log a decision**: "Note that we decided to go with option B, it's cheaper."
18. **Decisions log**: "What did we decide about the launch?" A table of dates, decisions and reasons.

## Capture and sort

19. **Quick capture**: "Capture: ring the dentist." Goes into your inbox.
20. **Inbox to sort**: "What's in my inbox?" Pops up each note with a suggested place; click one to sort it.
21. **Sort a note**: "Sort the dentist note into my to-dos." / "Make note 2 a reminder at 9 tomorrow." It moves the
    note to your to-dos, a reminder, an errand or Sorted notes in the Ideas folder, and clears it from the inbox.
22. **Add an errand**: "Errand: pick up the parcel at the post office."
23. **Errands by place**: "Batch my errands." Pops up your errands grouped by place, biggest batch first.
24. **Errand done**: "I've done the stamps errand."
25. **Log a win**: "Log a win: finished the report."
26. **This week's wins**: "Show my wins this week." / "...this month." Your wins, closed items and focus time.

## Preferences, dates and memory

27. **Save a preference**: "I don't like calls before 10." / "Never book me meetings on Fridays."
28. **Show my preferences**: "What do you know about how I like things?"
29. **Forget a preference**: "Forget my preference about calls." (asks first)
30. **Anniversary or renewal**: "The wedding anniversary is 3 October." / "Car insurance renews on 20 November, 480
    pounds, warn me 30 days ahead." Close ones show in the morning briefing.
31. **Countdowns**: "What's coming up: anniversaries and renewals?" A table with days to go and when to compare deals.
32. **Remove a countdown**: "Remove the car insurance renewal." (asks first)
33. **Things I keep forgetting**: "I keep forgetting to take my keys." A couple are nudged in each morning briefing.
34. **Show the list**: "What do I keep forgetting?" (pops up; today's nudges highlighted)
35. **Take one off**: "I don't need the keys reminder any more." (asks first)
