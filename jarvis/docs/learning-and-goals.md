# Learning and goals

Things you can say to Alfred to learn, set goals and look back on your week. Everything is kept in small
`growth-*.json` files in your memory folder, on your own PC.

## Studying
- **Make a flashcard deck:** "Make a flashcard deck called Spanish verbs."
- **Add a card:** "Add a card to Spanish verbs: front 'to eat', back 'comer'."
- **Quiz me:** "Quiz me on Spanish verbs." (Alfred asks the next card that's due.)
- **Mark right or wrong:** "I got it right." / "I got that wrong." (Right cards come back later; wrong ones come back soon.)
- **Deck stats:** "How am I doing on my flashcards?"
- **Phrase of the day:** "What's today's Zulu phrase?" (Afrikaans, Zulu, Spanish or French.)
- **Language quiz:** "Quiz me on some French."
- **Log study time:** "I studied maths for 90 minutes."
- **Study this week:** "How many hours have I studied this week?"

## Goals and fitness
- **Set a goal:** "New goal: read 20 books by the end of December."
- **Log progress:** "Add 2 to my reading goal."
- **Progress report:** "How are my goals going?"
- **Mark achieved:** "Mark my guitar goal as achieved."
- **Log a workout:** "I did bench press, 3 sets of 8 at 60 kilos." / "I went for a 30 minute run."
- **This week's workouts:** "What workouts have I done this week?"
- **Personal bests:** "What are my personal bests?"
- **Log steps:** "I walked 8,000 steps today."
- **Step goal:** "Set my weekly step goal to 60,000."
- **Steps this week:** "How are my steps this week?"
- **Set today's top 3:** "My priorities today are the gym, calling mum and the tax return."
- **Tick one off:** "Priority two is done."
- **Read them:** "What are my priorities today?" (They start fresh each day.)

## Reading and watching
- **Add a book:** "Add Dune by Frank Herbert to my reading list."
- **Start a book:** "I've started reading Dune."
- **Finish a book:** "I finished Dune, four stars."
- **What am I reading:** "What am I reading at the moment?"
- **Books this year:** "Which books have I finished this year?"
- **Add to the watch list:** "Add Slow Horses to my watch list, it's a series."
- **Mark watched:** "I've watched Oppenheimer."
- **Watch list:** "What's on my watch list?"
- **Pick for tonight:** "Pick something for me to watch tonight."
- **Save a link:** "Save this link as Python docs, tagged coding." (Links stay on your PC.)
- **Links by tag:** "Show my coding links."
- **Open a link:** "Open my Python docs link."
- **Save a quote:** "Save this quote: stay hungry, stay foolish, by Steve Jobs."
- **Random quote:** "Read me one of my quotes."

## Reflecting
- **Today I learned:** "Today I learned octopuses have three hearts."
- **Learning recap:** "What did I learn this week?"
- **Log a win:** "Log a win: I fixed the boiler myself."
- **This month's wins:** "Read me my wins this month."
- **Record a decision:** "Record a decision: I'm taking the new job, I expect more growth."
- **Revisit decisions:** "Any decisions I should look back on?" (Ones older than 30 days.)
- **Note how it went:** "The new job decision turned out well."
- **Weekly review:** "Give me my weekly review."
