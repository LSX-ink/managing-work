# Writing income

Planning, measuring and tracking for people who earn (or hope to earn) from writing online: a newsletter, a blog, books on
Amazon KDP, paid subscriptions and paid articles. It sits beside the [writing studio](writing.md) (which holds your drafts),
[repurposing content](repurpose-content.md) and [digital products](digital-products.md); it doesn't repeat them.

Everything is stored on this PC in `writingincome-*.json` files in your memory folder. Nothing is sent, posted or looked up:
no email service, blog, Amazon or publication is ever contacted. Money figures are numbers you type, and every result is a
what-if, never a promise of income. UK tax notes are general guidance: check GOV.UK.

## Newsletter (writingincome_newsletter)

| Say | What pops up |
|---|---|
| "Plan a newsletter issue called Autumn reads for 10 October" | Saves it on the calendar (idea, drafting, ready, sent) |
| "Show my newsletter calendar" | Table of issues with countdowns, and a warning if nothing is planned soon |
| "Mark Autumn reads as ready" | Updates status, date, subject or notes |
| "Remove the Autumn reads issue" | Asks first |
| "What sections should an essay issue have?" | Layouts: digest, essay, tips, personal |
| "Give me subject lines about sourdough" | List of 12 starters; tap one to check it |
| "Check this subject line: Three small sourdough fixes" | Length, words, spammy words, shouting, emoji, honesty |
| "I have 140 subscribers today" | Logs your typed count, with the change since last time |
| "Show my subscriber growth" | Line chart, weekly pace and (if you give a goal) a rough what-if date |
| "Issue 4 was sent to 200 and 90 opened" | Logs open rate (and clicks) |
| "Show my open rates" | Bar chart and average, with a note that opens can be inflated |
| "Draft my welcome emails" | Guide of a 3 to 5 email sequence with placeholders |
| "Save my version of welcome email 1" | Keeps your edited text |
| "Lead magnet ideas for baking" | Table of 12 ideas with effort |
| "Newsletter pre-send checklist" | Tick list, including the unsubscribe link |

## Blog (writingincome_blog)

| Say | What pops up |
|---|---|
| "Add a blog post called Baking Bread, keyword baking bread, 1200 words" | Saves it as an idea |
| "Mark Baking Bread as published" | Moves it along (idea, draft, published) |
| "Show my blog pipeline" | Three columns; tap a post for its SEO checklist |
| "Remove the post Old Post" | Asks first |
| "Show the SEO checklist for Baking Bread" | 14 plain-words steps with ticks |
| "Tick SEO step 3 for Baking Bread" | Ticks or unticks a step |
| "Check this title and meta description" | Lengths, keyword present, suggested web address |
| "Is baking bread in my headings? (pastes text)" | Keyword in title, first 100 words, headings and how often |
| "Check my heading structure" | One main title, no skipped levels, outline |
| "How readable is this?" | Flesch reading ease worked out on your PC, grade level, long sentences, words against target |
| "How long should a how-to post be?" | Rough lengths by post type; with a post, words so far against target |
| "Link Baking Bread to Sourdough Tips" | Adds to the internal link plan |
| "Show my internal links" | Planned links and posts nothing links to |
| "Which posts need refreshing?" | Table of published posts untouched for 180 days (or your number) |
| "I refreshed Old Post" | Resets its reminder |
| "Make a web address for this title" | Short lowercase slug |

## Book on KDP (writingincome_book)

| Say | What pops up |
|---|---|
| "Start a book called Ashes, 60,000 words, 500 a day" | Creates the project |
| "Show my books" | Table with words, progress and streak |
| "Remove the book Ashes" | Asks first |
| "Chapter 3 is at 2,800 words" | Sets a chapter's word count (new chapters are created) |
| "I wrote 600 words on Ashes today" | Logs words, shows today against your goal and the streak |
| "Set my daily book goal to 400" | Changes the goal |
| "How is Ashes going?" | Progress bars, chapters, streak, a rough finish estimate and words a day for the deadline |
| "Chart my writing this fortnight" | Bar chart of the last 14 days |
| "KDP royalty for a 4.99 ebook with 30p delivery" | 35% and 70% options side by side (paperback: price, printing cost you type) |
| "Show an ebook price ladder" | Table of prices and both royalties |
| "How many copies to earn 100 pounds?" | A what-if number of copies |
| "How many pages is 55,000 words?" | Page and reading-time estimate |
| "Blurb structure for fiction" | Hook, setup, conflict, stakes, close (or the non-fiction version) |
| "Check my blurb" | Length, limit, opening line, risky claims; saves it to the book |
| "Set keyword slot 1 to cosy fantasy romance" | Fills one of 7 slots |
| "Note categories: Fantasy, Romance" | Saves your category notes |
| "Show my KDP keywords" | Slots, categories and tips |
| "Launch checklist for Ashes" | 15 steps with ticks |
| "Tick launch step 4" | Ticks or unticks a step |
| "Draft review requests" | Three honest drafts plus Amazon's rules against paid or swapped reviews |
| "Tell me about KDP" | Plain notes on formats, royalties, ISBN, tax |

KDP's rates, price ranges and printing costs change and differ by country. Type the delivery and printing costs KDP shows you;
every result says to check KDP's current rates.

## Money (writingincome_money)

| Say | What pops up |
|---|---|
| "100 paid subscribers at 5 pounds on Substack" | Take-home after platform and card fees, monthly and yearly |
| "Subscribers needed to take home 500 a month" | Number needed at your price |
| "If 1,000 free subscribers convert" | Table of 1% to 10% what-ifs (real rates can be lower) |
| "Break-even for 25 pounds a month of costs" | Paid subscribers needed to cover them |
| "150 pounds for 1,000 words in 5 hours" | Pence per word and pounds per hour |
| "Write an affiliate disclosure for my newsletter" | UK-style wording (add the Amazon line if you like) |
| "Track a pitch to Food Weekly: winter soups" | Saves it (idea, drafted, pitched, accepted, published, paid, rejected) |
| "The soups pitch was accepted at 150 pounds" | Updates status and fee |
| "Show my pitches" | Board with money agreed and follow-up nudges after two weeks |
| "Give me a pitch structure" | Subject, opening, idea, why you, close (you send it yourself) |
| "Delete the soups pitch" | Asks first |
| "Be honest about writing income" | Realistic expectations and tax note |

Fee presets (Substack about 10% plus card fees, for example) are approximate; you can override any of them. Medium pays from
member reading time, so there is no subscribers-times-price sum for it.

## Left out on purpose

Client work, proposals and niche scoring belong to other themes. Posting, sending, uploading and anything needing a login are
never done: pitches and emails are drafts for you to send yourself.
