Good to know before you start

How long does it take?

A Starter website takes 1 to 2 weeks, a Business website 2 to 4 weeks and an Online shop 4 to 6 weeks, counted from your go-ahead.

How do I pay?

Half is paid up front to book your start date, and the rest when your site goes live. You get a clear invoice for each.

What do I need to give you?

Your vision in your own words, your three colours, and your logo and photos if you have them. No logo? Logo design is £150. No photos? Photo editing and stock images are £100.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Starter websites include 2 rounds of changes and Business websites and shops include 3.

What's in monthly hosting and care?

Your domain name, fast and secure hosting, security updates, backups and small text changes, all for £20 a month. Need lots of big videos or downloads? Extra file storage is £5 a month per 10 GB.

Who owns my website and domain?

You do. Your domain is registered in your name and your accounts belong to you. If you ever leave, we hand everything over.

I already have a website. Can you improve it?

Yes, and you get 20% off a Business website or Online shop. Tick "I already have a website" in the quote, add your website link to your brief and tell us what you like about it. We keep what works, fix what doesn't and send you three new designs in your colours.

Do you write the words too?

We write the copy with you. If you'd like us to write whole pages for you, copywriting is £60 a page.

Will people find me on Google?

Starter websites include the Google basics. Business websites and shops link your existing Google Business Profile. Full Google Business Profile setup and optimisation (creating or verifying the profile, categories, photos, services and a review link) is £150 as an add-on.

The legal bits

The details UK law asks every business to share, in plain English.

Who we are

Kinetic Web Designs is a trading name of [owner's full name], a sole trader in England. Business address: [business address]. Email: alfredw.lsx@gmail.com. We are not VAT registered, so our prices have no VAT to add. If that changes, we'll show our VAT number here and on every invoice.

Privacy notice

What we collect. Your name, business name, email, phone number, website link and the details you put in your brief or sign-up form, plus your payment records if you take the hosting and care plan.

Why. To reply to your brief, quote, build and look after your website (needed for our contract with you), to keep our accounts (a legal duty), and to send design tips and offers only if you ticked the box (your consent, which you can withdraw any time by replying "unsubscribe").

Who else sees it. Only the services that run this site for us: Formspree (forms), Stripe (payments, which never share your card or bank details with us) and our email provider. We never sell your details.

How long we keep it. Enquiries that don't go ahead: 12 months. Client and payment records: 6 years, as HMRC requires.

Your rights. You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or object to how we use them, by emailing alfredw.lsx@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies. We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. Your brief draft and colour choices are saved in your own browser only, so you don't lose them.

Terms of business

Prices. Prices on this site are the full price for what's listed. Your written quote is fixed for 30 days.

Paying. 50% deposit to book your start date, the rest when your site goes live. You own your website and content once the final invoice is paid.

Your right to cancel. If you're buying as a private individual rather than a business, you can cancel within 14 days of agreeing to go ahead and get a full refund. If you ask us to start work within those 14 days and then cancel, you pay only for the work already done.

Hosting and care. £20 a month, paid monthly, no minimum term. Cancel any time with one month's notice and we hand your website and domain over to you.

Changes. Starter websites include 2 rounds of changes, Business websites and shops 3. Extra changes are charged at an hourly rate we agree with you first.

Complaints. Email alfredw.lsx@gmail.com and we'll reply within 2 working days and do our best to put things right.
