## Weak spots a website would fix
1. **No website at all.** A Facebook profile stands in for one, so Google searches like "barber Croydon" have nowhere better to send people.
2. **No online booking.** Customers have to phone or walk in, and there's no Fresha or Booksy link either, so 10pm bookers go elsewhere.
3. **No prices online.** People can't see what a fade or a beard trim costs before they come.
4. **486 reviews at 4.8 stars are only on Google.** There's no page of their own showing that reputation off.
5. **No gallery of cuts.** The listing has photos, but there's no portfolio of fades and styles that sells the work.
6. **The MK story is invisible.** Years of loyal regulars and "go to MK himself" are a strong brand that a site would show.
